# Dan Hooker: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Switch stance (UFC Stats), 6'0", 75-inch reach, born 1990-02-13 (age 36). Nickname "The Hangman". New Zealander; trains at City Kickboxing in Auckland (ESPN) and received his BJJ black belt on Oct 25, 2025 (Wikipedia). UFC record 14-11 in fighters.json (18 lightweight and 7 featherweight bouts); 24-15 as a pro (ESPN, Wikipedia, Sherdog).*

> Research note: the earlier offline pass could not re-verify the Ortega booking or find a ranking. Both were confirmed on the web on Nov 6, 2026 (ESPN, Wikipedia, Sherdog, ranking mirrors).

## Current status
**Active, on a three-fight losing streak, all by stoppage.**
- **Nov 22, 2025:** Arman Tsarukyan submitted him with an arm-triangle from mount (R2 3:34) in the main event in Doha.
- **Jan 31, 2026:** Benoit Saint Denis stopped him with punches from mount (R2 4:45) at UFC 325 in Sydney.
- **Sep 5, 2026:** Salahdine Parnasse stopped him at 2:35 of round 1 in the UFC Paris main event: a liver kick hurt him and punches finished it (MMA Junkie/Yahoo recap; Wikipedia "TKO (body kick and punches)"; UFC Stats logs the last strike as a punch to the head).

**Next fight (confirmed):** **Brian Ortega** at lightweight on **Nov 21, 2026** at UFC Fight Night 294 ("UFC Qatar", headlined by Prochazka vs. Stirling) at ABHA Arena (Ali Bin Hamad Al Attiyah Arena), **Doha**, on the main card (ESPN fight listing, Wikipedia, Sherdog, Qatar Tribune). It is a 77-day turnaround. Hooker says he asked for it because he was frustrated not to show his camp's improvements against Parnasse and came out of that fight without serious injury: "Surely I can beat Brian Ortega... he's old as well." **Ranking:** **#14** in the UFC's official media-panel rankings (updated Nov 3, 2026, down one place; ufcrankings.org Nov 5); he is not in the UFC's Meta algorithmic top 15 (Oct 31). **Injuries:** none current. A hand injury forced him out of a UFC 313 fight with Gaethje in early 2025 (Wikipedia; Bloody Elbow reported it as a broken hand).

## Recent fights
- **L Parnasse (Sep 2026), TKO R1.** He landed 12 of 19 leg strikes but none of 9 to the head. A liver kick dropped him and punches ended it.
- **L Saint Denis (Jan 2026), TKO R2.** Saint Denis landed 97 of 127 significant strikes, 67 of them on the ground, with 6:14 of control. Hooker tried two submissions.
- **L Tsarukyan (Nov 2025), sub R2.** He was taken down once per round and controlled for 6:02. He landed only 10 significant strikes.
- **W Mateusz Gamrot, UFC 305 (Aug 2024), split decision.** He stuffed 10 of 15 takedowns and won the body battle 24-6.
- **W Jalin Turner, UFC 290 (Jul 2023), split decision.** He outlanded Turner 125-100, with 23 leg strikes and a knockdown.
- **W Claudio Puelles, UFC 281 (Nov 2022), front kick to the body, R2.**

## Style
He is a rangy kickboxer who switches stance. He leads with the jab and long straights, kicks the legs at volume (20% of his landed strikes career; median about 15%) and the body (24% recency-weighted), and throws knees in the clinch. UFC Stats records two clinch-knee KOs (Pearson, Miller). Off his back or a front headlock he hunts guillotines; two of his UFC wins came that way.

## Strengths
- **Finishing power on the feet:** 9 knockdowns and 7 KO/TKO wins (0.55 knockdowns per 15 minutes; median about 0.31).
- **Varied kicking game:** heavy leg and body volume from range.
- **Five-round experience:** four five-round fights, including a split-decision win over Paul Felder and a 25-minute decision loss to Dustin Poirier.
- **Takedown defence:** 77% career (median about 67%).

## Weaknesses
- **Elite wrestlers and grapplers:** Makhachev, Tsarukyan and Saint Denis all finished him on the mat.
- **Defence:** 40% significant-strike defence recency-weighted. He absorbs 5.6 significant strikes per minute (median about 4.1).
- **Opponents shoot often** (7.7 attempts per 15 recency-weighted) and controlled him 29% of the time recently.

## Durability and cardio
He has 5 KO/TKO losses (Barboza, Chandler, Arnold Allen, Saint Denis, Parnasse), two of them in 2026. Knockdowns absorbed per 15 minutes have risen from 0.18 career to 0.39 recency-weighted. His five-round pace (1.00 / 1.65 / 1.37 / 0.60 / 1.00) shows output rising after round 1, dipping in round 4 and recovering. He went 25 minutes twice (Felder, Poirier); recent damage is the bigger worry.

## What the numbers say
Elo 1589, 32nd among the 100 lightweights who have fought since May 2025, down after three losses. His career rates: 4.8 significant strikes landed per minute at 49% accuracy (median about 4.1 at 47%) and 4.9 absorbed. The recency-weighted numbers are worse: 4.4 landed and 5.6 absorbed per minute. He attempts submissions at 1.26 per 15 minutes recency-weighted.
