He then boxed twice: a unanimous-decision loss to Jake Paul (**Aug 5, 2023**) and a majority-decision win over Jorge Masvidal (**Jul 6, 2024**). He has never announced a retirement. He has no title, no UFC ranking and no confirmed next fight.

## Recent UFC fights
- **W Tony Ferguson, UFC 279 (Sep 2022), guillotine R4.** He out-landed Ferguson 101-80, but Ferguson landed 34 of 39 leg kicks.
- **L Leon Edwards, UFC 263 (Jun 2021), decision (46-49 x3).** Edwards won on leg kicks (23 of 23) and takedowns (4 of 5, 4:59 of control). Diaz landed 31 significant strikes in round 5, his best round.
- **L Jorge Masvidal, UFC 244 (Nov 2019), doctor stoppage after R3 for facial cuts.** He was knocked down twice and out-landed 112-43.
- **L/W Conor McGregor, UFC 202 / UFC 196 (2016).** He lost a majority decision at UFC 202 (McGregor landed 40 of 45 leg kicks) after choking McGregor out at UFC 196.

## Style
He is a long, upright southpaw who walks forward behind a pawing right jab and left straight, and he stays busy in the clinch. He lands 4.6 significant strikes per minute (lightweight median 4.1) plus a lot of non-significant volume: 7.4 total strikes landed per minute. 84% of his significant attempts go to the head and only 5% to the legs. Off his back he is a real grappler. Five of his ten UFC submission wins are guillotines, and he has also won by triangle, armbar and rear-naked choke.

## Strengths
- **Submission threat:** 1.16 attempts per 15 minutes (lightweight median 0.28).
- **Volume and pressure**, with output that rises over the fight.
- **Durability:** one KO loss by strikes in 27 UFC fights (Josh Thomson's head kick, 2013).

## Weaknesses
- **Leg kicks:** 25% of what opponents land goes to his legs (league about 15%).
- **Takedown defence:** 44% (median 67%). Henderson controlled him for 16:58 in 2012.
- **Cuts**, and **little one-punch power** (0.18 knockdowns per 15 minutes vs a median of 0.31).
- **Age and inactivity.**
