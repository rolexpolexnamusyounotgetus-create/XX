# Nate Diaz: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Southpaw, 6 ft 0 in, 76-inch reach, born 1985-04-16 (age 41). UFC record 16-11 in fighters.json (10 submissions, 2 KO/TKO, 4 decisions).*

**Research note:** UFC facts: UFC Stats snapshot of 2026-10-31. Status, record and appearance were re-checked on the web on 2026-11-06 (Wikipedia, Sherdog, UFCalendar, news reports; sources at the end).

## Current status
**Free agent, not in the UFC.** His last MMA fight was a loss to **Mike Perry** on Most Valuable Promotions' MVP MMA 1 "Rousey vs. Carano" card on **May 16, 2026** (Intuit Dome, Inglewood; Netflix). His cutman Jacob "Stitch" Duran had it stopped after round 2 because of cuts (referee Mike Beltran, per Sherdog). He needed **15 staples and 20 stitches**, mostly for a gash on the right side of his forehead, and broke a finger in the fight ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/nate-diaz-needed-15-staples-155722893.html)). It was his first MMA fight since UFC 279 (**Sep 10, 2022**), where his round-4 guillotine of Tony Ferguson completed his UFC contract. In between he boxed twice (Wikipedia): a unanimous-decision loss to Jake Paul (**Aug 5, 2023**) and a majority-decision win over Jorge Masvidal (**Jul 6, 2024**). He brushed off retirement talk after the Perry loss, and in Sep 2026 he said he would fight "everywhere", including MMA in a ring ([MMA Junkie via Central Oregon Daily](https://www.centraloregondaily.com/sports/nate-diaz-weighs-next-options-mike-perry-scott-coker-mvp-definitely-no-dillon-danis/article_bb343d64-5ea5-5b8d-b107-fc5ebafa8f11.html)). Nothing is booked as of Nov 6, 2026 ([UFCalendar](https://www.ufcalendar.com/fighters/nate-diaz)). Pro MMA record: 21-14 (Sherdog, Wikipedia, UFCalendar). No title or ranking.

## Recent fights
- **L Mike Perry, MVP "Rousey vs. Carano" (May 2026), TKO (corner stoppage) after R2.** Stopped on cuts (15 staples and 20 stitches), and he broke a finger. Not a UFC bout; no stats in the data.
- **W Tony Ferguson, UFC 279 (Sep 2022), guillotine R4.** He out-landed Ferguson 101-80, but Ferguson landed 34 of 39 leg kicks.
- **L Leon Edwards, UFC 263 (Jun 2021), decision (46-49 x3).** Edwards won on leg kicks (23 of 23) and takedowns (4 of 5, 4:59 of control). He landed 31 significant strikes in round 5.
- **L Jorge Masvidal, UFC 244 (Nov 2019), doctor stoppage after R3 for facial cuts.** He was knocked down twice and out-landed 112-43.
- **L/W Conor McGregor, UFC 202 / UFC 196 (2016).** He lost a majority decision at UFC 202 (McGregor landed 40 of 45 leg kicks) after choking McGregor out at UFC 196.

## Style
He is a long, upright southpaw who walks forward behind a pawing right jab and left straight, and he stays busy in the clinch. He lands 4.6 significant strikes per minute (lightweight median 4.1) plus a lot of non-significant volume: 7.4 total strikes landed per minute. 84% of his significant attempts go to the head and only 5% to the legs. Off his back he is a real grappler. Five of his ten UFC submission wins are guillotines, and he has also won by triangle, armbar and rear-naked choke.

## Strengths
- **Submission threat:** 1.16 attempts per 15 minutes (lightweight median 0.28).
- **Volume and pressure**, with output that rises over the fight.
- **Durability:** one KO loss by strikes in 27 UFC fights (Thomson's head kick, 2013).

## Weaknesses
- **Leg kicks:** 25% of what opponents land goes to his legs (league about 15%).
- **Takedown defence:** 44% (median 67%). Henderson controlled him for 16:58 in 2012.
- **Cuts**, and **little one-punch power** (0.18 knockdowns per 15 minutes vs a median of 0.31).
- **Age (41)**, and the corner stoppage against Perry in his first MMA fight in more than three and a half years.

## Durability and cardio
9 knockdowns absorbed in 337 UFC minutes, but rarely finished: one KO by strikes in 27 UFC fights, plus the Masvidal doctor stoppage and the Perry corner stoppage. His cardio is a defining trait. In five-round fights his pace by round, relative to round 1, is 1.00 / 1.38 / 1.06 / 1.21 / 1.00. In three-round fights it is 1.00 / 1.18 / 1.37.
