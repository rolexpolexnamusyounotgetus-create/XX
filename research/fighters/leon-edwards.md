## Style breakdown

A long southpaw kickboxer who scores at range and picks his shots. He throws only 4.0 significant strikes per minute on the decayed sums (welterweight 8.44) but lands 61.5%. He kicks far more than most: on the decayed sums, 19.7% of his attempts go to the body and 18.2% to the legs (division 13.9% and 9.3%). In the clinch (13% of his landed strikes) he works the body. The head kick that took the title from Usman is his signature. He takes the fight down when it is offered (2/3 against Prates) but does not build his game on wrestling.

## Strengths

- Accuracy and shot selection: 61.5% decayed, 57.6% at distance.
- Kicking game to the legs, body and head.
- Five-round experience: 9 scheduled five-rounders, with no fade (round-5 output 0.99x round 1).
- Defends takedowns well for stretches (Covington landed 2/10).

## Weaknesses

- Wrestlers control him: over the last 3 years his takedown defense is 46.7% and he spends 348 s per 15 min being controlled (division 167).
- His output is too low to win rounds when the opponent is busier. He was out-landed in each of his last three fights.
- Age 35, three losses in a row, and a year out.

## Durability and cardio

2 knockdowns absorbed in 335.6 UFC minutes (Barberena 2017, Prates 2025) and 1 KO loss (Prates). His chin ratio is 0.40 per 100 head strikes absorbed career, and 0.76 over the last 3 years (division 0.84). His cardio is steady over 25 minutes.
