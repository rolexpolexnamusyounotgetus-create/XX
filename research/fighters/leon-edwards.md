# Leon Edwards: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Southpaw, 6 ft 2 in, 74 in reach, born 1991-08-25 (age 35). Pro record 22-6, 1 NC; UFC record 14-5, 1 NC (ufcstats). Nickname "Rocky". Former UFC welterweight champion.

**Research notes.** The first research pass and its fact-check (2026-11-06) were done offline from the ufcstats snapshot (through 2026-10-31); the fact-check re-derived every date, method, score and per-fight number from data/raw, and all match. A later web pass the same day re-checked status, ranking, bookings, injuries and appearance against the sources listed below. Fight data and the style breakdown still come from ufcstats.

## Current status

- **Three straight losses.** Belal Muhammad (UD, UFC 304, Jul 27, 2024; lost the title), Sean Brady (guillotine from mount R4, London, Mar 22, 2025) and Carlos Prates (KO R2 1:28, UFC 322, Nov 15, 2025).
- **Inactive about 12 months.** He has not fought since Nov 15, 2025.
- **Scrapped return:** Daniel Rodriguez said in April 2026 that he would fight Edwards at UFC 329 (Jul 11, 2026), but the bout never happened. Rodriguez later said his side asked for it and Edwards' side "were leaning towards another direction". Rodriguez fought Uros Medic instead, and Medic called Edwards out after that win (UFC Belgrade, Aug 1, 2026).
- **Next:** nothing booked as of Nov 6, 2026 (Wikipedia, ESPN profile, ufc.com rankings tables). No injury or retirement announcement found.
- **Ranking:** #8 welterweight in the UFC media rankings (Nov 3, 2026); #10 in the Meta UFC rankings (Oct 31, 2026).

## Style breakdown

A long southpaw kickboxer who scores at range and picks his shots. He throws only 4.0 significant strikes per minute on the decayed sums (welterweight 8.44) but lands 61.5%. He kicks far more than most: on the decayed sums, 19.7% of his attempts go to the body and 18.2% to the legs (division 13.9% and 9.3%). In the clinch (13% of his landed strikes) he works the body. The head kick that took the title from Usman is his signature. He takes the fight down when it is offered (2/3 against Prates) but does not build his game on wrestling.

## Strengths

- Accuracy and shot selection: 61.5% decayed, 57.6% at distance.
- Kicking game to the legs, body and head.
- Five-round experience: 9 scheduled five-rounders, with no fade (round-5 output 0.99x round 1).
- Defends takedowns well for stretches (Covington landed 2/10).

## Weaknesses

- Wrestlers control him: over the last 3 years his takedown defense is 46.7% and he spends 348 s per 15 min being controlled (division 167).
- His output is too low to win rounds when the opponent is busier. He was out-landed in each of his last three fights.
- Age 35, three losses in a row, and a year out.

## Durability and cardio

2 knockdowns absorbed in 335.6 UFC minutes (Barberena 2017, Prates 2025) and 1 KO loss (Prates). His chin ratio is 0.40 per 100 head strikes absorbed career, and 0.76 over the last 3 years (division 0.84). His cardio is steady over 25 minutes.

## What the numbers in fighters.json say

Elo 1710, sixth among welterweights who have fought since November 2024. Decayed: 2.46 significant landed and 2.81 absorbed per minute (a negative differential), and 292 s controlled per 15 minutes. Target split of landed strikes: 47% head, 26% body, 27% legs, the most leg-and-body-heavy of this group. The model should give him a precise, low-volume kicking game whose main risk is wrestling, plus a decline adjustment for age and recent form.
