## Style

She is a wrestle-boxer whose game rests on toughness. At range she throws only punches: 93% of her time-decayed attempts go to the head and 0.5% to the legs. She will stand in the pocket and trade, as she did when she out-landed Nunes in their first fight. Her finishes come from the clinch and on top. She has two rear-naked chokes, and both KO/TKO wins were strikes from mount (TUF 18 final, 2013; elbows vs Dudieva, 2015). Against Shevchenko in 2017 she landed 25 of 30 clinch strikes, 22 of them to the body, before being armbarred from guard.

## Strengths

- Chokes from the back, and she keeps attempting submissions: 0.76 attempts per 15 min career (division 0.32).
- Takedowns are efficient when she commits: 55% career (division 40%).
- Her head defence holds up at range: opponents land 30% of time-decayed head strikes (division 38%).

## Weaknesses

- No kicking game and limited one-shot power: zero knockdowns scored in 12 UFC fights.

## What the numbers say

Her time-decayed numbers are below the division. She lands 2.98 significant strikes per minute (division 3.76) and absorbs 3.28. She lands 0.96 takedowns per 15 min and concedes 1.73. Her Elo of 1611 is seventh among women's bantamweights who have fought since Nov 2024; Harrison is 1619. The engine should treat her as a durable but hittable boxer who gets taken down easily. Her wins should come mainly from chokes and close decisions, not knockouts. Her decayed sums cover only 31 minutes, so priors carry much of the weight.
