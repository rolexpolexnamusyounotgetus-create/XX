# Julianna Pena ("The Venezuelan Vixen"): scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats: `data/derived/fighters.json` and `data/raw/` (ufcstats snapshot to Oct 31, 2026). Status, record, ranking and appearance were re-checked on the web on Nov 6, 2026 (sources at the end).

## Current status

- **Last MMA fight:** lost the UFC women's bantamweight title to Kayla Harrison by kimura from side control at 4:55 of R2, UFC 316 (Jun 7, 2025, Newark). She said afterwards that she went in with a broken thumb and a torn elbow ligament from camp.
- **Since then:** no MMA bout, 17 months to the as-of date. She had elbow surgery in July 2025 and back surgery after that, and in Sep 2025 said she would not be back before 2026 and had not talked to the UFC about a return ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/mma/article/julianna-pena-admits-unclear-timeline-for-ufc-return-amid-mounting-injuries-ive-been-going-through-it-233240070.html)). She has been a Real American Freestyle (RAF) wrestling commentator since Oct 2025.
- **Next competition, not MMA:** a freestyle wrestling match against Diana Avsaragova at RAF Abu Dhabi, Etihad Arena, Nov 14, 2026 ([Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Julianna-Pena-books-wrestling-match-following-UFC-title-loss-to-Kayla-Harrison-203195), [MMA Mania](https://www.mmamania.com/raf-wrestling-schedule-cards-live-results/478814/raf-picks-up-another-former-ufc-champ-julianna-pena-to-debut-in-abu-dhabi)). No MMA bout is booked ([UFCalendar](https://www.ufcalendar.com/fighters/julianna-pena)). She is not retired.
- **Ranking:** No. 1 at women's bantamweight and No. 11 women's pound-for-pound in the UFC media-panel rankings of Nov 3, 2026 (mirrored by UFCalendar and ufcrankings.org). UFC's algorithmic Meta rankings (Oct 31, 2026) have her No. 5.
- **Titles:** none now. She is a two-time former champion. She won the belt from Amanda Nunes at UFC 269 (Dec 11, 2021, RNC R2), lost it to Nunes at UFC 277 (Jul 30, 2022), and won it again from Raquel Pennington at UFC 307 (Oct 5, 2024, split decision).
- **Record:** 12-6 pro (Wikipedia, Sherdog). She is 37. Wikipedia adds that she was promoted to BJJ black belt in Nov 2025.

## Style

She is a wrestle-boxer whose game rests on toughness. At range she throws only punches: 93% of her time-decayed attempts go to the head and 0.5% to the legs. She will stand in the pocket and trade, as she did when she out-landed Nunes in their first fight. Her finishes come from the clinch and on top. She has two rear-naked chokes, and both KO/TKO wins were strikes from mount (TUF 18 final, 2013; elbows vs Dudieva, 2015). Against Shevchenko in 2017 she landed 25 of 30 clinch strikes, 22 of them to the body, before being armbarred from guard.

## Strengths

- Chokes from the back, and she keeps attempting submissions: 0.76 attempts per 15 min career (division 0.32).
- Takedowns are efficient when she commits: 55% career (division 40%).
- Her head defence holds up at range: opponents land 30% of time-decayed head strikes (division 38%).

## Weaknesses

- Takedown defence of 24% career. Opponents average 309 s of control per 15 min against her.
- Submitted three times (Shevchenko armbar, de Randamie guillotine, Harrison kimura).
- No kicking game and limited one-shot power: zero knockdowns scored in 12 UFC fights.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** no KO/TKO losses, but four knockdowns absorbed. That is 1.53 per 100 head strikes absorbed, against a division rate of 0.33.
- **Cardio:** her 5-round pace by round is 1.00, 1.64, 0.78, 0.65 and 1.01 of round 1 (division 1.00, 1.26, 0.95, 1.22, 1.11). Rounds 3-5 come from only two fights. She had her strongest late round vs Pennington (27 landed in R5) but lost both late rounds on volume.

## What the numbers say

Her time-decayed numbers are below the division. She lands 2.98 significant strikes per minute (division 3.76) and absorbs 3.28. She lands 0.96 takedowns per 15 min and concedes 1.73. Her Elo of 1611 is seventh among women's bantamweights who have fought since Nov 2024; Harrison is 1619. The engine should treat her as a durable but hittable boxer who gets taken down easily. Her wins should come mainly from chokes and close decisions, not knockouts. Her decayed sums cover only 31 minutes, so priors carry much of the weight.
