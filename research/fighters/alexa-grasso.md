## Style

She is a boxer first. 82% of her landed significant strikes come at distance, mostly punches to the head. She adds more low kicks than most flyweights: 19% of her time-decayed landed strikes go to the legs (division 16%). She also strikes well in the clinch. Her grappling is opportunistic: she rarely shoots (0.31 takedowns per 15 min, decayed) but takes the back when an opponent turns or overcommits. That is how she beat Shevchenko and Joanne Wood, both by rear-naked choke.

## Strengths

- An iron chin: zero knockdowns absorbed in 16 UFC fights.
- Back takes and the rear-naked choke: 0.63 submission attempts per 15 min career (division 0.41).
- Recent power: two knockdowns in 2026 (the Barber KO and Fiorot in R1). Her head defence is about average: opponents land 34% of their head strikes (division 35%).

## Weaknesses

- Takedown defence: 55% career and 47% time-decayed (division 61%). A committed wrestler can hold her down for long spells, as Shevchenko did for 16 minutes.
- Accuracy and output are below the division: 38.5% accuracy and 3.71 landed per minute, decayed (division 43% and 3.98).
- Volume strikers can out-point her (Silva).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** elite at the UFC level. She has no knockdowns absorbed and no KO losses. Her only stoppage loss is a 2018 rear-naked choke by Tatiana Suarez.
- **Cardio:** she has gone five rounds four times. Her output by round is 1.00, 1.07, 0.83, 0.85 and 1.01 of round 1, against a division curve that rises to 1.32 in R5. Against Fiorot she landed 33, 23 and 24 by round.

## What the numbers say

Her Elo is 1652, sixth among active flyweights, below Fiorot (1672), whom she just beat. The engine should give her near-zero knockdown risk, average-to-low volume and accuracy, and weak takedown offence and defence. She should win by decision or back-take choke, with some recent punching power, and lose to wrestlers who control her or busier strikers who out-point her.
