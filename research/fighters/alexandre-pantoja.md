- **UFC record:** 14-5 (2 KO/TKO, 6 submissions, 6 decisions).
- **Title reign:** won the belt from Brandon Moreno by split decision at UFC 290 (Jul 8, 2023), then beat Brandon Royval (UD, Dec 2023), Steve Erceg (UD, May 2024), Kai Asakura (RNC, Dec 2024) and Kai Kara-France (RNC, Jun 2025).

- **Rematch:** lost to Van by unanimous decision at UFC 331 (Sep 19, 2026; 49-46, 48-47, 50-45). Outlets called it a Fight of the Year contender (per the Van file).
- **Unknowns:** ranking, next fight, new injuries. Van defends against Manel Kape at UFC 336 (Jan 30, 2027).

## Style

Pantoja is a pressure grappler.

- **Grappling:** he chains takedowns (2.8 per 15 min career, 3.7 decayed, against a 1.65 flyweight average) into long control phases, including 15:51 against Royval in 2023. All six submission wins came from the back: five rear-naked chokes and a standing neck crank.
- **Striking:** an ordinary target mix, but poor defence: opponents land 53% of their head strikes on him (decayed), against 37.5% for the division. He trades freely to reach grappling range.

## Strengths

- **Control:** 23.8 s/min decayed, about twice the division rate.
- **Submissions:** 0.88 submission attempts per 15 min career.
- **Accuracy:** 52% sig accuracy (decayed), against 45.6% for the division.
- **Experience:** five title wins, including five-round decisions.

## Weaknesses

- **Absorbing damage:** 5.15 sig absorbed per minute (decayed) against a 3.69 flyweight average.
- **Strikers who stay up:** a fast striker who survives the grappling, as Van did, can outwork him over 25 minutes.
- **Takedown success:** 5 of 18 in the rematch.
- **Age and form:** he is 36 and coming off two straight losses.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never stopped by strikes in the UFC; his only stoppage loss is the arm injury. Knocked down twice in 19 UFC fights, once by Van in the rematch.
- **Cardio:** 5-round output by round is 1.00, 0.92, 1.03, 0.56 and 1.16 of round 1: he keeps going into round 5 apart from a round-4 dip (small sample).

## What the numbers say

The engine should drive Pantoja's chances through grappling: high takedown attempts, long control and back-take submission threats. Opponents should land often on the feet; if his takedowns are defended, the fight becomes a striking contest he tends to lose. Elo 1675.
