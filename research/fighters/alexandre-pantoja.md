# Alexandre Pantoja: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" is `data/derived/fighters.json` plus the raw ufcstats CSVs (through Oct 31, 2026).

**Open-web pass (Nov 6, 2026).** The first pass could not search Pantoja directly. Ranking, record, post-UFC 331 plans, injury detail and appearance are now sourced (links below). Results and stats still come from ufcstats, and the style section is built from the dataset.

## Current status

- **Age and frame:** 36 (born Apr 16, 1990), 5'5" with a 67" reach, orthodox. Nickname "The Cannibal".
- **Record:** 30-7 pro (8 KO/TKO, 12 submissions, 10 decisions; losses: 1 TKO, which is the elbow injury, and 6 decisions) per Sherdog, ESPN and Wikipedia. UFC 14-5 (2 KO/TKO, 6 submissions, 6 decisions). Fights out of American Top Team.
- **Title reign:** won the belt from Brandon Moreno by split decision at UFC 290 (Jul 8, 2023), then beat Brandon Royval (UD, Dec 2023), Steve Erceg (UD, May 2024), Kai Asakura (RNC, Dec 2024) and Kai Kara-France (RNC, Jun 2025).
- **Losing the belt:** lost to Joshua Van at UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025) when he dislocated his elbow falling awkwardly after Van caught his head kick, ending the fight at 0:26 of round 1 (ESPN; Bloody Elbow via Wikipedia).
- **Rematch:** lost to Van by unanimous decision at UFC 331 (Sep 19, 2026; 49-46, 48-47, 50-45 per ufcstats). It earned Fight of the Night, and outlets called it a Fight of the Year contender.
- **Ranking:** #1 flyweight contender and #14 men's pound-for-pound in the official UFC rankings (media panel, released Nov 3, 2026; ufc.com via a Wayback capture of Nov 4); #2 flyweight, behind Manel Kape, in the Elo-based Meta UFC rankings released Oct 31, 2026.
- **Next fight:** none, and no timetable. On Nov 1, 2026 his ATT coach Marcos "Parrumpa" da Matta told Sherdog that Pantoja needs to rest "not just his body, but his mind too" after camp after camp of five-round fights since 2023, that "we have no timetable at all for his return", and that "maybe next year" they start thinking about fighting again. Demetrious Johnson suggested he consider retiring (MMA Junkie, Sep 23); Pantoja has announced nothing of the kind and posted a Rocky Balboa quote about moving forward (Heavy, Sep 20). The scouting file keeps him active: he has not retired and has no reported injury.
- **Injuries:** no new injury reported after UFC 331. Van defends against Manel Kape at UFC 336 (Jan 30, 2027).

## Style

Pantoja is a pressure grappler.

- **Grappling:** he chains takedowns (2.8 per 15 min career, 3.7 decayed, against a 1.65 flyweight average) into long control phases, including 15:51 against Royval in 2023. All six submission wins came from the back: five rear-naked chokes and a standing neck crank.
- **Striking:** an ordinary target mix, but poor defence: opponents land 53% of their head strikes on him (decayed), against 37.5% for the division. He trades freely to reach grappling range.

## Strengths

- **Control:** 23.8 s/min decayed, about twice the division rate.
- **Submissions:** 0.88 submission attempts per 15 min career.
- **Accuracy:** 52% sig accuracy (decayed), against 45.6% for the division.
- **Experience:** five title wins, including five-round decisions.

## Weaknesses

- **Absorbing damage:** 5.15 sig absorbed per minute (decayed) against a 3.69 flyweight average.
- **Strikers who stay up:** a fast striker who survives the grappling, as Van did, can outwork him over 25 minutes.
- **Takedown success:** 5 of 18 in the rematch.
- **Age and form:** he is 36 and coming off two straight losses.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never stopped by strikes in the UFC; his only stoppage loss is the arm injury. Knocked down twice in 19 UFC fights, once by Van in the rematch.
- **Cardio:** 5-round output by round is 1.00, 0.92, 1.03, 0.56 and 1.16 of round 1: he keeps going into round 5 apart from a round-4 dip (small sample).

## What the numbers say

The engine should drive Pantoja's chances through grappling: high takedown attempts, long control and back-take submission threats. Opponents should land often on the feet; if his takedowns are defended, the fight becomes a striking contest he tends to lose. Elo 1675.
