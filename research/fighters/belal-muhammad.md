## Style breakdown

A pressure wrestle-boxer. On the decayed sums he throws 10.2 significant strikes per minute (welterweight 8.44), almost all punches to the head at distance (88% of attempts to the head, 91% of landed strikes at distance), with very few kicks. He mixes in takedowns (4.5 attempts per 15 min, 35% landed) and rides top position when he gets it. His output climbs through five-round fights, to 1.52x round 1 in round 4 and 1.61x in round 5. He wins by accumulation: 12 of his 15 UFC wins are decisions.
