# Belal Muhammad: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 11 in, 72 in reach, born 1988-07-09 (age 38). Pro record 24-6, 1 NC; UFC record 15-6, 1 NC (ufcstats). Nickname "Remember the Name". Former UFC welterweight champion.

## Style breakdown

A pressure wrestle-boxer. On the decayed sums he throws 10.2 significant strikes per minute (welterweight 8.44), almost all punches to the head at distance (88% of attempts to the head, 91% of landed strikes at distance), with very few kicks. He mixes in takedowns (4.5 attempts per 15 min, 35% landed) and rides top position when he gets it. His output climbs through five-round fights, to 1.52x round 1 in round 4 and 1.61x in round 5. He wins by accumulation: 12 of his 15 UFC wins are decisions.

## Strengths

- Volume and cardio over 25 minutes.
- Takedown defense: 89.9% decayed (division 64.1%). Brady went 0/5 against him.
- Control when his takedowns land (12:02 against Edwards).
- Durability: no knockdowns absorbed since 2019.

## Weaknesses

- No power: 0 knockdowns in 22 UFC fights.
- Accuracy is low (40.4% decayed), and opponents now land 52.2% on him over the last 3 years (career 44.8%).
- Leg kicks: he absorbs 1.0 per minute decayed (division 0.66), and both Machado Garry and Bonfim chopped his legs.
- His offensive wrestling has stopped working: 0/9 takedowns in his last two fights.
- Age 38 and three losses in a row.

## Durability and cardio

6 career knockdowns absorbed, all in 2016-2019, and 1 KO loss (Vicente Luque, 2016). None in 263 head strikes absorbed over the last 3 years. His cardio is a real strength, but late rounds go both ways: in round 5 of five-round fights he absorbs 5.32 per minute and opponents land 57.6%.

## What the numbers in fighters.json say

Elo 1701, seventh among welterweights who have fought since November 2024. Decayed: 4.11 significant landed and 4.42 absorbed per minute. Over the last 3 years his strike differential is -0.92 per minute (career +0.44). Target split of landed strikes: 78% head, 16% body, 6% legs. His five-round pace curve rests on 25 minutes per round and is one of the better-supported late-surge signals in the dataset. The model should treat him as a volume decision fighter with no knockout threat whose form is declining.
