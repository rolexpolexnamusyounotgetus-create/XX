# Bryce "Thug Nasty" Mitchell — scouting dossier (as of Nov 6, 2026)

## Current status
- Bantamweight since mid-2025 (previously featherweight) and 2-0 at 135. Pro MMA record 19-3 (Sherdog, UFCalendar; ESPN and ufcrankings.org list 19-4, which Sherdog's fight list does not support). UFC record in the dataset: 10-3.
- Last MMA fight: arm-triangle submission of Santiago Luna at 4:52 of R3, UFC Vegas 118, Jun 6, 2026, with about eight seconds left. Luna was 8-0 and had replaced Victor Henry (withdrew May 28).
- Since then: he lost a 15-minute featherweight grappling show match to UFC BJJ bantamweight champion Mikey Musumeci by unanimous decision, headlining UFC BJJ 11 at Meta APEX on Sep 24, 2026 (no title at stake; Musumeci agreed not to finish with leglocks). Mitchell escaped a D'Arce and a guillotine and slammed Musumeci out of a flying triangle in round 3; Musumeci later took his back and hunted a rear-naked choke and an armbar (bjj.tv, citing UFC.com's recap). It does not count on his MMA record. Earlier, on Mar 11, 2026, he drew an 8-minute grappling match with Jean Silva at Hype Fighting: Brazil (Wikipedia, citing Tapology; UFCalendar agrees).
- Next: no MMA bout booked as of Nov 6 (UFCalendar; ESPN lists only the Luna fight). A Cageside Press column (Oct 5) suggested Deiveson Figueiredo as his next opponent.
- Ranking: No. 15 bantamweight in the UFC media-panel rankings of Nov 3, 2026, as mirrored by UFCalendar and ufcrankings.org (ufc.com returns 403 to scripts); No. 13 on the UFC's algorithmic Meta board of Oct 31. Injuries: none reported; he said he was "dinged up" after the Luna fight (Cageside).

## Recent fights
- **W Santiago Luna, sub (arm-triangle) R3, Jun 2026.** A grappling fight: 2 of 5 takedowns and 11:40 of control. Luna reversed and held top briefly in R2 before Mitchell took over again and finished late.
- **W Said Nurmagomedov, UD 29-28 x3, Jul 2025 (bantamweight debut).** He was dropped once, but 3 of 9 takedowns and 9:44 of control carried it. Only 13 significant strikes landed.
- **L Jean Silva, sub (front-headlock/"ninja" choke, officially guillotine) R2, UFC 314, Apr 2025.** He landed 21 of 21 leg kicks but only 1 of 14 takedowns. Silva dropped him with a left hook and right hand and choked him unconscious.
- **W Kron Gracie, TKO (elbows from top) R3, UFC 310, Dec 2024**: 2 of 2 takedowns, 8:33 of control.
- **L Josh Emmett, KO R1 1:57, UFC 296, Dec 2023**: one punch at distance. **W Dan Ige, UD, Sep 2023**: 5 of 15 takedowns, 7:33 control.

## Style
A southpaw grappler. He spends most of his fight time on top (55% control for his career, 61% decayed), passes, and finishes with arm-triangles (Luna), a twister (Matt Sayles, 2019) or elbows (Kron Gracie). Standing he is low-volume but accurate (59%). He kicks the legs and body from southpaw to set up shots, and a large share of his strikes go to the body (26% of attempts).

**Strengths:** top control and riding; submission chains from top; patience; accurate if sparse striking; a low strike-absorption rate (1.51 per minute).

**Weaknesses:** a plan that depends on takedowns, at 36% career accuracy and far lower against good defenders (Silva 1 of 14, Topuria 1 of 9). When the fight stays standing against a puncher he has been dropped (four knockdowns absorbed) or stopped (Emmett). Low output (2.19 landed per minute) leaves little margin on the feet.

**Durability:** a concern. Four knockdowns absorbed in 13 UFC fights despite little incoming volume; one KO loss and two submission losses that followed knockdowns. **Cardio:** he has fought hard 15-minute grappling fights, but his round-3 landed rate is 0.61 of round 1. That is partly because late rounds become control time. He has never had a scheduled 5-round fight.
