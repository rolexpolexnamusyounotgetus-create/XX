# Curtis Blaydes: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" = `data/derived/fighters.json` (ufcstats snapshot to Oct 31, 2026). Fight numbers re-checked against raw ufcstats rows on Nov 6.

**Research note (updated Nov 6, 2026):** the network was open for this pass. Status, ranking, record, bookings, injuries and appearance were re-checked on the web. The pages on the "Nov 6 re-check" and "Photos" lines under Sources were fetched on Nov 6 unless marked as a search summary; older source lines are as the earlier pass left them. ufc.com returned 403, so the official rankings were read through two mirrors that agree. ## Current status

- **Ranking:** #5 heavyweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026) and #6 in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31, 2026).
- **Form:** 1-1 in 2026. He lost a 28-29 x3 decision to Josh Hokit at UFC 327 in Miami (Apr 11, 2026), then beat Waldo Cortes Acosta 29-28 x3 at Noche UFC in Glendale, Arizona (Sep 12, 2026).
- **Title history:** his last title fight was the interim-belt loss to Tom Aspinall at UFC 304 (Jul 27, 2024), a KO in 60 seconds.
- **Record:** 20-6 (1 NC) pro per UFCalendar and Wikipedia; 15-6 (1 NC) in the UFC per ufcstats. The NC is a 2017 win overturned after a failed drug test.
- **Age and frame:** 35 (born Feb 18, 1991), 6'4", 80" reach, orthodox. Nickname "Razor".
- **Next:** nothing booked on Nov 6. His UFCalendar page lists no next fight, and ufcrankings.org's list of upcoming bouts for ranked heavyweights does not include him. After the Cortes Acosta win he said he wants a top-5 opponent next (BJPenn, Sep 15). Hokit now challenges champion Ciryl Gane for the heavyweight title at UFC 334 (Nov 14, 2026).
- **Injuries:** the Hokit fight left him with a fractured orbital bone and a broken nose (MMA Fighting, Apr 12, 2026); he was back five months later. Before that he had meniscus surgery after the June 2025 Kuniev fight and sat out the rest of 2025 (Afro Combat, Aug 2025).

## Style

- **Wrestling:** he has 70 UFC takedowns, the most of any fighter listed at heavyweight in the dataset, and 26 s/min of career control.
- **Damage from top:** three of his finishes are elbows from top (East, Overeem, Abdurakhimov).
- **Standing power:** punches at range knocked out Junior dos Santos and Chris Daukaus.
- **Recent shift:** since 2025 the takedowns have landed less often (33% decayed), and he has fought more on the feet and in the clinch.

## Strengths

- **Takedowns and control:** 5.0 takedowns per 15 min career, against a 1.1 heavyweight average.
- **Reach and power:** an 80" reach and 8 KO/TKO wins.

## Weaknesses

- **Chin against big punchers:** he has four KO/TKO losses by strikes, all in rounds 1-2, against Ngannou, Lewis, Pavlovich and Aspinall. He has been knocked down five times.
- **Takedowns landing less:** 2 of 15, 2 of 7 and 4 of 10 in his last three fights.
- **Brawling:** standing exchanges invite damage. He absorbs 4.5 sig strikes per minute (decayed), against 2.7 career.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** every knockdown since Mark Hunt (Feb 2018) came in a KO loss to a top puncher.
- **Cardio:** fine over three rounds (he out-landed Hokit 56-54 in R2). He has gone five rounds only once (Volkov, 2020, a decision win), so his five-round pace vector carries almost no information.

## What the numbers say

Decayed: lands 4.91 per minute, absorbs 4.53; 9.6 takedown attempts and 3.2 landed per 15 min; 17 s/min control; attempt split 84.4% head, 5.9% body, 9.7% leg. Elo 1694. The engine should still make him a heavy takedown threat at below-career accuracy. He should be an above-average finisher from top. His knockout-loss risk should sit above the heavyweight prior against power punchers.

## Appearance

Read from ESPN's fight-week photos (headshot and right stance Sep 10, 2026; left stance Apr 9, 2026). No photo-based likeness sheet exists for him.

- **Hair:** short black hair, a close-cropped textured top with faded sides. Not a shaved head, as the earlier draft guessed.
- **Facial hair:** full, thick black beard.
- **Skin:** dark.
- **Tattoos:** a large black tribal piece over the right shoulder and upper arm, a red-and-black design on the left shoulder, and faint linework on the right arm. The detail is too small to describe further.
