# Benoit Saint Denis: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Southpaw, 5'11", 73-inch reach, born 1995-12-18 in Nîmes, France. Nicknamed "God of War"; served in the French Army special forces before turning pro. 17-4 pro (Yahoo Sports), 9-4 in the UFC.*

## Current status
**Active and booked.** He headlines **UFC Fight Night Sydney** against unbeaten **Quillan Salkilld** over five rounds at Afterpay Arena on **Feb 7, 2027** (Feb 6 US time; announced Oct 27, 2026). His last fight was a 52-second submission loss to **Paddy Pimblett** at UFC 329 on **Jul 11, 2026**, the first submission loss of his career. He dropped three places in the rankings afterwards. Early-November lists have him anywhere from #6 (Home of Fight) to #8 (ufcalendar.com). He has no reported injuries.

## Recent fights
- **L Pimblett, UFC 329 (Jul 2026), D'Arce / Peruvian-necktie choke in round 1 at 0:52.** He threw a high kick and shot. Pimblett sprawled and choked him unconscious.
- **W Dan Hooker, UFC 325 (Jan 31, 2026), TKO in round 2 at 4:45 by punches from mount.** He landed 97 of 127 significant strikes, 67 of them on the ground, with 6:14 of control.
- **W Beneil Dariush, UFC 322 (Nov 2025), KO in 16 seconds** with a left hand after Dariush's calf kick had briefly put him down (UFC Stats scored no knockdown for Dariush).
- **W Mauricio Ruffy (Sep 2025, rear-naked choke in round 2) and Kyle Prepolec (May 2025, arm triangle in round 2).**
- **L Renato Moicano (Sep 2024), doctor stoppage after round 2** of a five-round main event. Moicano landed 33 ground strikes.
- **L Dustin Poirier, UFC 299 (Mar 2024), KO in round 2.** He won round 1 clearly (38-12 in significant strikes, 3:20 of control) and was knocked out early in round 2.

## Style
He is a southpaw wrestle-boxer who works at a relentless pace. He walks forward behind a heavy left hand and body kicks (26% of his landed strikes go to the body, against a median of 22%), clinches, and chains takedown attempts against the fence: about 12 per 15 minutes, landing about 35%. On top he hammers from mount and finishes with rear-naked chokes or arm triangles. He also has stand-up stopping power: a head-kick KO of Matt Frevola and the 16-second KO of Dariush.

## Strengths
- **Ground damage and control:** 31-38% of his significant strikes land on the ground (median about 9%), and he controls 47-50% of fight time.
- **Finishing:** all of his UFC wins are KO/TKOs or submissions; knockdowns at 0.73-0.86 per 15 minutes.
- **Volume:** 5.6-5.9 significant strikes landed per minute at 59-62% accuracy.

## Weaknesses
- **His entries are exploitable:** Pimblett choked him off a sprawl, and Poirier attempted four submissions against him.
- **Defence:** about 42% significant-strike defence (median about 54%).
- **Five-round record of 0-2:** both losses came in round 2 after fast starts.

## Durability and cardio
He was KO'd once (Poirier) and stopped by a doctor once (Moicano), with only one knockdown absorbed in the UFC. Against Poirier his output fell from 38 significant strikes in round 1 to 12 in round 2. fighters.json has pace data only for rounds 1-2 (1.00 / 1.04), so his output in rounds 3-5 against Salkilld is unproven.

## What the numbers say
His Elo is 1653 (11th among recently active lightweights). His profile mixes elite pressure grappling, real power and poor defence. He beats opponents who cannot stop his shots or get up, and he is vulnerable to clean counter-punchers and front-headlock specialists.
