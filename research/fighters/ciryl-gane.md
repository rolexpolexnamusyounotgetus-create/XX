# Ciryl Gane: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Bon Gamin". Orthodox (switches often), 6 ft 4 in, 81 in reach, listed 245 lb, born 1990-04-12 (age 36). UFC record 11-2, 1 NC (ufcstats).

## Current status

- **Title:** UFC interim heavyweight champion. He stopped Alex Pereira at 1:27 of round 2 at UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn (Jun 14, 2026). A counter jab put Pereira down, and Gane out-landed him 54-12 (ufcstats).
- **Context:** Tom Aspinall still holds the undisputed title. Their UFC 321 fight (Oct 25, 2025) was ruled a no contest at 4:35 of round 1 after Gane's eye poke.
- **Next fight:** Josh Hokit, booking reported by ESPN in Oct 2026. The date, event and whether the interim belt is at stake were not confirmed in the sources I had. Pereira has publicly asked for a rematch.
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style breakdown

A Muay Thai-based striker who fights like a kickboxer in MMA. He circles, switches stance, jabs, and kicks the legs and body from the edge of range, so bigger punchers struggle to set their feet. He is a patient counter-puncher with the jab and finishes hurt opponents with punches on the ground. His wrestling is limited (8 of 34 takedowns), and his get-ups and takedown defense are the weak link. Ngannou beat him by wrestling over the last three rounds, and Jones finished him from a takedown.

## Strengths

- Leg-kick accuracy of 89% career, and he lands 79% of his body strikes.
- Movement and stance switches make him hard to hit cleanly. The one knockdown he absorbed in 14 fights came against Tuivasa.
- Out-landed Spivac, Tuivasa, Lewis and Pereira by wide margins.
- Five-round experience: 9 five-round fights.

## Weaknesses

- Takedown defense of 47% career.
- His output falls off in round 5.
- Fouls: the eye poke, and Pereira's complaint about shots to the back of the head.
- Head accuracy of only 47%; much of his work goes to the legs and body.

## Durability and cardio

No KO/TKO losses and one knockdown absorbed. His pace in five-round fights runs 1.00, 1.57, 1.64, 1.06 and 0.60 of round 1. That is strong in rounds 2-3, then a fade, though the round-5 sample is only 15 minutes.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums: 9.2 significant attempts and 6.1 landed per minute at 66% accuracy; he absorbs 3.5 per minute, and opponents land 52%. Of his attempts, 60% go to the head, 20% to the body and 20% to the legs. On the ground he lands 0.63 takedowns per 15 min, while opponents control him about 92 s per 15 min. Elo 1755.
