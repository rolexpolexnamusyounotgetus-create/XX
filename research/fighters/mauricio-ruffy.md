# Mauricio Ruffy: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Orthodox, 5'11", 75-inch reach, born 1996-06-17 (age 30) in Coruripe, Alagoas, Brazil. Real name Maurício de Lima Santos; his late brother gave him the name "Ruffy", taken from One Piece (Wikipedia). Pro record 14-3-0 with 13 KO/TKO wins (ESPN, UFCalendar); UFC 5-2 in fighters.json (7 fights since his May 2024 debut).*

> Research note: this file was first written without web access. On Nov 6, 2026 the status, ranking, record, camp and appearance were checked on the web (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org, MMA Mania, Superlutas/Superfight, MiddleEasy, Cageside Press, Wikipedia via its API, ESPN studio photos and a public-domain White House photo). ufc.com returned 403 to scripts and Bloody Elbow returned 403, so the official rankings page and the Bloody Elbow camp story were not read directly. Anything still unconfirmed is marked below.

## Current status
**Active, coming off a knockout loss, and not fighting again in 2026 by his own account.** On **Sep 19, 2026**, in the five-round UFC 331 co-main event in Los Angeles, Arman Tsarukyan knocked him out with an elbow in the clinch at 4:56 of round 1. The loss ended a two-fight win streak:
- **W Rafael Fiziev**, UFC 325, Sydney (**Jan 31, 2026** US time; Feb 1 local), $100,000 Performance of the Night
- **W Michael Chandler**, UFC Freedom 250, White House South Lawn, Washington, DC (**Jun 14, 2026**)

- **Ranking:** **No. 8** at lightweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, per UFCalendar; ufcrankings.org, updated Nov 5, agrees). **No. 10** in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31 board, published Nov 3; UFCalendar, MMA Mania). An earlier version of this file called No. 10 his media-panel spot; that mixed up the two boards.
- **Next fight:** none. On **Oct 15, 2026** he denied reports of a December fight with Salahdine Parnasse: "I'm not going to fight now." The news he had teased was about his own training space, where he said Volkanovski and Israel Adesanya are coming to train (Superfight/Superlutas). ESPN and UFCalendar list no booking on Nov 6.
- **Injury/medical:** the California commission suspended him 45 days (30 with no contact) after UFC 331, plus 60 days unless a physician clears a cut near his right eye. That runs to about **Nov 18, 2026** (MMA Mania, Sep 22). No other injury was reported.
- **Camp:** he prepared for UFC 325 at Volkanovski's Freestyle MMA in Sydney while saying he was still a Fighting Nerd (Cageside Press, Jan 29, 2026). A May 8, 2026 Bloody Elbow headline says he "officially left the Fighting Nerds" (search snippet only), and Superlutas calls him a "former Fighting Nerds member" (Oct 15). ESPN still lists Fighting Nerds as his team.
- **Personal:** he proposed to his partner on the White House lawn after the Chandler win (Yahoo Sports headline).

## Recent fights
- **L Tsarukyan, UFC 331 (Sep 2026), KO R1 4:56.** Tsarukyan went 1 of 10 on takedowns but held 3:00 of control, scored a knockdown and finished him with an elbow in the clinch. Ruffy landed 4 significant strikes.
- **W Chandler, Freedom 250 (Jun 2026), TKO R1 4:29.** He outlanded Chandler 19-3 with two knockdowns, stopped all 3 of Chandler's takedown attempts and finished with ground punches (UFC Stats). Reports say spinning kicks did much of the damage. MiddleEasy describes spinning back kicks to the body and head, the last of which dropped Chandler, and MMA Fighting's headline says spinning heel kick and uppercut. Wikipedia says spinning wheel kick and punches.
- **W Fiziev, UFC 325 (Jan 2026), TKO R2 4:30.** He landed 55 of 88 significant strikes, 45 of them to the head, scored a knockdown and finished on the ground. Fiziev had answered with 15 body and 12 leg strikes.
- **L Benoit Saint Denis, Paris (Sep 6, 2025), submission R2 2:56.** Saint Denis went 3 of 10 on takedowns with 4:38 of control. UFC Stats calls it a rear-naked choke; Wikipedia calls it a face crank.
- **W King Green, UFC 313 (Mar 2025), spinning wheel kick KO R1 2:07.** ESPN, MMA Fighting and others named it Knockout of the Year for 2025 (Wikipedia).
- **W James Llontop, UFC 309 (Nov 2024), 29-28 x3.** Llontop outlanded him 90-67, but Ruffy landed 53 head strikes to Llontop's 29 and scored a knockdown.
- **W Jamie Mullarkey, UFC 301 (May 2024), KO R1 4:42.**

## Style
He is a rangy power striker. 86% of his significant attempts go to the head, he lands 56.5% of them (median about 47%), and he rarely throws to the body. His finishes come three ways: straight punches at distance (Mullarkey), knockdowns followed by ground strikes (Fiziev, Chandler) and spinning kicks (the wheel-kick KO of Green, plus the spinning kicks that hurt Chandler). He does not wrestle offensively: 0 of 1 on takedowns and no submission attempts.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 6 knockdowns in 48.7 minutes (1.85 per 15), about six times the lightweight median.
- **Head defence at range:** only 37% of the significant strikes opponents land on him go to the head (lightweight landed-head median about 62%). Overall he defends 59% of significant strikes (median about 54%).
- **First-contact takedown defence:** 87% (26 of 30 stopped).

## Weaknesses
- **Sustained grappling pressure:** both losses came against wrestlers or grapplers who kept shooting and clinching (Saint Denis, Tsarukyan).
- **Body and leg damage:** 63% of what opponents land goes to the body or legs.
- **Sample size:** all his rates rest on under 49 minutes.

## Durability and cardio
Tsarukyan dropped him once and finished him with an elbow. It is his only KO loss and the only knockdown he has absorbed in the UFC. His cardio is **unknown**: only the Llontop fight went 15 minutes, and his only five-round booking (Tsarukyan, UFC 331) ended in round 1.
