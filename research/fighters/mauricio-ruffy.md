## Style
He is a rangy power striker. 86% of his significant attempts go to the head, he lands 56.5% of them (median about 47%), and he rarely throws to the body. His finishes come three ways: straight punches at distance (Mullarkey), knockdowns followed by ground strikes (Fiziev, Chandler) and a spinning wheel kick (Green). He does not wrestle offensively: 0 of 1 on takedowns and no submission attempts.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 6 knockdowns in 48.7 minutes (1.85 per 15), about six times the lightweight median.
- **Head defence at range:** opponents land only 37% of their significant strikes to his head. Overall he defends 59% of significant strikes (median about 54%).
- **First-contact takedown defence:** 87% (26 of 30 stopped).

## Weaknesses
- **Sustained grappling pressure:** both losses came against wrestlers or grapplers who kept shooting and clinching (Saint Denis, Tsarukyan).
- **Body and leg damage:** 63% of what opponents land goes to the body or legs.
- **Sample size:** all his rates rest on under 49 minutes.

## Durability and cardio
Tsarukyan dropped him once and finished him with an elbow, his only KO loss and only knockdown absorbed in the UFC. His cardio is **unknown**: only the Llontop fight went 15 minutes, and he has never fought past round 3.
