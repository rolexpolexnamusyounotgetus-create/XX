## Current status
- **Former UFC welterweight champion.** He beat Tyron Woodley for the title at UFC 235 (Mar 2, 2019). He defended it five times: Covington (TKO R5, UFC 245), Masvidal (UD), Gilbert Burns (TKO R3), Masvidal again (KO R2, UFC 261) and Covington again (UD, UFC 268). Leon Edwards took the belt with a head kick at 4:04 of R5 at UFC 278 (Aug 20, 2022).
- **Since then:** L Edwards (MD, Mar 2023), L Khamzat Chimaev (MD at middleweight, Oct 2023), W Joaquin Buckley (UD, five-round welterweight main event, Jun 14, 2025), and L Dricus Du Plessis (UD 50-45, 49-46, 49-46, five-round middleweight main event of UFC Fight Night 281, Oklahoma City, Jul 18, 2026; Fight of the Night).
- **Record:** 21-5 pro (ESPN, Wikipedia); UFC 16-4 (4 KO/TKO, 1 SUB, 11 DEC); 1-4 in his last five; 0-2 at middleweight.
- **Ranking:** still ranked at welterweight: #9, tied with Mike Malott, on the UFC media panel (Nov 3-5, 2026) and #11 in the Meta UFC Rankings (Oct 31, 2026). Not in the middleweight top 15.
- **Future:** no fight booked and no retirement announced. On his podcast after the loss he said he had to sit with his coaches and decide whether it is worth continuing (ESPN, Jul 24, 2026). His manager Ali Abdelaziz said on Aug 6 that he is not retiring and should go back to welterweight (MMA Mania, citing MMA Junkie). A Bloody Elbow headline of Sep 29 says he is open to a fight with YouTuber KSI "amid uncertainty over return" (403, not read). One low-quality site claims he retired in July; ESPN and his manager contradict it.
- **Injuries:** co-host Henry Cejudo said on their podcast that Usman fought Du Plessis with a torn pec, and Usman did not use it as an excuse (MMA Mania, Jul 24). Usman said an early thumb-punch to the eye left him seeing double for the rest of the fight (AgentMMA, Jul 19).

## Recent fights
- **L Du Plessis (UD, Jul 2026):** He went 0 for 7 on takedowns and was out-landed 136-92, nearly all of it at distance. He was out-landed in R1-R4 and edged R5 23-22 in significant strikes. His best weapon was the body, where he landed 37 of 58.
- **W Buckley (UD, Jun 2025):** He landed 4 of 13 takedowns and banked 12:57 of control, front-loaded: 4:25 in R1, then 3:08, 2:35, 2:18 and 0:31. Buckley out-landed him 44-41 overall and 20-10 in R5, but the control won the cards (49-46, 49-46, 48-47).
- **L Chimaev (MD, Oct 2023):** He lost R1 to 4:35 of Chimaev control and won the striking in R2-R3 (33-22). Two judges still had Chimaev ahead.
- **L Edwards (MD, Mar 2023):** Edwards out-landed him 120-87, with 50 of 53 leg strikes landing. Usman went 4 for 15 on takedowns.

## Style
A wrestle-boxer. In his title years he walked opponents to the fence, mixed punches with clinch work and takedowns, and held top position. His career control share is 40%, and 40% of his landed strikes came in the clinch or on the ground. He goes to the body much more than average (20% of attempts against a league 14%; 41 of 50 body strikes landed against Covington at UFC 268). He has stopping power: Masvidal was knocked out by a punch at 1:02 of R2. His takedown defense is excellent (90% career; Covington went 0 for 11).

**Strengths:** pressure, body punching, fence control, takedown defense, five-round conditioning, durability.

**Weaknesses:** His takedowns have stopped landing (27.6% decayed vs 42% career, 4 of 20 over his last two fights). He is undersized at middleweight. Kickers trouble him: Edwards landed 50 of 53 leg strikes in 2023 and finished him with a head kick in 2022. At 39 he has fought three times since March 2023.

## Durability and cardio
In 20 UFC fights he has been knocked down **once**, by the Edwards head kick, which is also his only stoppage loss. In 12 five-round fights his R2-R5 output runs 1.49, 1.25, 1.41 and 1.21 times R1, busier than the welterweight curve (1.27, 1.15, 1.16, 1.08). The Buckley R5 (out-landed 20-10, 0 for 6 on takedowns) hints at slippage.
