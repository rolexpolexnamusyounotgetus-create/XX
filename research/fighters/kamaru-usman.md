## Current status
- **Former UFC welterweight champion.** He beat Tyron Woodley for the title at UFC 235 (Mar 2, 2019). He defended it five times: Covington (TKO R5, UFC 245), Masvidal (UD), Gilbert Burns (TKO R3), Masvidal again (KO R2, UFC 261) and Covington again (UD, UFC 268). Leon Edwards took the belt with a head kick at 4:04 of R5 at UFC 278 (Aug 20, 2022).
- **Since then:** L Edwards (MD, Mar 2023), L Khamzat Chimaev (MD at middleweight, Oct 2023), W Joaquin Buckley (UD, five-round welterweight main event, Jun 14, 2025), and L Dricus Du Plessis (UD 50-45, 49-46, 49-46, five-round middleweight main event, Oklahoma City, Jul 18, 2026).
- **UFC record 16-4** (4 KO/TKO, 1 SUB, 11 DEC); 0-2 at middleweight. He won the TUF 21 welterweight tournament final in 2015 by arm triangle. No booking found.

## Style
A wrestle-boxer. In his title years he walked opponents to the fence, mixed punches with clinch work and takedowns, and held top position. His career control share is 40%, and 40% of his landed strikes came in the clinch or on the ground. He goes to the body much more than average (20% of attempts against a league 14%; 41 of 50 body strikes landed against Covington at UFC 268). He has one-punch power: Masvidal was knocked out at 1:02 of R2. His takedown defense is excellent (90% career; Covington went 0 for 11).

**Strengths:** pressure, body punching, fence control, takedown defense, five-round conditioning, durability.

**Weaknesses:** His takedowns have stopped landing (27.6% decayed vs 42% career, 4 of 20 over his last two fights). He is undersized at middleweight. Kickers trouble him: Edwards took him apart with leg kicks in 2023 and finished him with a head kick in 2022. At 39 he has fought three times since March 2023.
