As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats are from `data/derived/fighters.json` (ufcstats snapshot through Oct 31, 2026) unless a link is given.

## Current status

- **BMF belt holder** (ceremonial, not a UFC championship). He won it from Max Holloway at UFC 326 (Mar 7, 2026) by unanimous decision, 50-45 on all three cards ([UFC scorecards](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-326-official-scorecards-holloway-vs-oliveira-2), [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/mma/story/_/id/48091127/ufc-326-live-results-analysis-bmf-title-max-holloway-vs-charles-oliveira-2)).
- **Next fight:** the UFC announced on Oct 4, 2026 that he defends the belt against Diego Lopes in the UFC 335 main event, Dec 12, 2026, T-Mobile Arena. It is Lopes's lightweight debut after two featherweight title losses ([UFC.com](https://www.ufc.com/news/oliveira-defends-bmf-title-against-lopes-pereira-faces-pavlovich-ufc-335), [Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/events/UFC-335-Oliveira-vs-Lopes-114294)).
- **Ranking:** No. 3 lightweight on the media-panel list of Nov 3, 2026 ([ufcrankings.org](https://ufcrankings.org/), [UFCalendar](https://www.ufcalendar.com/rankings/lightweight)).
- Age 37. Former UFC lightweight champion (won it vs Chandler at UFC 262, 2021). No current injury found.

## Style

He is a submission grappler who strikes to get the fight to the mat. Seventeen of his 25 UFC wins are submissions: 7 rear-naked chokes, 4 guillotines (several after dropping to guard), 3 anacondas, 2 triangles and a calf slicer. Standing, he walks forward behind a long jab and right hand (74-inch reach). At UFC 274 he dropped Gaethje with a right, then took his back and choked him ([Cageside](https://cagesidepress.com/2022/05/08/ufc-274-results-justin-gaethje-vs-charles-oliveira)). His clinch share (18% of landed strikes career) and body share (20% of attempts) are both above the lightweight norms (9.5% and 15%). He shoots 5.7 times per 15 minutes at 40% (league 4.3 at 36%).

**Strengths:** back takes, scrambles and finishing from bad spots. Submission attempts run at 2.6 per 15 minutes (league 0.40). Control lately is heavy: 485 seconds per 15 minutes decayed (league 174).

**Weaknesses:** he gets hit. Over the last three years opponents landed 63% of their significant strikes on him (league 48%). Analyst Michael Bisping has flagged his forward walk with the chin up ([LowKick](https://www.lowkickmma.com/charles-oliveira-fight-style-warning/)). His takedown defense over three years is 50%. His striking output is low: 3.6 significant attempts per minute, ground strikes included (league 9.0).

## Durability and cardio

He has absorbed 6 knockdowns and lost 5 times by KO/TKO in 37 UFC fights; one, Holloway in 2015, was an injury stoppage. His decayed chin rate is 1.12 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed (league 0.95). The Topuria knockout is the most recent. In five-round fights his landed rate falls to 0.67 and 0.62 of round 1 in rounds 4 and 5, but that is mostly control time. Against Holloway he held 4:16 or more of control in each of rounds 1-4 and 2:54 in round 5. In the two fights that went 25 minutes, he absorbed 4.5 significant strikes per minute in round 5, and opponents landed 80% of their attempts.

## What fighters.json says

His Elo of 1778 is the highest among current lightweights in the file. UFC record 25-11 (1 NC); last fight Mar 7, 2026; 10 five-round bouts. His decayed target split is 70% head, 20% body and 11% legs. Ground strikes are 29% of what he lands (league 10%). For the sim, that means a dangerous grappler with a below-average defensive shell.
