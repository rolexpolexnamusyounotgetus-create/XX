## Style

A wrestler first and last.

- **Takedowns:** she attempts 9.5 per 15 min (decayed), about 2.5 times the strawweight average of 3.8, and chains them against the fence.
- **Control:** 447 s per 15 min decayed and 536 career, against a division 185.
- **Strikes:** mostly a route to the clinch. 46.6% of her landed strikes go to the body, and 34.6% land in the clinch.
- **Finishes:** guillotines (two), rear-naked chokes (two), a D'Arce, and a ground-strike TKO of Carla Esparza.

## Strengths

- **Volume of takedown attempts:** relentless pressure for the clinch and the mat.
- **Top control:** long stretches of it in most fights.
- **Submissions:** 0.61 attempts per 15 min, against a division 0.40.
- **Durability:** no knockdowns absorbed in 10 UFC fights.

## Weaknesses

- **Takedown accuracy:** down to 33% decayed from 46% career.
- **Defence at range:** opponents land 61.5% on her (decayed), against a norm of 45%, and both of her last two opponents out-landed her.
- **No plan B:** against Zhang she had nothing once the takedown failed.
- **Inactivity:** no UFC fight from Jun 2019 to Feb 2023 (cause not verified in this pass).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never knocked down or stopped in the UFC.
- **Cardio:** less proven. In her one five-round fight she went 0 of 5 on takedowns and landed 4 significant strikes in rounds 4-5 combined. Her three-round pace (1.00, 1.02, 1.55) rests on few late minutes because many of her fights end in round 2.

## What the numbers say

Decayed Elo is 1703. The engine should give her very high takedown attempt rates with falling success, long control, a submission threat (guillotine and rear-naked choke) and very weak striking defence at range. If she can't get the fight down, she should lose clearly on the feet.
