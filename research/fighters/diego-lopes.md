# Diego Lopes — scouting dossier (as of Nov 6, 2026)

## Current status
- #2 featherweight on the UFCalendar and ufcrankings.org boards (Nov 3, 2026). Pro record 28-8 per ufcrankings.org (one results report says 29-8); UFC record in the dataset 7-3.
- Last fight: KO of Steve Garcia, R2 2:42, the opening bout of UFC Freedom 250 at the White House, Jun 14, 2026. This ended Garcia's seven-fight win streak.
- Next: moves up to lightweight to challenge Charles Oliveira for the BMF title in the UFC 335 main event, Dec 12, 2026, T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas. Oliveira won that belt from Max Holloway at UFC 326 in March.
- Injuries: none reported.

## Recent fights
- **W Steve Garcia, KO R2, Jun 2026.** Lopes started slowly and Garcia won the first round (47-24 significant strikes over the fight, 11 of 11 leg strikes). In R2 a counter left hook dropped Garcia and punches from top finished it.
- **L Alexander Volkanovski, UD (49-46, 49-46, 50-45), UFC 325, Jan 31, 2026.** Closer on the feet than the cards: 98-70 significant strikes, 77-67 through four rounds. Lopes landed 17 body and 20 leg strikes and took Volkanovski down twice. Volkanovski swept round 5 (21-3, two takedowns). Late in the fight Lopes tried a rear-naked choke and Volkanovski escaped.
- **W Jean Silva, TKO R2 4:48, Noche UFC, Sep 13, 2025.** Lopes took Silva down and mounted him in R1. Near the end of R2 a spinning back elbow dropped and cut Silva, and ground strikes finished it.
- **L Volkanovski, UD (49-46, 49-46, 48-47), UFC 314, Apr 12, 2025**, for the vacant title. He dropped Volkanovski with a jab and overhand right late in R2 but was out-landed 158-63, with rounds 4-5 lopsided.
- **W Brian Ortega, UD, UFC 306, Sep 2024** (106-63 significant strikes, 30-27 on two cards). Earlier UFC run: KOs of Sabatini and Yusuff, a triangle armbar on Tucker, and a decision over Ige.

## Style
An orthodox head-hunter (81% of attempts to the head) who trades willingly. His weapons are the jab-overhand right, the left hook (often as a counter), and spinning elbows. He is unusual for a striker in how often he finishes on the mat: 23% of his landed significant strikes are ground strikes, and he chains takedowns into mount against hurt strikers. Off his back he attacks with triangles and armbars (four submission attempts against Evloev on short notice in 2023).

**Strengths:** power in both hands; spinning elbow; top game and finishing on the ground; dangerous guard; 74% takedown defence (career).

**Weaknesses:** hittable (4.68 absorbed per minute, 47% defence); slow starts; the two elite-volume decisions against Volkanovski; leg kicks land on him (Volkanovski 23 of 36 in the rematch, Garcia 11 of 11). His lightweight debut is against a far bigger submission specialist.

**Durability:** never knocked down in 10 UFC fights; all three losses are decisions. **Cardio:** has gone 25 minutes twice and was still attacking late, but his landed rate fell in round 5 against Volkanovski both times.
