## Current status

- **Title:** UFC bantamweight champion. He won the belt from Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025; 49-46, 49-46, 48-47) and retained it at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi (Oct 24, 2026; 48-47 on all three cards).
- **Ranking:** #3 men's pound-for-pound in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, up one).
- **Record:** 21-5 pro (UFCalendar, Wikipedia, MMA Junkie); 13-4 UFC per ufcstats.
- **Age and frame:** 33 (born Feb 11, 1993), 5'7", 67" reach, switch stance. Nickname "No Mercy".
- **Next:** nothing booked on Nov 6. UFCalendar lists no next fight, and ufcrankings.org shows no upcoming bout for any ranked bantamweight. Dana White named Song Yadong and Sean O'Malley as contenders (Bloody Elbow, Oct 25). O'Malley says "me versus Petr is happening next" (MMA Fighting, Oct 28). Headlines in Sherdog and Boxing News had Yan favoured in an O'Malley rematch. Flyweight champion Joshua Van named Yan as a future target (Bloody Elbow, Oct 30).
- **Injuries:** nine-hour lumbar (lower-back) surgery on Jan 26, 2026, after UFC 323 (CBS Sports, Jan 28). He came back to win at UFC 333. No new injury reported since.

## Style

- **Distance boxing:** accurate straight punches, hooks and uppercuts from both stances; 83% of career attempts come at distance.
- **Counters:** he usually throws less than opponents but lands more.
- **Wrestling when needed:** he went 7 of 7 against Sterling in 2021 and 6 of 13 against O'Malley.
- **Best-known finishes:** a head kick (Faber, 2019) and ground-and-pound (Aldo, R5, 2020).

## Strengths

- **Accuracy:** 55% career sig accuracy, 57% decayed, against a 45.6% average.
- **Takedown defence:** 87.7% career and 89.4% decayed. Dvalishvili went 7 of 87 against him over 2025-26.
- **Defence:** opponents land just 32.9% of head attempts.

## Weaknesses

- **Slow starts:** quiet first rounds; at UFC 333 Merab likely banked R1-R2.
- **Close cards:** he is out-thrown (10.8 vs 9.4 attempts per minute career), and has two split-decision losses.
- **Fading power:** his knockdown rate has dropped to 0.14 per 15 min (decayed) from 0.49 career. He has not stopped anyone since 2020.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** he has been knocked down once in 17 UFC fights (John Dodson, 2019) and has never been stopped.
- **Cardio:** his output climbs through five-round fights: 1.00 / 1.29 / 1.58 / 2.03 / 2.42 over 8 fights, against a bantamweight prior of 1.00 / 1.32 / 1.32 / 1.37 / 1.53.

## What the numbers say

Decayed: lands 5.07 per minute, absorbs 4.64; attempt split 80.8% head, 10.8% body, 8.4% leg. Elo 1754. In the engine he should rarely be taken down, drop or split early rounds against high-output opponents, and pull ahead from round 3. His finishing threat should sit near the league prior rather than at his career knockdown rate.
