## Current status

- **Title:** UFC bantamweight champion. He won the belt from Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025; 49-46, 49-46, 48-47) and retained it at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi (Oct 24, 2026; 48-47 on all three cards).
- **Age and frame:** 33 (born Feb 11, 1993), 5'7", 67" reach, switch stance. Nickname "No Mercy".
- **Next:** nothing booked. Dana White named Song Yadong and Sean O'Malley as contenders (Bloody Elbow, Oct 25). Headlines in Sherdog and Boxing News had Yan favoured in an O'Malley rematch. Flyweight champion Joshua Van named Yan as a future target (Bloody Elbow, Oct 30).
- **Injuries:** none reported in the sources available.

## Style

Yan is a compact switch-stance boxer.

- **Distance boxing:** he works behind accurate straight punches, hooks and uppercuts from both stances. 83% of his career attempts come at distance.
- **Counters:** he usually throws less than his opponent and lands more.
- **Wrestling when needed:** he went 7 of 7 against Sterling in 2021 and 6 of 13 against O'Malley.
- **Finishes:** a head kick (Urijah Faber, 2019) and ground-and-pound (Jose Aldo, R5, 2020).

## Strengths

- **Accuracy:** 55% career sig accuracy, 57% decayed, against a 45.6% average.
- **Takedown defence:** 87.7% career and 89.4% decayed. Dvalishvili went 7 of 87 against him over 2025-26.
- **Defence:** opponents land only 32.9% of head attempts on him.

## Weaknesses

- **Slow starts:** his first rounds are quiet, and Merab banked early rounds twice.
- **Close cards:** being out-thrown (10.8 vs 9.4 attempts per minute career) leaves decisions close. Two split-decision losses show it.
- **Fading power:** his knockdown rate has dropped to 0.14 per 15 min (decayed) from 0.49 career. He has not stopped anyone since 2020.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** he has been knocked down once in 17 UFC fights (John Dodson, 2019) and has never been stopped.
- **Cardio:** among the best in the data. Five-round pace runs 1.00 / 1.29 / 1.58 / 2.03 / 2.42 over 8 fights, against a bantamweight prior of 1.00 / 1.32 / 1.32 / 1.37 / 1.53.

## What the numbers say

Decayed rates: he lands 5.07 per minute and absorbs 4.64, with opponents landing 39% against him. His decayed attempt split is 80.8% head, 10.8% body and 8.4% leg. His Elo is 1754, the highest of the five fighters in this batch. In the engine he should rarely be taken down, lose or split early rounds against high-output opponents, and pull ahead from round 3 in five-round fights. His finishing threat should sit near the league prior rather than at his career knockdown rate.
