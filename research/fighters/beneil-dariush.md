# Beneil Dariush: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Southpaw, 5'10", 72-inch reach, born 1989-05-06 in Urmia, Iran. Fights out of Kings MMA in Huntington Beach. UFC record 17-8-1 in fighters.json; 23-8-1 as a pro.*

## Current status
**Inactive and coaching; not formally retired.** After first-round knockout losses to **Benoit Saint Denis** (0:16, UFC 322, **Nov 15, 2025**) and **Quillan Salkilld** (3:29, UFC Perth, **May 2, 2026**), he has taken a head-coaching role. He is the chief corner for Uros Medic and also helps coach Kelvin Gastelum and Marvin Vettori. In early **November 2026** he said he has no plans to fight right now and is not interested in UFC offers unless something big comes up, though he left the door open for next year. He had said in February 2026 that his next fight could be his last. His coach Rafael Cordeiro has described repeated head trauma in his 2023 training camps. Re-checked on the web Nov 6, 2026: ESPN lists no upcoming bout, and he is still **#15 in the UFC's official media-panel rankings** (Nov 3, 2026; ufcrankings.org Nov 5) but **not in the Meta algorithmic top 15** (Oct 31). Before the Salkilld fight he had been booked against Manuel Torres at UFC 327 (Apr 11, 2026), but Torres withdrew injured. At UFC 322 he missed weight (157.2 lb) and forfeited 20% of his purse to Saint Denis (Wikipedia).

## Recent fights
- **L Salkilld, UFC Perth (May 2026), TKO in round 1.** He hurt Salkilld early with a jab and completed 2 of 5 takedowns, then was dropped by a right hook and stopped by ground strikes.
- **L Saint Denis, UFC 322 (Nov 2025), KO in 16 seconds.** His opening calf kick briefly put Saint Denis down (UFC Stats scored no knockdown), and Saint Denis answered with a left hand that ended it.
- **W Renato Moicano, UFC 317 (Jun 2025), unanimous decision (29-28 x3).** He returned from a 19-month layoff (Dec 2023 to Jun 2025), was knocked down, then went 5 for 5 on takedowns with 5:53 of control.
- **L Arman Tsarukyan (Dec 2023) and L Charles Oliveira (Jun 2023), both first-round KO/TKOs.**
- **W Mateusz Gamrot, UFC 280 (Oct 2022), unanimous decision.** He stopped 15 of Gamrot's 19 takedown attempts and outlanded him 59-33.

## Style
He is a southpaw Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt and was a world no-gi champion at the coloured belts. His game is clinch, bodylock takedown, back take and rear-naked choke: 5 of his submission wins are RNCs, and he also has an armbar (Dober), an arm triangle and a triangle-armbar. His standing game is a left straight and kicks, plus the occasional spinning back fist (KO of Holtzman, 2020). In his prime he could ride top position for whole fights (12:15 of control over Tony Ferguson).

## Strengths
- **Control:** 41-44% control time (median about 17%), and he is rarely controlled himself (about 9%).
- **Takedown defence:** 83% career.
- **Grappling credentials:** a long submission résumé and top-class technique.

## Weaknesses
- **Durability:** 7 KO/TKO losses in the UFC, including first-round stoppages in four of his last five fights.
- Even the Moicano win included a knockdown against him.
- He is 37 and now coaching full-time.

## Durability and cardio
His chin is the defining issue. He has absorbed 9 knockdowns in UFC bouts, and his recency-weighted rate is 1.59 per 15 minutes against a median of about 0.24. His cardio over three rounds is solid: he kept wrestling throughout the Moicano fight after being dropped. He has only one five-round fight (Tsarukyan, which ended in 64 seconds), so his five-round pace is unknown.

## What the numbers say
His Elo is 1669 (10th among recently active lightweights). The numbers describe a low-output (3.4-3.7 significant strikes per minute), high-control grappler with excellent takedown defence, whose results now depend on whether he survives the first exchanges. Any simulation of him should carry a well-below-average durability setting.
