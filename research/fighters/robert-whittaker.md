# Robert Whittaker ("The Reaper")

As of Nov 6, 2026. Orthodox fighter, age 35 (born Dec 20, 1990), 6'0" with a 73" reach. He is a middleweight by history (19 of 25 UFC fights) and fought his latest bout at light heavyweight. Dataset: `data/derived/fighters.json`, ufcstats snapshot of Oct 31, 2026.

**Research limits:** no web access in the research pass or the Nov 6 fact-check. Results come from the ufcstats snapshot. His ranking, division plans and next fight are **unverified**.

## Current status
- **Former UFC middleweight champion.** He won the interim title over Yoel Romero (UD, UFC 213, Jul 8, 2017) and beat Romero again by split decision (UFC 225, Jun 2018). He lost the title to Israel Adesanya (KO R2, UFC 243, Oct 5, 2019) and lost the 2022 rematch by UD.
- **Last fight:** a KO of Nikita Krylov at 1:01 of R3 at **light heavyweight**, UFC 329 (Jul 11, 2026). It was his first UFC fight at 205.
- Before that: L Reinier de Ridder (SD, Jul 26, 2025) and L Khamzat Chimaev (RNC R1 3:34, Oct 26, 2024).
- **UFC record 18-7** (7 KO/TKO, 0 SUB, 11 DEC). No booking found.

## Recent fights
- **W Krylov (KO R3, Jul 2026):** Krylov took him down 3 times in 5 attempts in R1 (2:10 of control) and attacked the legs and body (30 of his 51 landed strikes). Whittaker threw only to the head, landing 54 of 111, and ended it with a punch at distance early in R3.
- **L De Ridder (SD 47-48, 48-47, 47-48, Jul 2025):** He stopped 13 of 15 takedown attempts but still spent 9:06 under control, clinch included. He dropped De Ridder in R3 and landed 11 ground strikes, but the significant strikes were even at 66-67.
- **L Chimaev (sub R1, Oct 2024):** He was taken down twice in four attempts and submitted in 3:34, landing two strikes.
- **W Aliskerov (KO R1 1:49, Jun 2024)** with a punch at distance.
- **W Paulo Costa (UD, Feb 2024):** He out-landed Costa 95-67, including 28 of 28 leg strikes.
- **L Dricus Du Plessis (KO R2, Jul 2023):** He was dropped and out-landed 62-31.

## Style
A compact, explosive striker who works at range (90% of his landed strikes) and goes almost entirely to the head (88% of attempts). He has finished fights with punches (Krylov, Aliskerov), head kicks (Derek Brunson 2016, Jacare Souza 2017) and a clinch knee (Clint Hester 2014). His leg kicks come and go: he landed 28 against Costa and none against Krylov or De Ridder. He rarely goes to the body. His karate-based, in-and-out movement is widely described in pre-2026 coverage, not re-checked here.

**Strengths:** hand speed and power at range, kicks to the head, a long record of stopping takedowns (80% career), and experience in title fights.

**Weaknesses:** **chin and damage.** He has absorbed eight UFC knockdowns (three from Adesanya, two from Romero, one each from Du Plessis, Stephen Thompson and Colton Smith) and has three KO/TKO losses. Grapplers have caught up with him since 2024: opponents now shoot 5.2 times per 15 minutes and control him 18% of the time. He offers little to the body, and his pace stays flat rather than building.
