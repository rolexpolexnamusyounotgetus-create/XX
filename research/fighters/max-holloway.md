## Style

He is a volume boxer-kickboxer. He lands 6.9 significant strikes per minute career (league 4.3) on 14.4 attempts. The jab is the engine: analysts describe him doubling it, often to the body, to back opponents up and open combinations ([Face Punching](https://facepunching.substack.com/p/max-holloway-death-by-three-quarters), [Fight Primer](https://www.fightprimer.com/the-tactical-guide-to-volkanovski-vs-holloway-ii-this-time-its-on-yas-island)). His mix has moved lower: body share of attempts was 16% career and 22% over the last three years, and leg share went from 7% to 15%. 93% of his landed strikes come at distance. He almost never shoots (0.4 takedown attempts per 15 minutes).

**Strengths:** pace and cardio, body work, shot selection that improves as the fight goes on, and composure in wild exchanges.

**Weaknesses:** wrestling defense. It was 81% career but 56% over three years, and Oliveira took him down at will. He has a short reach for his height (69 inches at 5 ft 11 in). He takes leg kicks (1.03 per minute career, league 0.64); breakdowns note that calf kicks counter a jab-first fighter ([Medium](https://medium.com/@arabi-masri/max-holloway-an-all-time-great-d4789ba9fcf3)). He also stays in the pocket long enough to get countered, as Topuria showed.

## Durability and cardio

In 33 UFC fights he has absorbed 2 knockdowns, both in the last three years (Topuria, Poirier). His career chin rate is 0.13 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed. The 3-year rate is 0.89, close to the league's 0.95, which hints at decline. His cardio is a strength: across 19 five-round bouts his landed rate runs 1.33, 1.58, 1.83 and 1.39 times round 1 in rounds 2-5.

## What fighters.json says

His Elo of 1775 ranks second among lightweights in the file. UFC record 24-9: 12 KO/TKO wins and 7 decision losses. He has fought 19 five-round fights and 10 title fights. Decayed target split: 70% head, 19% body, 11% legs; of landed strikes, 28% go to the body. For the sim, he is a high-pace striker whose danger is late, and whose big risk is being held down.
