# Brian "T-City" Ortega — scouting dossier (as of Nov 6, 2026)

## Current status
- Two-time featherweight title challenger. Record 16-5 (1 NC); UFC 8-5 (1 NC) in the dataset. No current ranking found.
- Two straight losses: Diego Lopes (UD, UFC 306, Sep 14, 2024) and Aljamain Sterling (UD 50-45 x3, Shanghai, Aug 23, 2025).
- Withdrew from his planned lightweight debut against Renato Moicano at UFC 326 (Mar 7, 2026), announced in late January. He cited nagging injuries that needed rest and physical therapy but no surgery. One report says the rebooked Moicano fight also fell through when Ortega withdrew again a few days out. The dataset shows Moicano instead fought Tom Nolan on Oct 31, 2026.
- Next: Dan Hooker at lightweight, UFC Fight Night, ABHA Arena, Doha, Nov 21, 2026. Hooker is on a three-fight skid.

## Recent fights
- **L Aljamain Sterling, UD, Aug 2025.** Scheduled at 145, moved to a 153 lb catchweight after Ortega missed weight. Ortega later said he was unconscious for about 30 minutes during the cut and woke in the ER. In the fight he was out-landed 124-55 and gave up 3 of 6 takedowns and 26 of 26 leg strikes.
- **L Diego Lopes, UD (30-26, 30-27, 30-27), Sep 2024.** Out-landed 106-63; went 0 of 2 on takedowns.
- **W Yair Rodriguez, sub (arm-triangle) R3, Feb 2024, Mexico City.** He survived a knockdown, then 3 of 6 takedowns and 5:21 of control set up the finish from side control.
- **L Yair Rodriguez, TKO R1, Jul 2022**: a shoulder injury.
- **L Alexander Volkanovski, UD, UFC 266, Sep 2021**, for the title. He threatened with chokes in round 3 (two submission attempts there, one in round 4, per ufcstats) but was out-landed 214-88.
- **W Chan Sung Jung, UD 50-45 x3, Oct 2020**: two knockdowns, 127-62, the best striking performance of his career.

## Style
A switch-stance jiu-jitsu specialist. On the feet he leads with a long jab from either stance and throws almost everything at the head (86% of attempts). His career was built on surviving early and finishing late: five of his eight UFC wins ended in round 3, by guillotine (Moicano, standing vs Swanson), triangle (Brandao), arm-triangle (Rodriguez), a knee (Guida) or strikes (Tavares). He also stopped Frankie Edgar with strikes in the clinch in round 1 (2018).

**Strengths:** submission threat from any position, especially the guillotine; switch-stance jab; durability and composure under fire; pace that historically rose in later rounds.

**Weaknesses:** low accuracy (37%) and heavy damage taken (6.38 significant strikes absorbed per minute); recent fights show low output (55 landed in 25 minutes vs Sterling). He gets out-wrestled by strong grapplers (55% career takedown defence). Missed weight at 145, and has had injuries and long layoffs. The move to 155 is untested.

**Durability:** never knocked out by strikes in the UFC. The Holloway loss was a doctor stoppage and the 2022 loss an injury. Two knockdowns absorbed in 14 fights. **Cardio:** historically strong over five rounds (pace by round 1, 1.35, 1.49, 1.04, 1.54). The Sterling fight is the exception, explained by the weight cut.
