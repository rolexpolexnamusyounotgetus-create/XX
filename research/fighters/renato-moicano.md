# Renato Moicano: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats come from `data/derived/fighters.json` and the round stats in `data/raw/` (ufcstats snapshot through Oct 31, 2026). Real name Renato Alves Carneiro, born May 21, 1989 in Brasília. He trains at American Top Team (ESPN) and calls himself "Money". Pro record 21-8-1 (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org).

> Research note: this file was first written without web access. On Nov 6, 2026 the status, ranking, record, medical news and appearance were checked on the web (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org, MMA Mania, Sherdog, Superfight, Wikipedia via its API, ESPN studio photos). ufc.com returned 403 and Bloody Elbow returned 403 to scripts.

## Current status

- **Last fight:** TKO loss to Tom Nolan (elbows from half guard, R3 1:28) in the UFC Fight Night 292 / "UFC Vegas 123" main event, Las Vegas, **Oct 31, 2026**. Moicano controlled 4:07 of R1 and 3:02 of R2. He had Nolan's back with a body triangle in R2 when referee Dan Miragliotta stood the fight up, a call widely criticised (MMA Mania). Nolan then dropped him late in R2 and landed 30 ground strikes in R3, and out-landed him 69-9. Moicano said he will not appeal.
- **Medical:** taken to hospital that night for precautionary CT scans of the head and face (MMA Mania). No result or commission suspension had been published by Nov 6 that we found.
- **Career plans:** on Nov 2 he told Ariel Helwani he has three fights left on his UFC deal, will finish it and then stop fighting MMA, and wants to do influencer boxing. "I don't wanna be like Tony Ferguson" (MMA Mania, Sherdog). Before the fight he had talked about testing free agency.
- **Ranking:** **No. 12** at lightweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, per UFCalendar; ufcrankings.org, updated Nov 5, agrees). **No. 14** in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31 board, down six from No. 8; UFCalendar, MMA Mania). MMA Mania's "boots Moicano from the top 10" story is about the Meta board.
- **Next fight:** none booked (ESPN, UFCalendar on Nov 6). Media suggestions: Mauricio Ruffy (MMA Mania) or Esteban Ribovics (Superfight). A planned Brian Ortega fight at UFC 326 (Mar 2026) fell through when Ortega withdrew in January (ESPN). Age 37.

## Style

Moicano is a submission grappler who strikes behind a head-heavy punching mix: 85% of his decayed attempts go to the head (lightweight league 76%). He used to kick the legs more at featherweight (38 leg strikes on Kattar in 2018). At 155 the fight usually turns on a takedown. All seven of his UFC submissions are rear-naked chokes, and over the last three years 40% of his landed strikes came on the ground (league 10%).

**Strengths:** back takes and the RNC; top pressure and ground strikes (control 345 s per 15 min decayed, league 174); 0.69 submission attempts per 15 (league 0.40); accuracy of 54% decayed (league 48%).

**Weaknesses:** standing output is low (2.45 landed per minute over three years, league 4.32). His takedown defense is 47% decayed and 33% over three years. When opponents win the grappling he gets controlled (Dariush) or finished (Makhachev, Nolan).

## Durability and cardio

The chin is the concern. He has 4 KO/TKO losses (Aldo, Jung in 58 seconds, Fiziev, Nolan) and has absorbed 5 knockdowns, including the R2 drop by Turner. That is 1.30 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed (decayed), against a league 0.95. In three-round fights his output climbs (1.40 and 1.45 times round 1 in R2 and R3). Only one of his six five-round fights reached round 5, so his 25-minute cardio is unproven. Against Nolan the fight turned after the R2 knockdown, with him well ahead on control.

## What fighters.json says

UFC record 13-8: 2 KO, 7 submissions, 4 decisions; losses are 4 KO/TKO, 2 submissions and 2 decisions. Elo 1640, 14th among lightweights active since 2025, one point behind Nolan. He is 3-3 from UFC 300 on. Decayed target split is 85/9/6 head/body/legs. For the sim he is a dangerous scrambler and back-taker with a fragile chin and little standing volume.
