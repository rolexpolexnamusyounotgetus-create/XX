## Style

Moicano is a submission grappler who strikes behind a head-heavy punching mix: 85% of his decayed attempts go to the head (lightweight league 76%). He used to kick the legs more at featherweight (38 leg strikes on Kattar in 2018). At 155 the fight usually turns on a takedown. All seven of his UFC submissions are rear-naked chokes, and over the last three years 40% of his landed strikes came on the ground (league 10%).

**Strengths:** back takes and the RNC; top pressure and ground strikes (control 345 s per 15 min decayed, league 174); 0.69 submission attempts per 15 (league 0.40); accuracy of 54% decayed (league 48%).

**Weaknesses:** standing output is low (2.45 landed per minute over three years, league 4.32). His takedown defense is 47% decayed and 33% over three years. When opponents win the grappling he gets controlled (Dariush) or finished (Makhachev, Nolan).

## Durability and cardio

The chin is the concern. He has 4 KO/TKO losses (Aldo, Jung in 58 seconds, Fiziev, Nolan) and has absorbed 5 knockdowns, including the R2 drop by Turner. That is 1.30 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed (decayed), against a league 0.95. In three-round fights his output climbs (1.40 and 1.45 times round 1 in R2 and R3). Only one of his six five-round fights reached round 5, so his 25-minute cardio is unproven. Against Nolan the fight turned after the R2 knockdown, with him well ahead on control.
