# Rafael Fiziev: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Switch stance (UFC Stats, ESPN), 5'8", 71-inch reach, born 1993-03-05 (age 33) in Korday, Kazakhstan, and raised in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Nickname "Ataman". He fights for Azerbaijan, lives in Phuket and trains at Tiger Muay Thai (Wikipedia, ESPN). Pro record 14-5-0 (ESPN, UFCalendar); UFC record 8-5 in fighters.json. His last two wins (2025 and 2026) both came in Baku, Azerbaijan.*

> Research note: this file was first written without web access. On Nov 6, 2026 the status, ranking, record, injuries and appearance were checked on the web (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org, MMA Mania, Yahoo/Uncrowned, Wikipedia via its API, ESPN studio photos). ufc.com returned 403 to scripts, so the official rankings page was not read directly.

## Current status
**Active, coming off a win, no fight booked.** On **Jun 27, 2026** he headlined UFC Fight Night 280 in **Baku** and stopped Manuel Torres 15 seconds into round 2. A spinning wheel kick dropped Torres and punches finished it (Wikipedia; Yahoo/Uncrowned). UFC Stats logs ground punches. It earned a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus. Before that, Mauricio Ruffy stopped him at UFC 325 in Sydney (**Jan 31, 2026** US time; Feb 1 local). He is 2-3 in his last five.
- **Ranking:** **No. 11** at lightweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, per UFCalendar; ufcrankings.org, updated Nov 5, agrees). **No. 13** in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31 board, up one; UFCalendar, MMA Mania, Wikipedia).
- **Next fight:** none. ESPN and UFCalendar list no upcoming bout on Nov 6, and a news search found no booking since Baku.
- **Injuries:** he told The Ariel Helwani Show that a round-1 jab from Torres left him seeing "quadrupled" out of his left eye, and that he threw the finishing kick almost blind (Yahoo/Uncrowned, Jun 29). No follow-up medical report was found. Earlier, a knee injury pulled him out of an Oct 11, 2025 main event against Charles Oliveira (ESPN). The 2023 Gamrot stoppage was a knee/leg injury.

## Recent fights
- **W Torres, Baku (Jun 2026), TKO R2 0:15.** He landed 25 of 39 significant strikes, 10 of 14 to the legs, went 2 of 2 on takedowns and scored a knockdown, with a spinning wheel kick that set up the finishing punches. Performance of the Night.
- **L Ruffy, UFC 325 (Jan 2026), TKO R2 4:30.** He won the body and leg battle (15 body and 12 leg strikes landed) but was outlanded 45-9 to the head, dropped and finished on the ground.
- **W Ignacio Bahamondes, Baku (Jun 2025), 30-27 x3.** 62-50 in significant strikes, 25 of them to the body, and 4 of 4 takedowns.
- **L Justin Gaethje, UFC 313 (Mar 2025), 28-29 x3.** He landed 43 body strikes, but Gaethje won the head exchanges 56-20 and scored a knockdown.
- **L Mateusz Gamrot (Sep 2023), TKO R2 2:03 (knee/leg injury).** He had stopped 5 of Gamrot's 6 takedowns before his leg gave out. His next fight came 17 months later.
- **L Gaethje, UFC 286 (Mar 2023), majority decision.** 97 significant strikes landed, 52 to the body.
- **W Rafael Dos Anjos (Jul 2022), KO 18 seconds into R5.**

## Style
He is a switch-stance Muay Thai striker. His signature is body kicking: 39% of his landed significant strikes career (42% recency-weighted) go to the body, about double the lightweight median of 22%. He mixes in head kicks and a spinning wheel kick (Brad Riddell KO, 2021; it also dropped Torres in 2026), clinch knees (10% of his landed strikes come in the clinch) and dangerous punches (Moicano, Dos Anjos, Torres). He wrestles only by choice, at 10 of 14 on takedowns, and has never attempted a submission.

## Strengths
- **Body attack:** heavy, sustained body volume (52 and 43 body strikes against Gaethje).
- **Takedown defence:** 90% career (47 of 52 stopped). Opponents control him only about 9% of the time.
- **Variety and power:** kicks to all three levels, a spinning finish, and three knockdowns (Moicano, Dos Anjos, Torres).

## Weaknesses
- **Head defence in exchanges:** 64-68% of what opponents land goes to his head. Gaethje (twice) and Ruffy beat him there.
- **Overall defence:** 50% significant-strike defence and 4.8 absorbed per minute (medians about 54% and 4.1).
- **Little grappling offence:** no submission threat at all.

## Durability and cardio
He has absorbed 3 knockdowns in 149.9 minutes (0.30 per 15; median about 0.24). fighters.json lists 3 KO/TKO losses, but one was the Gamrot leg-injury stoppage, so two came from strikes (Mustafaev 2019, Ruffy 2026). He went four full rounds with Dos Anjos and finished him early in round 5. His five-round pace (1.00 / 1.32 / 1.13 / 0.80) rests mostly on that one fight.

## What the numbers say
Elo 1616, 18th among the 100 lightweights who have fought since May 2025. Career rates: 4.7 significant strikes landed per minute at 53% accuracy and 4.8 absorbed. Recency-weighted: 4.5 landed, 4.7 absorbed and 1.7 takedowns per 15 minutes at 82%. For the director, the target split (head 0.41 / body 0.42 / leg 0.18 landed, recency-weighted) is the key input. A Fiziev who rarely kicks the body would not look like Fiziev.
