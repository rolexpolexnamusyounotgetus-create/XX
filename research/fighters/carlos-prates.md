## Style breakdown

A Muay Thai-style striker who fights long. 89% of his landed strikes come at distance, and under 1% in the clinch. He switches stances, kicks the legs (13% of attempts vs 9.3% for welterweight), and finishes with whatever lands: four punch KOs, a knee to the body (Radtke), a spinning back elbow (Neal) and ground elbows (Della Maddalena). He scores 2.32 knockdowns per 15 minutes, seven times the division rate. He almost never shoots for takedowns himself (0.19 per 15).

## Strengths

- Stopping power from many weapons and angles: seven KOs from seven wins.
- Reach (78 in, 5 in more than his height) and accuracy: 54.1% decayed (division 47.7%).
- Takedown defense: 79.5% against 7.55 attempts per 15 min.
- Never knocked down in the UFC.

## Weaknesses

- Hittable: opponents land 52.3% of their significant strikes on him, 4.35 per minute.
- Leg kicks: 1.49 absorbed per minute (division 0.66).
- A busy, rangy striker beat him on points over five rounds (Machado Garry).
- He gives up some control time when wrestlers commit (Edwards 3:00 in R1, Garry 3:09).

## Durability and cardio

0 knockdowns absorbed in 77.4 UFC minutes and 182 head strikes absorbed. His only 25-minute fight was a decision loss, and his five-round pace data (three scheduled five-rounders: Magny ended in R1, Della Maddalena in R3) is too thin to read.
