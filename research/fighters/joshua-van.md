# Joshua Van: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 5 in, 65 in reach, born 2001-10-10 (age 25). Nickname "The Fearless". UFC record 11-1 (ufcstats). Trains at 4oz. Fight Club, Houston (EssentiallySports).

## Current status

- **Title:** UFC flyweight champion since UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025), the second-youngest UFC champion per CBS. Pantoja hurt his arm when Van caught a head kick (0:26, R1).
- **Defences:** TKO of Tatsuro Taira at 1:32 of round 5, UFC 328 (May 9, 2026). Unanimous decision over Pantoja at UFC 331 (Sep 19, 2026; 49-46, 48-47, 50-45 per ufcstats); Yahoo called it a Fight of the Year contender.
- **Next:** Manel Kape, UFC 336 main event, Scotiabank Arena, Toronto, Jan 30, 2027, his third defence. On Oct 30, 2026 he named Petr Yan as a possible future opponent.
- **Ranking:** Champion in the Nov 3, 2026 flyweight lists (ufcrankings.org, ufcalendar.com). Pound-for-pound position not verified.
- **Injuries:** RotoWire listed an undisclosed injury before the postponed Taira fight; unconfirmed elsewhere.

## Style breakdown

A pressure boxer with extreme output. The UFC described him walking Zhumagulov down with constant volume; RotoWire says he beat Royval to the punch. He studied Dustin Poirier's fights flipped to orthodox. AgentMMA notes heavy body work. Decayed attempts: 84% head, 11% body, 4% legs; 89% at distance.

## Strengths

- Output and accuracy: 8.0 significant strikes landed per minute (decayed) at 59%, against a flyweight prior of 3.7 at 46%.
- Power from volume: 8 knockdowns in 170.8 UFC minutes (0.70 per 15 min, prior 0.32).
- Took over after conceding early grappling in both five-round fights that went past round 1.
- Takedown defense: 75% career (23 of 92 landed against him).

## Weaknesses

- Gets hit: 5.7 significant strikes absorbed per minute (decayed), with 56% defense.
- Early control against elite grapplers: Taira and Pantoja each banked 9+ minutes of control.
- Knocked out once, by Charles Johnson in round 3 (Jul 13, 2024), reported as a right uppercut.

## Durability and cardio

One knockdown absorbed in 12 UFC fights (the Johnson KO). He has gone 25 minutes once and reached round 5 twice. In five-round fights his output from round 2 on runs 1.6 to 5.8 times round 1 (small sample; round 1 cut short or slowed by grappling).

## What the numbers say

He has the highest Elo in the flyweight snapshot (1719; Pantoja 1675). Seven of his 11 UFC wins are decisions, won by out-landing opponents rather than by control: his career control share is 8%, his opponents' 18%. Decayed and career rates are close.
