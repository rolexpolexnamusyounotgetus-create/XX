# Joshua Van: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 5 in, 65 in reach, born 2001-10-10 (age 25). Nickname "The Fearless". UFC record 11-1 (ufcstats). Trains at 4oz. Fight Club in Houston, with boxing work at Aztlan Boxing Gym.

## Current status

- **Title:** UFC flyweight champion since UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025), the second-youngest UFC champion per CBS. Van caught a Pantoja head kick and Pantoja hurt his left arm in the fall (0:26, R1).
- **Defences:** TKO of Tatsuro Taira at 1:32 of round 5, UFC 328 (May 9, 2026). Unanimous decision over Pantoja at UFC 331 (Sep 19, 2026; 49-46, 49-47, 50-45), which Yahoo called a Fight of the Year contender.
- **Next:** Manel Kape, UFC 336 main event, Scotiabank Arena, Toronto, Jan 30, 2027. It would be his third defence. On Oct 30, 2026 he named Petr Yan as a possible future opponent.
- **Ranking:** Champion in the Nov 3, 2026 flyweight lists (ufcrankings.org, ufcalendar.com). Pound-for-pound position not verified.
- **Injuries:** RotoWire listed an undisclosed injury before the Taira fight, which was pushed back; not confirmed elsewhere.

## Style breakdown

A pressure boxer with extreme output. The UFC described him walking Zhumagulov down with constant volume and pressure; RotoWire says he stood his ground and beat Royval to the punch. He has said he studied Dustin Poirier's fights flipped to orthodox. AgentMMA notes heavy body work. The ufcstats split agrees: 84% of his decayed significant attempts go to the head, 11% to the body and 4% to the legs, and almost all of them come at distance (89%).

## Strengths

- Output and accuracy together: 8.0 significant strikes landed per minute (decayed) at 59%, more than double the flyweight prior of 3.7 at 46%.
- Power from volume: 8 knockdowns in 170.8 UFC minutes (0.70 per 15 min, prior 0.32).
- Took over after losing round 1 in both five-round fights.
- Takedown defense: 75% career (23 of 92 landed against him).

## Weaknesses

- Gets hit: 5.7 significant strikes absorbed per minute (decayed), with 56% defense.
- Early control against elite grapplers: Taira and Pantoja each banked more than 9 minutes of control on him.
- Knocked out once, by Charles Johnson in round 3 (Jul 13, 2024), reported as a right uppercut.

## Durability and cardio

One knockdown absorbed in 12 UFC fights (the Johnson KO). He has gone 25 minutes once and reached round 5 twice. In five-round fights his output from round 2 on runs 1.6 to 5.8 times round 1, on a small sample where round 1 was cut short or slowed by grappling.

## What the numbers say

He has the highest Elo in the flyweight snapshot (1719, ahead of Pantoja's 1675). Seven of his 11 UFC wins are decisions, and he wins them by out-landing opponents rather than by control: his own control share is only 8%, against 18% for his opponents. Decayed and career rates are close, so the title run did not inflate his profile.
