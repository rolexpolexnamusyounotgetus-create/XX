## Style

Song is an orthodox boxer-puncher. UFC.com lists his style as "Boxer", and Sherdog calls boxing the cornerstone of his game.

- **Setups:** he works off low kicks and body shots. Right hands and long right hooks to the body discourage level changes.
- **Finishes:** he punishes people who come in. Six UFC KOs, all punches at distance except a clinch elbow in 2018.
- **Grappling:** his grappling is mainly defensive. A strong sprawl and frames lead to scrambles or the guillotine, which won him his first UFC fight (2017) and the Figueiredo bout.

## Strengths

- **Power:** 0.53 knockdowns per 15 min career, against a 0.31 bantamweight prior.
- **Takedown defence:** 73.5% career and 76% time-decayed, against a 65.7% league average.
- **Leg kicks:** 15.8% of his attempts go to the legs, against a 9.7% league average, and they land at 78%.

## Weaknesses

- **Volume:** decayed output is 3.76 sig landed per minute, below the 3.97 bantamweight average. Higher-volume strikers (O'Malley, Yan, Kyler Phillips) have beaten him on the cards.
- **Defence:** opponents landed 49% of their sig strikes on him (decayed), against a 45.6% league average.
- **Kick risk:** wrestlers can catch his kicks.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** he has never been knocked down in 18 UFC fights (dataset: 0 knockdowns absorbed). His only stoppage loss was a doctor stoppage for a cut against Cory Sandhagen in 2022.
- **Cardio:** he has been booked for five rounds six times. He KO'd Ricky Simon in R5 (2023) and won 50-44/45/45 over Chris Gutierrez. In those fights his output in rounds 2-5 ran 1.3-2.5x his round-1 pace, a small sample that hints at a slow start.

## What the numbers say

Song's game is efficient rather than busy. His striking is almost even (decayed 3.76 landed vs 3.48 absorbed per minute), and his leg-kick and knockdown rates are above average. His control is minimal (6.6 s/min), and his takedown defence is strong. His decayed Elo is 1711. In the engine he should rarely be taken down, win the low-kick exchange, and carry real KO equity. In decisions against volume strikers he should be roughly even or slightly behind.
