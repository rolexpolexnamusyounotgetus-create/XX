## Current status

- **Ranking:** #3 bantamweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, ufcalendar.com; ufcrankings.org agrees on Nov 5) and #2 in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31, 2026). The "#2 on ufcalendar.com" in the earlier draft was the Meta list.
- **Records:** pro 24-9-1 (1 NC) per UFCalendar and Wikipedia; UFC 13-4-1 in the dataset.
- **Age and frame:** 28 (born Dec 2, 1997), 5'8" with a 67" reach, orthodox. Nickname "Kung Fu Kid".
- **Title picture:** Petr Yan beat Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 333 (Oct 24, 2026) to keep the belt. On Oct 25 Dana White named Song and Sean O'Malley as the two contenders. No UFC bout is booked on Nov 6 (UFCalendar).
- **Next:** a freestyle wrestling match, not MMA: a rematch with Henry Cejudo for Cejudo's RAF lightweight crossover title at RAF 15, NOW Arena, Chicago, Nov 28, 2026. It was announced on Oct 21 as the main event and moved to co-main on Oct 27, when Arman Tsarukyan vs Anthony Pettis took the top slot (MMA Junkie, MMA Crossfire, MMA Mania).
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style

An orthodox boxer-puncher: UFC.com lists his style as "Boxer", and Sherdog calls boxing the cornerstone of his game.

- **Setups:** low kicks open his fights; 15.8% of his attempts go to the legs. His body share (12%) is below the division's 14%, though coverage of the Umar fight notes right hands to the body.
- **Finishes:** six UFC KOs, all punches at distance except a clinch elbow (Felipe Arantes, 2018).
- **Grappling:** mainly defensive. A strong sprawl leads to scrambles or the guillotine, which won his UFC debut (2017) and the Figueiredo bout.

## Strengths

- **Power:** 0.53 knockdowns per 15 min career, against a 0.31 bantamweight prior.
- **Takedown defence:** 73.5% career and 76% time-decayed, against a 65.7% average.
- **Leg kicks:** land at 78% career, at about 1.6 times the division's leg share.

## Weaknesses

- **Volume:** 3.76 sig landed per minute (decayed), below the 3.97 average. O'Malley out-landed him on fewer attempts and Yan out-landed him 99-86.
- **Defence:** opponents land 49% of their sig strikes on him (decayed), against 45.6%.
- **Grappling losses:** Kyler Phillips beat him in 2021 with 3 of 5 takedowns despite being out-landed 67-59. Wrestlers can catch his kicks.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never knocked down in 18 UFC fights. His only stoppage loss was a doctor stoppage for a cut against Cory Sandhagen (2022).
- **Cardio:** six five-round bookings, including a R5 KO of Ricky Simon (2023) and a 50-44, 50-45, 50-45 win over Chris Gutierrez. Rounds 2-5 ran 1.3-2.5 times his round-1 output, a small sample hinting at a slow start.

## What the numbers say

Song is efficient rather than busy: 3.76 landed vs 3.48 absorbed per minute (decayed), minimal control (6.6 s/min). Elo 1711. In the engine he should rarely be taken down, win the low-kick exchange and carry real KO equity, while running roughly even in decisions against sharp volume strikers.
