# Tatsuro Taira: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 7 in, 70 in reach, born 2000-01-27 (age 26). Nickname "The Best". Japanese, from Naha, Okinawa. Pro record 18-2 (6 KO/TKO, 8 submissions, 4 decisions; losses 1 TKO, 1 decision; Sherdog, ESPN and Wikipedia agree), UFC 8-2 (ufcstats). Trains at The Blackbelt Japan.

## Current status

- **Title:** None. He lost to champion Joshua Van by TKO at 1:32 of round 5, UFC 328 (May 9, 2026); Sherdog and Wikipedia list the method as a front kick to the body and punches. The fight was first set for UFC 327 (Apr 11) and moved after Van hurt his knee. Both men got Fight of the Night.
- **Next:** no booking as of Nov 6, 2026 (Sherdog, UFC Calendar; "TBD" in both Wikipedia rankings tables). Charles Johnson called him out earlier, but Johnson is now booked against Brandon Moreno at UFC 335.
- **Ranking:** #4 flyweight in both the official UFC rankings (media panel, released Nov 3, 2026; ufc.com via a Wayback capture of Nov 4) and the Elo-based Meta UFC rankings released Oct 31, 2026.
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style breakdown

A grappler who keeps attacking takedowns: 3.57 landed per 15 minutes career (4.15 decayed) at 44%, against a flyweight prior of 1.65. On the floor he controls, takes the back and finishes: an armbar from back control (Vergara), a triangle-armbar from bottom guard (Aguilar), a neck crank from the back (Park), and ground-strike TKOs of Hernandez and Moreno. Standing, he is low-volume; 23% of his decayed strikes (30% career) are thrown on the ground.

## Strengths

- Control: 54% of his UFC fight time is spent in control, against about 19% for the division.
- Submission threat from top and bottom: 1.28 attempts per 15 minutes, with 3 submission wins.
- Persistence: 21 takedown attempts vs Van, 15 vs Royval.

## Weaknesses

- Striking defense: 44% career and 42% decayed, against a division prior near 54%. He absorbs 3.1 to 3.8 significant strikes per minute and lands only 2.7 to 2.9.
- Strikers who stay up beat him: both losses came to men who out-landed him three to one.
- Takedown defense: 46% (7 of 13 attempts against him landed).

## Durability and cardio

He has been knocked down three times in the UFC (Chairez once, Van twice), and his only stoppage loss is the Van TKO. He has twice reached the late rounds of a five-round fight and lost both times (Royval decision, Van round-5 TKO). His output in rounds 2 to 4 of five-round fights runs 0.73 to 0.86 of round 1, consistent with grappling-heavy middle rounds rather than clear fatigue.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1652, 3rd among active flyweights in the snapshot, behind Van and Pantoja. Grappling dominates: 53% control (decayed) against 7% for opponents. His striking differential is negative: he lands 2.72 significant strikes per minute and absorbs 3.76 (decayed). He has 3 KO/TKO wins (one is Perez's injury) and 3 submissions in 8 UFC wins. At 26, he is the youngest top-ten flyweight by Elo after Van.
