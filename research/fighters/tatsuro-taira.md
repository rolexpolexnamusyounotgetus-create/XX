## Style breakdown

A grappler who keeps attacking takedowns: 3.57 per 15 minutes career (4.15 decayed) at 44%, against a flyweight prior of 1.65. Once the fight is on the floor he controls, takes the back and finishes. His UFC submissions are an armbar from back control (Vergara), a triangle-armbar from bottom guard (Aguilar) and a neck crank from the back (Park), and his ground-strike TKOs came against Hernandez and Moreno. Standing, he is low-volume, and about a quarter of his strikes are thrown on the ground (23% decayed, 30% career).

## Strengths

- Control: 54% of his UFC fight time is spent in control, against about 19% for the division.
- Submission threat from top and bottom: 1.28 attempts per 15 minutes, with 3 submission wins.
- Persistence: he kept shooting against Van (21 attempts) and Royval (15).

## Weaknesses

- Striking defense: 44% career and 42% decayed, against a division prior near 54%. He absorbs 3.1 to 3.8 significant strikes per minute and lands only 2.7 to 2.9.
- Strikers who stay up beat him: both losses came against men who out-landed him three to one.
- Takedown defense: 46% (7 of 13 attempts against him landed).

## Durability and cardio

He has been knocked down three times in the UFC (Chairez once, Van twice), and his only stoppage loss is the Van TKO. He has twice reached the late rounds of a five-round fight and lost both times: Royval took the decision, and Van finished him in round 5. In his five-round fights, his output in rounds 2 to 4 runs 0.73 to 0.86 of round 1. That is consistent with grappling-heavy middle rounds rather than clear fatigue.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1652, 3rd among active flyweights in the snapshot, behind Van and Pantoja. Grappling dominates his rates: 53% control (decayed) against 7% for his opponents.
