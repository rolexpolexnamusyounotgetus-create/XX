## Style breakdown

A grappler who keeps attacking takedowns: 3.57 landed per 15 minutes career (4.15 decayed) at 44%, against a flyweight prior of 1.65. On the floor he controls, takes the back and finishes: an armbar from back control (Vergara), a triangle-armbar from bottom guard (Aguilar), a neck crank from the back (Park), and ground-strike TKOs of Hernandez and Moreno. Standing, he is low-volume; 23% of his decayed strikes (30% career) are thrown on the ground.

## Strengths

- Control: 54% of his UFC fight time is spent in control, against about 19% for the division.
- Submission threat from top and bottom: 1.28 attempts per 15 minutes, with 3 submission wins.
- Persistence: 21 takedown attempts vs Van, 15 vs Royval.

## Weaknesses

- Striking defense: 44% career and 42% decayed, against a division prior near 54%. He absorbs 3.1 to 3.8 significant strikes per minute and lands only 2.7 to 2.9.
- Strikers who stay up beat him: both losses came to men who out-landed him three to one.
- Takedown defense: 46% (7 of 13 attempts against him landed).

## Durability and cardio

He has been knocked down three times in the UFC (Chairez once, Van twice), and his only stoppage loss is the Van TKO. He has twice reached the late rounds of a five-round fight and lost both times (Royval decision, Van round-5 TKO). His output in rounds 2 to 4 of five-round fights runs 0.73 to 0.86 of round 1, consistent with grappling-heavy middle rounds rather than clear fatigue.
