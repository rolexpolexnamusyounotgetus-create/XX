## Style
A tall, rangy orthodox kicker who works from long range. He kicks to all three levels and uses sudden, unorthodox weapons: a switch head kick knocked out Andre Fili (2016), and an elbow at the final second of round 5 knocked out Chan Sung Jung (2018). He is a real threat off his back (the Emmett triangle) and finishes from top when an opponent is hurt.

**Strengths:** range and kick variety; accurate body and leg kicks; output that rises late in long fights; submission threat from guard.

**Weaknesses:** wrestlers. Takedown defence is 59% for his career, and opponents who held top position (Volkanovski, Ortega) beat him. Long layoffs and pull-outs. Out-worked by elite volume (Holloway).

**Durability:** ufcstats records no knockdowns against him in 16 UFC fights. His two KO/TKO losses were ground strikes (Volkanovski) and a doctor stoppage for an eye injury (Edgar, 2017). **Cardio:** has gone the full 25 minutes twice (Caceres 2016, Holloway 2021) and knocked out Jung at 4:59 of round 5; in five-round fights his round-5 output is 1.19 times his round-1 output, though rounds 4-5 rest on those three fights only. By December he will have been out about 20 months, so rust is a real unknown.
