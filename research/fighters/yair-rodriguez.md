# Yair "El Pantera" Rodriguez — scouting dossier (as of Nov 6, 2026)

## Current status
- Former UFC interim featherweight champion. Won the interim belt by submitting Josh Emmett (UFC 284, Feb 11, 2023); lost the unification fight to Alexander Volkanovski (UFC 290, Jul 8, 2023, R3 TKO).
- Inactive since Apr 12, 2025 (UD over Patricio "Pitbull" Freire, UFC 314). He was booked to headline Noche UFC against Jean Silva on Sep 12, 2026 but withdrew with an undisclosed injury (news Aug 19-20, 2026). He wrote on Instagram that he tried to keep training but competing in that condition would not have been responsible (ESPN). He has not named the body part.

As of Nov 6 it is not on the ESPN, Sherdog or UFCalendar UFC 335 cards and no UFC release was found, so treat it as likely but unconfirmed. Garcia is No. 10 on the media panel (MMA Mania's "No. 14" matches the Meta board) and is coming off a KO loss to Diego Lopes.

Pro record 20-5 (1 NC) per Sherdog, Wikipedia and UFCalendar; ESPN and MMA Mania give 21-5 and ufcrankings.org 21-6, which seem to count extra bouts. UFC record in the dataset: 11-4 (1 NC).

## Recent fights
- **W Pitbull Freire, UD 30-27 x3, Apr 2025.** A one-sided return: 70-17 significant strikes, one knockdown, 25 ground strikes. He hit Freire's body (18 of 23) and legs (14 of 17).
- **L Brian Ortega, sub (arm-triangle) R3, Feb 2024, Mexico City.** Yair scored a knockdown, but Ortega landed 3 of 6 takedowns, held 5:21 of control and finished him from side control.
- **L Volkanovski, TKO R3, UFC 290.** Volkanovski landed 7 of 12 takedowns with 7:13 of control and finished with ground strikes. Yair still landed 16 of 17 leg strikes.
- **W Emmett, sub (triangle from bottom) R2, UFC 284**, for the interim title, after a strong striking start (62-22).
- **W Ortega, TKO R1 (Ortega's shoulder injury), Jul 2022.** **L Max Holloway, UD, Nov 2021**: out-landed 230-159 but 57 of 64 leg strikes.

## Style
A tall, rangy orthodox kicker who works from long range. He kicks to all three levels and uses sudden, unorthodox weapons: a switch head kick knocked out Andre Fili (2016), and an elbow at the final second of round 5 knocked out Chan Sung Jung (2018). He is a real threat off his back (the Emmett triangle) and finishes from top when an opponent is hurt.

**Strengths:** range and kick variety; accurate body and leg kicks; output that rises late in long fights; submission threat from guard.

**Weaknesses:** wrestlers. Takedown defence is 59% for his career, and opponents who held top position (Volkanovski, Ortega) beat him. Long layoffs and pull-outs. Out-worked by elite volume (Holloway).

**Durability:** ufcstats records no knockdowns against him in 16 UFC fights. His two KO/TKO losses were ground strikes (Volkanovski) and a doctor stoppage for an eye injury (Edgar, 2017). **Cardio:** has gone the full 25 minutes twice (Caceres 2016, Holloway 2021) and knocked out Jung at 4:59 of round 5; in five-round fights his round-5 output is 1.19 times his round-1 output, though rounds 4-5 rest on those three fights only. By December he will have been out about 20 months, so rust is a real unknown.
