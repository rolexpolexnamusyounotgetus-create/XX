# Nassourdine Imavov ("The Sniper")

As of Nov 6, 2026. Orthodox middleweight, age 30 (born Mar 1, 1996), 6'3" with a 75" reach. Dataset: `data/derived/fighters.json`, snapshot of Oct 31, 2026. Web claims come from search-result summaries, because direct page fetches were blocked in this session.

## Current status
- **Ranked #3** at middleweight. His pro record is 17-4 (1 NC) and he is **5-0 (1 NC)** since losing to Sean Strickland in Jan 2023.
- **Last fight:** Sep 6, 2025, a unanimous decision over Caio Borralho at UFC Paris (50-45, 49-46, 49-46). He has been out about 14 months, with no injury reported.
- **Why he hasn't fought:** He wanted to face Dricus du Plessis to stay active, but says the UFC told him to wait for the belt. Strickland then took the title from Khamzat Chimaev by split decision at UFC 328 (May 9, 2026).
- **Next:** A Strickland rematch for the title has been reported. A September report put it on UFC 335 (Dec 12, 2026), but that fell through over Strickland's pay dispute. MMA Junkie (Oct 27) now says it is targeted for **February 2027 in Florida**. It is not officially announced.

## Recent fights
- **W Borralho (UD, 5 rds, Sep 2025):** Imavov led on range and won R1-R2 (10-6 and 13-7 in sig strikes). He then took over R4 (24-12) and outlanded Borralho 81-66 overall. Borralho went 0 for 5 on takedowns.
- **W Adesanya (KO R2 0:30, Feb 2025):** He lost R1 on all cards to leg and body kicks (sig 8-20), then dropped Adesanya with a straight right and finished him.
- **W Brendan Allen (UD 29-28 x3, Sep 2024):** Allen slammed him once early, then went 1 for 10 on takedowns. Imavov had the bigger moments on the feet (57-30 sig).
- **W Jared Cannonier (TKO R4, Jun 2024)** with punches at distance. **W Roman Dolidze (MD, Feb 2024).**
- **L Strickland (UD, Jan 2023):** a short-notice bout at 205 lb. Strickland's pressure took over in R4, and Imavov's cardio was cited.

## Style
A **footwork-first boxer**. His feet stay parallel, and he springs in behind a fast jab rather than managing distance with kicks. The right hand is his finisher. In close he uses a short elbow, and against wrestlers he wins head position and breaks off with an elbow. He has some wrestling but rarely holds top position and has no UFC submission wins.

**Strengths:** range control, the jab, clean straight punching (55% sig accuracy against a 48% middleweight average), takedown defense and late-round output.
**Weaknesses:** He leans forward to deter takedowns, which has exposed him to knees (Sherdog). He offers little top game. He can lose early rounds to a kicker, as he did in R1 against Adesanya. He is coming off a long layoff.

## Durability and cardio
In 12 UFC fights he has **never been knocked down or stopped**. His cardio was questioned against Strickland in 2023. Since then, his five-round pace rises late: R4 output is 1.18x his R1 output and R5 is 1.21x.
