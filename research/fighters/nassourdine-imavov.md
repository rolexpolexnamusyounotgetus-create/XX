# Nassourdine Imavov ("The Sniper")

As of Nov 6, 2026. Orthodox middleweight, age 31 (born Mar 1, 1995 per Sherdog and Wikipedia; ESPN lists 1996), 6'3" with a 75" reach. Trains at Venum Training Camp, Paris (Sherdog). ## Current status
- **Ranked #3** at middleweight on the UFC media rankings updated Nov 3, 2026 (CBS Sports; ufcalendar.com mirror). He is also #3 on the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31, 2026).
- **Record:** 17-4 (1 NC) pro (Sherdog, Wikipedia, ESPN). He is **5-0 (1 NC)** since losing to Sean Strickland in Jan 2023.
- **Last fight:** Sep 6, 2025, a unanimous decision over Caio Borralho at UFC Paris (50-45, 49-46, 49-46). He has been out about 14 months. **No injury reported.**
- **Why he hasn't fought:** He wanted to face Dricus du Plessis to stay active, but says the UFC told him to wait for the belt. On Oct 5 he offered $500k of his own money to make the fight (Sherdog), and on Oct 7 he confronted Strickland at the UFC Performance Institute.
- **Next (reported, not official):** MMA Junkie's Mike Bohn reported on Oct 27-28, 2026 that **Strickland vs Imavov 2 is targeted for February 2027 "somewhere in Florida"** and is close to signed. Last Word on Sports names UFC 337. As of Nov 3 (Sportsnaut) it was **still not announced**, and it is not on the ESPN or Sherdog schedules.

## Recent fights
- **W Borralho (UD, 5 rds, Sep 2025):** Imavov led on range and won R1-R2 (10-6 and 13-7 in sig strikes). He then took over R4 (24-12) and outlanded Borralho 81-66 overall. Borralho went 0 for 5 on takedowns.
- **W Adesanya (KO R2 0:30, Feb 2025):** He lost R1 on all cards to leg and body kicks (sig 8-20), then dropped Adesanya with a straight right and finished him.
- **W Brendan Allen (UD 29-28 x3, Sep 2024):** Allen slammed him once early, then went 1 for 10 on takedowns. Imavov had the bigger moments on the feet (57-30 sig).
- **W Jared Cannonier (TKO R4, Jun 2024)** with punches at distance. **W Roman Dolidze (MD, Feb 2024)**, with a point deducted in R4 for an illegal soccer kick.
- **L Strickland (UD, Jan 2023):** a short-notice bout at 205 lb. Strickland's pressure took over in R4, and Imavov's cardio was cited.

## Style
A **footwork-first boxer**. He uses a side-on, bouncy stance and springs in behind a fast jab, lead hand extended, rather than managing distance with kicks. Feints set up one-twos and hook-crosses, and the right hand is his finisher (Wikipedia, citing Sherdog's UFC Paris preview). In close he lands hard knees, and against wrestlers he wins head position and breaks off with an elbow (Wikipedia, citing MMA Mania's 2023 breakdown). He has some wrestling, but his control time is below the division norm (14% vs about 18%), and he has no UFC submission wins.

**Strengths:** range control, the jab, clean straight punching (55% sig accuracy against a 48% middleweight average), takedown defense and late-round output.
**Weaknesses:** He leans forward to deter takedowns, which has exposed him to knees (Sherdog). He offers little top game. He can lose early rounds to a kicker, as he did in R1 against Adesanya. He will be about 17 months out of the cage if the February date holds.

## Durability and cardio
In 12 UFC fights he has **never been knocked down or stopped**. His cardio was questioned against Strickland in 2023. Since then, his five-round pace rises late: R4 output is 1.18x his R1 output and R5 is 1.21x.
