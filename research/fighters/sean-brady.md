# Sean Brady: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 10 in, 72 in reach, born 1992-11-23 (age 33). Pro record 19-2; UFC record 9-2 (ufcstats). Welterweight.

**Research notes.** The first research pass and its fact-check (2026-11-06) were done offline from the ufcstats snapshot (through 2026-10-31); the fact-check re-derived every date, method, score and per-fight number from data/raw, and all match. A later web pass the same day re-checked status, ranking, bookings, injuries and appearance against the sources listed below. Fight data and the style breakdown still come from ufcstats.

## Current status

- **Last fight:** unanimous decision over Joaquin Buckley at UFC 328 in Newark (May 9, 2026; 30-25, 30-25, 30-27). He landed 4 of 10 takedowns and controlled 12:09 of the 15 minutes. He out-landed Buckley 77-5 in significant strikes, 71 of them on the ground.
- **Before that:** stopped by Michael Morales in round 1 at UFC 322 (Nov 15, 2025).
- **Withdrawn:** he was booked to headline UFC Vegas 124 (UFC Fight Night 293, Meta APEX, Nov 7, 2026) against Gabriel Bonfim, but pulled out on Oct 29 with a staph infection in his left knee. He said the knee swelled after a bike session, he could not walk that night, and he spent three days in hospital on IV antibiotics. Bonfim was taken off the card too.
- **Next:** nothing booked as of Nov 6, 2026. Brady said the UFC told him its Nov 21, Dec 5 and Dec 12 events are full, and he is aiming for the Jan 16, 2027 Fight Night in Dallas, against Bonfim or a new opponent.
- **Ranking:** #6 welterweight in the UFC media rankings (Nov 3, 2026); #5 in the Meta UFC rankings (Oct 31, 2026).
- **Injuries:** the left-knee staph infection above (Oct 2026).

## Style breakdown

A submission grappler who wrestles to get there. He tries 7.96 takedowns per 15 minutes on the decayed sums (welterweight 3.75) and lands 48.5%. Once on top he stays there (501 s control per 15 vs 167) and either grinds with strikes or finishes. His four UFC submissions are two guillotines from mount, an arm-triangle and a kimura. Standing, 87% of his attempts go to the head. His most accurate weapon is the low kick (127 of 145 career attempts landed), though he barely threw it in his last two fights. He almost never targets the body.

## Strengths

- Control and finishing on the mat: 4 submissions in 9 wins, 0.82 submission attempts per 15 min career.
- Takedown defense: 84.5% decayed (division 64.1%). He is the one who decides where the fight goes.
- Cumulative damage control: 2.07 significant strikes absorbed per minute decayed (division 4.03).
- He has won two five-round fights (Burns 50-45 on two cards; Edwards stopped in R4).

## Weaknesses

- When the takedown fails, he loses badly. He was 0/5 against Muhammad and 0/1 against Morales, and was stopped by punches at distance both times.
- No UFC knockouts. His power ratio (0.05 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed) is far below the division's 0.84.
- Little body work (4.1% of attempts vs 13.9%).

## Durability and cardio

2 KO/TKO losses and 1 knockdown absorbed in 146.8 UFC minutes. His three-year chin ratio (1.47 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed) is worse than the division's (0.84), but it rests on one event. Against Burns, his round-5 output (6.2 landed per minute) was his second-best round of the fight, after round 2 (6.6), but that is a single 25-minute sample.

## What the numbers in fighters.json say

Elo 1716, fifth among welterweights who have fought since November 2024, behind Makhachev, Rakhmonov, Morales and Machado Garry. Decayed: 4.12 significant landed and 2.07 absorbed per minute, 51.3% accuracy. 40% of his landed strikes come on the ground (division 8.9%). Target split of landed strikes: 80% head, 6% body, 14% legs. The model should treat him as a takedown-first fighter whose stand-up risk rises sharply against strong takedown defenders with power.
