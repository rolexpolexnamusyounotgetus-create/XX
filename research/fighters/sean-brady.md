## Style breakdown

A submission grappler who wrestles to get there. He tries 7.96 takedowns per 15 minutes on the decayed sums (welterweight 3.75) and lands 48.5%. Once on top he stays there (501 s control per 15 vs 167) and either grinds with strikes or finishes. His four UFC submissions are two guillotines from mount, an arm-triangle and a kimura. Standing, 87% of his attempts go to the head. His most accurate weapon is the low kick (127 of 145 career attempts landed), though he barely threw it in his last two fights. He almost never targets the body.

## Strengths

- Control and finishing on the mat: 4 submissions in 9 wins, 0.82 submission attempts per 15 min career.
- Takedown defense: 84.5% decayed (division 64.1%). He is the one who decides where the fight goes.
- Cumulative damage control: 2.07 significant strikes absorbed per minute decayed (division 4.03).
- He has won two five-round fights (Burns 50-45 on two cards; Edwards stopped in R4).

## Weaknesses

- When the takedown fails, he loses badly. He was 0/5 against Muhammad and 0/1 against Morales, and was stopped by punches at distance both times.
- No UFC knockouts. His power ratio (0.05 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed) is far below the division's 0.84.
- Little body work (4.1% of attempts vs 13.9%).

## Durability and cardio

2 KO/TKO losses and 1 knockdown absorbed in 146.8 UFC minutes. His three-year chin ratio (1.47 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed) is worse than the division's (0.84), but it rests on one event. Against Burns, his round-5 output (6.2 landed per minute) was his second-best round of the fight, after round 2 (6.6), but that is a single 25-minute sample.
