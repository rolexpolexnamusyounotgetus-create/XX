## Current status

- **Title:** former UFC bantamweight champion. He won the belt from Sean O'Malley (UFC 306, Sep 14, 2024) and defended it three times in 2025, against Umar Nurmagomedov, O'Malley and Cory Sandhagen.
- **Ranking:** still #1 at bantamweight in both the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026) and the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31, 2026), and #9 men's pound-for-pound (media panel, Nov 3).
- **Form:** two straight losses to Petr Yan. UFC 323 (Dec 6, 2025): 46-49, 46-49, 47-48. UFC 333, Abu Dhabi (Oct 24, 2026): 47-48 on all cards.
- **Record:** 21-6 pro (UFCalendar, Wikipedia, MMA Junkie Nov 2); 14-4 UFC per ufcstats.
- **Age and frame:** 35 (born Jan 10, 1991), 5'6", 68" reach, orthodox. Nickname "The Machine".
- **Next:** no MMA bout booked. On Oct 26 he wrote that he is healthy and motivated and will be back soon, with no date or opponent (MiddleEasy). His next outing is a freestyle wrestling match, not MMA: Marlon Moraes at RAF Abu Dhabi, Nov 14, 2026, Etihad Arena (announced Nov 4; MMA Fighting, mmasucka, Yahoo). It is his fourth Real American Freestyle match: he beat Frankie Edgar (May 30) and Henry Cejudo (Jul 11), then lost the Cejudo rematch for the inaugural lightweight crossover title (Aug 22, 2026; Wikipedia, MMA Fighting).
- **Injuries:** a knee in sparring broke his nose in two places in April 2026. He chose not to have it repaired and fought UFC 333 with it (coach Ray Longo via mmasucka, Oct 22).

## Style

Merab is a wrestle-boxer built on volume.

- **Punch-to-shot rhythm:** he throws punches in bunches (10.9 sig attempts per minute career, against an 8.7 average) and level-changes again and again. He has attempted 396 takedowns in 18 UFC fights.
- **Re-shooting:** he does not hold position for long. He takes the opponent down, lets them up and shoots again.
- **Takedown record:** his 124 takedowns are the most in the ufcstats data. Georges St-Pierre is second with 90.

## Strengths

- **Output and late pace:** five-round pace runs 1.00 / 1.47 / 1.15 / 1.83 / 1.71.
- **Takedown defence:** 76.5% career.
- **Durability:** one knockdown absorbed in 18 UFC fights, by Marlon Moraes in 2021. He came back to stop Moraes in round 2.

## Weaknesses

- **Takedown accuracy:** 31% career, 24% decayed, and 7 of 87 against Yan in their last two fights.
- **Power:** one knockdown in 324 minutes. 12 of his 14 UFC wins are decisions.
- **Striking defence:** opponents land 51% of sig attempts on him (decayed), against a 45.6% average. Yan out-landed him at a far higher accuracy in both 2025-26 fights.
- **Age:** he is 35.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** he has never been stopped by strikes. His only stoppage loss is a 2018 technical guillotine against Ricky Simon at the end of round 3, in his second UFC fight.
- **Cardio:** elite. He has 7 five-round fights and his output rises late in them.

## What the numbers say

Decayed rates: he lands 4.68 sig strikes per minute and absorbs 3.66. He attempts 20.6 takedowns and lands 4.95 per 15 min, and holds 15.9 s/min of control. His decayed attempt split is 82.6% head, 9.1% body and 8.3% leg. His Elo is 1735. The engine should give him extreme takedown volume with modest and declining accuracy. Against wrestlers and strikers with average takedown defence, that volume plus his late pace should win decisions. Against an opponent with 85%+ takedown defence and accurate counters (Yan), the data favour the opponent. His finishing chance should stay low.
