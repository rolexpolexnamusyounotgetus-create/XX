## Style
He is a heavyweight wrestle-boxer with a Division I wrestling background. At range he works a jab and straight right (84% of his significant attempts go to the head). He counters going backwards: right hands finished Werdum for the title and Junior dos Santos. When needed he switches to the body or shoots. His career rate of 1.74 takedowns per 15 minutes is well above the heavyweight median of 0.68. He controls opponents for 26.5% of fight time and lands ground strikes from top. He has no submission game (0 attempts).
