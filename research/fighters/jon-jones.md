# Jon Jones: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Bones". Orthodox (switches stance), 6 ft 4 in, 84 in reach, born 1987-07-19 (age 39). UFC record 22-1, 1 NC (ufcstats). Pro record 28-1 (1 NC) (ESPN, Wikipedia). His only loss is a 2009 disqualification.

**Re-check, Nov 6, 2026:** status, ranking, record, injuries and appearance were re-verified on the open web (sources at the end). Rankings come from ufcalendar.com's mirror because ufc.com returned 403.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He won the vacant heavyweight title at UFC 285 (Mar 4, 2023) and defended it once, at UFC 309 (Nov 16, 2024).
- **Retirement:** Dana White announced Jones's retirement on Jun 21, 2025, at the UFC Baku post-fight press conference, and Tom Aspinall was promoted to undisputed champion (MMA Mania; Wikipedia).
- **Since then:** he said he re-entered the drug-testing pool in July 2025 and campaigned for the White House card, but was left off UFC Freedom 250 (Jun 14, 2026). On Mar 9, 2026 he publicly asked the UFC to release him: "If the UFC truly feels like I'm done, then I respectfully ask to be released from my contract today" (Yahoo). No release has been reported; in October Jake Paul publicly asked the UFC to free him for a Francis Ngannou fight. On Oct 9, 2026 he wrote: "More than likely, I won't fight again, but as of right now I'm still getting drug tested. Leaving doors open, you never know what the future holds" (Yahoo/SB Nation; DAZN). He has been tested four times in 2026 per the UFC's anti-doping site, as reported by SB Nation. He also trains Olympic wrestling champion Gable Steveson.
- **Ranking:** unranked (retired). **Next fight:** none. **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style breakdown

A complete fighter with a wrestling base. At range he uses his reach with low kicks and oblique kicks to the lead knee, side kicks and body kicks, plus a long jab and straight right. In the clinch he lands elbows and knees (the knee that finished Rua) and trips opponents to the mat. On top he chains control, elbows and chokes. Six of his 22 UFC wins are submissions, four of them guillotines. His output peaks in round 3 of five-round fights.

## Strengths

- Has never been knocked down in the UFC; no KO/TKO or submission losses.
- 95% takedown defense (opponents 2 of 40) and about 213 s of control per 15 min career.
- Body and leg accuracy of about 76% career and 82-83% decayed.
- Five-round experience: 17 five-round fights.

## Weaknesses

- Age 39, about two years without a fight and a stated retirement; he says he will "more than likely" not fight again.
- Small heavyweight sample: two fights, 17.7 decayed minutes.
- His later light heavyweight fights were close on the scorecards (Santos split decision; Reyes out-struck him).

## Durability and cardio

No knockdowns absorbed in 356.8 UFC minutes. His pace in five-round fights runs 1.00, 1.10, 1.36, 1.16 and 1.01 of round 1, so he is strong late.

## What the numbers say

Career: 4.38 significant strikes landed per minute at 59% accuracy, and he absorbs 2.24 per minute. Of his attempts, 60% go to the head, 19% to the body and 21% to the legs. Takedowns: 1.89 per 15 min at 46%. The decayed sums (71% accuracy, 5.2 landed per minute) are inflated by the one-sided Miocic fight and should be shrunk toward career numbers. Elo 1891, the highest of the heavyweights in this batch.
