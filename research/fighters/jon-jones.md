# Jon Jones: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Bones". Orthodox (switches stance), 6 ft 4 in, 84 in reach, born 1987-07-19 (age 39). UFC record 22-1, 1 NC (ufcstats). His only loss is a 2009 disqualification.

**Research note:** web search was unavailable this session (search budget used up; Wikipedia and ESPN blocked). Fight facts are from the ufcstats snapshot of 2026-10-31; his status after mid-2025 rests on widely reported coverage I could not re-check (low confidence).

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He won the vacant heavyweight title at UFC 285 (Mar 4, 2023) and defended it once, at UFC 309 (Nov 16, 2024).
- **Retirement:** his retirement was announced in June 2025, and Tom Aspinall was promoted from interim to undisputed champion (widely reported; not re-verified this session).
- **Since then:** he has had no UFC fight through the Oct 31, 2026 snapshot. He talked publicly about returning for the White House card, but UFC Freedom 250 (Jun 14, 2026) went ahead without him, with Ciryl Gane beating Alex Pereira for the interim heavyweight title.
- **Next fight:** none found. **Injuries:** none confirmed.

## Style breakdown

A complete fighter with a wrestling base. At range he uses his reach with low kicks and oblique kicks to the lead knee, side kicks and body kicks, plus a long jab and straight right. In the clinch he lands elbows and knees (the knee that finished Rua) and trips opponents to the mat. On top he chains control, elbows and chokes. Six of his 22 UFC wins are submissions, four of them guillotines. His output peaks in round 3 of five-round fights.

## Strengths

- Has never been knocked down in the UFC; no KO/TKO or submission losses.
- 95% takedown defense (opponents 2 of 40) and about 213 s of control per 15 min career.
- Body and leg accuracy of about 76% career and 82-83% decayed.
- Five-round experience: 17 five-round fights.

## Weaknesses

- Age 39, about two years without a fight and a stated retirement.
- Small heavyweight sample: two fights, 17.7 decayed minutes.
- His later light heavyweight fights were close on the scorecards (Santos split decision; Reyes out-struck him).

## Durability and cardio

No knockdowns absorbed in 356.8 UFC minutes. His pace in five-round fights runs 1.00, 1.10, 1.36, 1.16 and 1.01 of round 1, so he is strong late.

## What the numbers say

Career: 5.46 significant strikes landed per minute at 63% accuracy, and he absorbs 2.71 per minute. Of his attempts, 60% go to the head, 19% to the body and 21% to the legs. Takedowns: 1.89 per 15 min at 46%. The decayed sums (73% accuracy, 6.0 landed per minute) are inflated by the one-sided Miocic fight and should be shrunk toward career numbers. Elo 1891, the highest of the heavyweights in this batch.
