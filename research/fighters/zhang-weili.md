# Zhang Weili: scouting dossier

As of Friday, Nov 6, 2026. She/her. "Magnum". Switch stance, 5'4" with a 63" reach, born Aug 13, 1989 (age 37). Pro record 26-4 (ESPN, Wikipedia); UFC record 10-3 (2 KO/TKO, 2 submissions, 6 decisions; losses 1 KO, 2 decisions). Wikipedia gives her birth date as Sep 30, 1990; ufcstats and ESPN give Aug 13, 1989. "Dataset" means `data/derived/fighters.json` and the raw ufcstats CSVs, current through Oct 31, 2026.

**Update, Nov 6, 2026 (open network).** The first pass had no web access; status, ranking and appearance below have now been checked on the web (sources at the end). The fight analysis still comes from ufcstats data.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. She held the strawweight belt twice: she won it in 2019, lost it to Rose Namajunas in 2021, regained it in 2022 and defended it three times. That is 8 strawweight title fights (6-2).
- **Last fight:** she vacated the strawweight belt, moved up and lost a 50-45 x3 decision to Valentina Shevchenko for the flyweight title at UFC 322 (Nov 15, 2025). No fight since.
- **Division:** back at strawweight. She said after UFC 322 that she was moving back down; the UFC ranks her #1 at strawweight and #3 women's pound-for-pound (official rankings last updated Nov 3, 2026).
- **The strawweight belt now:** Mackenzie Dern won it against Virna Jandiroba at UFC 321 (Oct 25, 2025) and defended it against Gillian Robertson at UFC 330 (Aug 15, 2026). A Dern-Zhang fight was discussed (Macau, the White House card and New Jersey were mentioned) but Dern said she did not know why it fell through (Heavy, Jun 29, 2026).
- **Next:** nothing booked as of Nov 6, 2026. Fatima Kline called her out in July; Tatiana Suarez, who fights Jandiroba on Nov 7, is also pitching for the next title shot.
- **Injuries:** none reported; the reason for the year-long layoff was not found.
- **Other:** her 2020 fight with Joanna Jedrzejczyk entered the UFC Hall of Fame fight wing in July 2026 (Wikipedia).

## Style

A compact, powerful switch-stance striker who has become a heavy top player. She lands 55% (decayed) and throws more leg kicks than most strawweights (15% of attempts against a division 9%). She lands 1.65 takedowns and holds 279 s of control per 15 min (decayed), and a quarter of her strikes come on the ground.

## Strengths

- **Sudden power:** a 42-second title-winning KO of Andrade and the spinning back fist on Jedrzejczyk. She lands 0.47 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed (division 0.33).
- **Ground-and-pound:** heavy strikes from top control.
- **Stopping wrestlers:** 14 of 15 Suarez takedowns stuffed.

## Weaknesses

- **Shevchenko fight:** she was out-wrestled and out-struck at 125, where she is small.
- **Chin:** one KO loss (a Namajunas head kick) and two knockdowns absorbed. She takes 0.95 knockdowns per 100 head strikes taken (decayed), about three times the strawweight rate.
- **Hittable:** opponents land 48% on her. Her takedown defence (61% decayed) is only about average.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** solid but not elite.
- **Cardio:** her five-round output runs 1.00, 0.76, 0.80, 0.75 and 0.81 of round 1, against a division norm that rises to 1.15-1.21. She does her best striking early, and later rounds often become top control.

## What the numbers say

Decayed Elo is 1706. The engine should give her strong first-round striking and power, a real takedown and ground-and-pound game against strikers, and a slightly porous defence and chin. Only one of her 13 UFC fights was at flyweight, so a flyweight bout should lean on the division prior.
