## Style breakdown

A southpaw power puncher who comes forward and throws heavy straight punches and hooks in bunches at the head. His seven UFC knockouts all came in round 1, five of them inside 2:15 (Teixeira, Tuivasa, Lewis, Golm, Greene); Blaydes lasted 3:08. His reach is unusually long for his height, so he can land from outside. After the Aspinall and Volkov losses he became more measured: he wrestled Rozenstruik for a decision and mixed in body and leg strikes against Cortes Acosta.

## Strengths

- Knockout power: 3.4 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed career.
- Fast starts: every KO win came in round 1.
- Takedown defense: opponents are 1 of 4 career.
- Has a top-control option when the KO doesn't come.

## Weaknesses

- Very head-heavy: 90% of career attempts go to the head, at 41% head accuracy.
- Long, mobile kickers trouble him (Volkov out-landed him 83-46 at range).
- Two stoppage losses, and he has never fought beyond round 3.
- Output is low in decisions: 1.7 to 4.1 landed per minute.

## Durability and cardio

One knockdown absorbed in 12 fights, but stopped twice: by Overeem after a takedown in 2018, and by Aspinall's punch. He has gone 15 minutes three times, and in each his round-3 landed count matched or beat round 1 (Cortes Acosta 9, 22, 30; Volkov 15, 15, 16; Rozenstruik 11, 3, 11). A five-round fight is untested beyond round 1, so the late rounds against Pereira should lean on the heavyweight prior.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums: 8.7 significant attempts and 3.8 landed per minute at 44% accuracy. He absorbs 3.2 per minute, and opponents land 42%. His attempts go 88% to the head, 8% to the body and 5% to the legs, while opponents target his legs 20% of the time. Career: 8 knockdowns in 63.4 minutes. Elo 1686, the lowest of this batch.
