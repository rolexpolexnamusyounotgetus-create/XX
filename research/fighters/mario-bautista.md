## Current status

- **Ranking:** #5 bantamweight in the official UFC rankings (media panel, released Nov 3, 2026; ufc.com via a Wayback capture of Nov 4) and #4 in the Elo-based Meta UFC rankings released Oct 31, 2026. That explains the earlier split between ufcrankings.org (#5) and ufcalendar.com (#4): they mirror different lists. Petr Yan holds the title after beating Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 333 (Oct 24, 2026).
- **Age and frame:** 33 (born Jul 1, 1993), 5'9" with a 69" reach. ufcstats lists his stance as switch.
- **Record:** 18-3 pro (3 KO/TKO, 7 submissions, 8 decisions; one loss each by KO, submission and decision); UFC 12-3 (1 KO, 4 submissions, 7 decisions). Sherdog, ESPN and Wikipedia agree.
- **Gym:** MMA Lab in Arizona, under head coach John Crouch (Sherdog, ESPN, Wikipedia).
- **Form:** two straight wins in 2026 and 7 wins in his last 8.
- **Booking:** none as of Nov 6, 2026 (Sherdog; "TBD" in both Wikipedia rankings tables; BetMGM). After UFC 329 he asked for a title shot or a No. 1 contender fight (MMA Junkie).
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style

Bautista is a switch-stance all-rounder.

- **Standing:** he throws high volume at good accuracy and invests heavily in the body. 17% of his attempts go to the body, against a 14% league average, and 27% of his landed strikes do.
- **Grappling:** he chains takedown attempts, especially late, and hunts submissions in scrambles. That means the back (three rear-naked chokes and a triangle armbar from back control in the UFC) and, as UFC 329 showed, the legs.
- **Wildcard:** a flying-knee KO (Miles Johns, 2020).

## Strengths

- **Accurate volume:** 5.49 sig landed per minute at 52% (decayed), against 3.97 and 45.6% for the division.
- **Takedown volume:** 3.15 takedowns per 15 min, about double the league rate.
- **Submission threat:** 1.28 submission attempts per 15 min, about three times the league rate.
- **Late rounds:** his round-3 surge beat a top-10 striker.

## Weaknesses

- **Takedown defence:** 57% career and 52% decayed, against 65.7% for the league. The one elite wrestler he has faced, Umar, controlled him for most of 15 minutes.
- **Defence on the feet:** he absorbs 4.24 sig per minute.
- **Takedown finishing:** against strong defenders his shots often fail to land (0 of 10 vs Aldo).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** one KO loss, to Trevin Jones in 2021. He has been knocked down twice in 15 UFC fights.
- **Cardio:** his best work against Sandhagen came in round 3. He has only one five-round booking (the Oliveira main event, finished in R2), so his championship-round cardio is untested in the data.

## What the numbers say

Bautista's numbers describe a busy, efficient two-way fighter. His career strike differential is +1.35 per minute (5.52 landed vs 4.17 absorbed). His control (13.8 s/min career) and submission rates are well above the division. Elo 1698. In the engine he should out-land most strikers and threaten takedowns and submissions late. Against a strong wrestler the main risk is being the one who gets controlled.
