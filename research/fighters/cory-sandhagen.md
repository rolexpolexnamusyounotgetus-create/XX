# Cory Sandhagen: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" is `data/derived/fighters.json` (ufcstats through Oct 31, 2026). Web claims come from search summaries of the linked pages; his pro record, gym and MRI result are unconfirmed. Fact-check (Nov 6): results and stats re-checked against raw ufcstats; status could not be re-searched.

## Current status

- **Ranking:** #6 bantamweight on ufcalendar.com (Nov 3, 2026).
- **Age and frame:** 34 (born Apr 20, 1992), 5'11" with a 70" reach, switch stance per ufcstats. Nickname "Sandman" (Tapology).
- **Form:** two straight losses: a five-round title fight to Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 320 (Oct 4, 2025; 49-45, 49-45, 49-46), then Mario Bautista at UFC 329 (Jul 11, 2026; 29-28 x3).
- **Injury:** after UFC 329 he said he feared Bautista's kneebar had torn his hamstring in round 1 and that he was getting an MRI. No diagnosis or return timeline was found. Boxing News put pre-diagnosis estimates at weeks for a strain to 6-12 months for a full tear.
- **Booking:** none found.

## Style

A long, switch-stance striker whose game rests on footwork (Sportskeeda).

- **Range work:** circles, jabs and throws long straights from both stances, kicking the inside leg as he moves.
- **Finishers:** sudden highlight shots: the wheel kick on Marlon Moraes (2020) and the 28-second flying knee on Frankie Edgar (2021).
- **Wrestling:** he can wrestle when it suits him, as the Font fight showed.
- **Off his back:** after the R3 knockdown at UFC 329 he looked for a leglock (fight coverage), though ufcstats logged no submission attempt.

## Strengths

- **Volume:** 10.85 sig attempts per minute career, against an 8.70 bantamweight average.
- **Leg kicks:** about 90% accurate.
- **Range:** a height and reach edge on most bantamweights.
- **Finishing variety:** 6 UFC KO/TKO wins.

## Weaknesses

- **Wrestling defence:** 57% career and 55% decayed, against a 65.7% average. Opponents average 4.9 takedowns per 15 minutes on him (decayed). Of his five losses since 2021 (Dillashaw by split decision, Yan, Umar, Dvalishvili, Bautista), the last three out-grappled him.
- **Power:** knockdowns down to 0.07 per 15 min (decayed) from 0.24 career.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never stopped by strikes in 17 UFC fights; knocked down twice (Yan 2021, Bautista 2026). His only stoppage loss is a 2020 rear-naked choke by Aljamain Sterling.
- **Cardio:** nine five-round bookings, but his pace slides: rounds 2-5 run at 0.86, 0.94, 0.86 and 0.78 of round 1.

## What the numbers say

The striking edge has narrowed: 4.92 landed vs 3.72 absorbed per minute career, 4.33 vs 4.01 decayed. Opponents now grapple him: decayed control conceded is 11.6 s/min, more than his own 10.7. Elo 1672. In the engine he should win at range against strikers; against wrestlers his takedown defence, plus the unknown state of his hamstring, should decide the outcome.
