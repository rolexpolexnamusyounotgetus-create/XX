# Cory Sandhagen: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" is `data/derived/fighters.json` (ufcstats through Oct 31, 2026). Fact-check (Nov 6): results and stats re-checked against raw ufcstats. Open-web pass (Nov 6, later): ranking, record, booking, injury news and appearance re-checked against the pages linked below. Still unconfirmed: the MRI result on his hamstring and his current gym.

## Current status

- **Ranking:** #6 bantamweight in the official UFC rankings (media panel, released Nov 3, 2026; ufc.com via a Wayback capture of Nov 4) and #6 in the Elo-based Meta UFC rankings released Oct 31, 2026. The UFC began phasing in the Meta model on Jun 20, 2026, and both lists now run side by side (Wikipedia's UFC rankings page carries both).
- **Record:** 18-7 pro (8 KO/TKO, 3 submissions, 7 decisions; losses: 1 submission, 6 decisions), 11-6 in the UFC. Sherdog, ESPN and Wikipedia agree.
- **Age and frame:** 34 (born Apr 20, 1992), 5'11" with a 70" reach, switch stance per ufcstats. Nickname "Sandman" (Tapology).
- **Form:** two straight losses: a five-round title fight to Merab Dvalishvili at UFC 320 (Oct 4, 2025; 49-45, 49-45, 49-46), then Mario Bautista at UFC 329 (Jul 11, 2026; 29-28 x3).
- **Injury:** Bautista's kneebar hyperextended his left leg late in round 1 (Sherdog). Afterwards he wrote that he was "pretty sure the kneebar tore my hamstring" and was getting an MRI that week. No diagnosis, surgery or return timeline has been reported: the newest Sandhagen items on BJPenn's fighter news page date from Jul 13, 2026, and Sherdog lists no upcoming bout. Boxing News put pre-diagnosis estimates at weeks for a strain to 6-12 months for a full tear. **His health is still unconfirmed.**
- **Booking:** none as of Nov 6, 2026 (Sherdog; "TBD" in both Wikipedia rankings tables).
- **Gym:** listings conflict. Wikipedia has High Altitude Martial Arts in Aurora, Colorado (where he teaches) and Forge Fight Club (2024 on); ESPN and Sherdog still show Elevation Fight Team. Unconfirmed.

## Style

A long, switch-stance striker whose game rests on footwork (Sportskeeda).

- **Range work:** circles, jabs and throws long straights from both stances, kicking the inside leg as he moves.
- **Finishers:** sudden highlight shots: the wheel kick on Marlon Moraes (2020) and the 28-second flying knee on Frankie Edgar (2021).
- **Wrestling:** he can wrestle when it suits him, as the Font fight showed.
- **Off his back:** after the R3 knockdown at UFC 329 he looked for a leglock (fight coverage), though ufcstats logged no submission attempt.

## Strengths

- **Volume:** 10.85 sig attempts per minute career, against an 8.70 bantamweight average.
- **Leg kicks:** about 90% accurate.
- **Range:** a height and reach edge on most bantamweights.
- **Finishing variety:** 6 UFC KO/TKO wins.

## Weaknesses

- **Wrestling defence:** 57% career and 55% decayed, against a 65.7% average. Opponents average 4.9 takedowns per 15 minutes on him (decayed). Of his five losses since 2021 (Dillashaw by split decision, Yan, Umar, Dvalishvili, Bautista), the last three out-grappled him.
- **Power:** knockdowns down to 0.07 per 15 min (decayed) from 0.24 career.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** never stopped by strikes in 17 UFC fights; knocked down twice (Yan 2021, Bautista 2026). His only stoppage loss is a 2020 rear-naked choke by Aljamain Sterling.
- **Cardio:** nine five-round bookings, but his pace slides: rounds 2-5 run at 0.86, 0.94, 0.86 and 0.78 of round 1.

## What the numbers say

The striking edge has narrowed: 4.92 landed vs 3.72 absorbed per minute career, 4.33 vs 4.01 decayed. Opponents now grapple him: decayed control conceded is 11.6 s/min, more than his own 10.7. Elo 1672. In the engine he should win at range against strikers; against wrestlers his takedown defence, plus the still-unconfirmed state of his hamstring, should decide the outcome.

## Appearance (photo check, Nov 6)

No photo-based likeness sheet exists for him yet (`data/likeness/cory-sandhagen.json`), so this comes from his ESPN headshot and full-body studio cutout (files dated Jul 9, 2026, UFC 329 fight week), the Cageside Press UFC 329 ceremonial weigh-in photo (Jul 10) and a Getty fight photo from UFC 329 (Jul 11). The cutout's lettering reads correctly, so left and right are his own.

- **Hair:** short auburn-brown, faded at the sides with a longer top swept up.
- **Facial hair:** a short, trimmed ginger beard.
- **Skin:** light (fair), with light chest hair.
- **Tattoos:** a large black-and-grey back piece covering most of the back. EssentiallySports describes it as angels, a cross and other religious artwork and quotes him calling it "more or less like a religious piece"; the design was not confirmed from a clear photo. Smaller pieces: an anchor-like design on the right shin, a small mark inside the right arm, and a tattoo on the back of one calf. The chest, shoulders and the front of the arms are otherwise clean.
- **Kit:** black UFC (Venum) shorts with a grey geometric pattern and SANDHAGEN lettering at UFC 329, fought from the red corner. In the sim the corner colour wins.
