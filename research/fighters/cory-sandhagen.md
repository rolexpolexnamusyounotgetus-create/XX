## Style

Sandhagen is a long, switch-stance striker. He describes footwork as his core strength, and analysts call his game patient and versatile.

- **Range work:** he circles, jabs and throws long straights from both stances, kicking the inside leg as he moves.
- **Finishers:** sudden highlight shots, most famously the wheel kick on Marlon Moraes (2020) and the 28-second flying knee on Frankie Edgar (2021).
- **Wrestling:** he can wrestle when it suits him, as the Font fight showed.
- **Off his back:** he looks for leg locks when taken down.

## Strengths

- **Volume:** 10.85 sig attempts per minute career, against an 8.70 bantamweight average.
- **Leg kicks:** about 90% accurate.
- **Range:** his height and reach give him a range edge on almost every bantamweight.
- **Finishing variety:** 6 UFC KO/TKO wins.

## Weaknesses

- **Wrestling defence:** takedown defence is 57% career and 55% decayed, against a 65.7% league average. In the time-decayed data opponents average 4.9 takedowns per 15 minutes on him. His five losses since 2021 came against TJ Dillashaw (split decision), Petr Yan, Umar Nurmagomedov, Dvalishvili and Bautista. The last three all out-grappled him.
- **Power:** knockdown output has dropped to 0.07 per 15 min (decayed).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** he has never been stopped by strikes in 17 UFC fights. He has been knocked down twice (Yan 2021, Bautista 2026). His only stoppage loss is a 2020 rear-naked choke against Aljamain Sterling.
- **Cardio:** he has nine fights scheduled for five rounds, but his pace slides in them. Rounds 2-5 run at 0.86, 0.94, 0.86 and 0.78 of his round-1 output.

## What the numbers say

The striking edge is real but has narrowed: 4.92 landed vs 3.72 absorbed per minute career, and 4.33 vs 4.01 time-decayed. The main change is that opponents now grapple him. Decayed control conceded is 11.6 s/min, more than his own 10.7. His Elo is 1672. In the engine, Sandhagen should win at range against strikers. Against wrestlers his takedown defence, plus the unknown status of his hamstring, should dominate the outcome.
