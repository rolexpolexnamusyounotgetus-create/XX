## Style

A southpaw counter-striker: she throws 5.1 significant strikes per minute (decayed) against a division 9.2, but lands 53% (division 43%).

- **Kicks:** she is an accurate kicker. 88% of her leg kicks land, and her head kick KO'd Jessica Eye at UFC 238.
- **Ground:** she finishes from the crucifix with elbows (Andrade) or punches (Cerminara).
- **Recent title fights:** she has fought more as a top-control wrestler. She lands 2.8 takedowns per 15 min at 60% and holds 325 s of control per 15 min (division 180).

## Strengths

- **Accuracy:** 53% of significant strikes land; opponents land only 36% on her.
- **Grappling:** takedowns, control and ground strikes, with 82% takedown defence (decayed).
- **Power:** 0.46 knockdowns per 100 significant head strikes landed (division 0.25).
- **Late rounds:** her output rises late in five-rounders.

## Weaknesses

- **Volume:** low output costs her close striking fights (the 2023 draw).
- **Back exposure:** her one submission loss came when Grasso took her back.
- **Body:** 32% of the strikes that land on her go to the body.
- **Age and inactivity:** 38, with no fight in nearly a year.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** no KO/TKO losses in 19 UFC fights and one knockdown absorbed.
