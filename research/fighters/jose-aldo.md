# Jose Aldo: scouting dossier

As of Friday, Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" means `data/derived/fighters.json` and the raw ufcstats CSVs, current through Oct 31, 2026.

**Open-web pass (Nov 6, 2026).** The first pass could not reach the web. Status, record and appearance are now sourced (links below): **Aldo is retired.** The style, numbers and engine notes still come from ufcstats data.

## Current status

- **Status:** retired from MMA. He announced it in the cage after losing to Aiemann Zahabi at UFC 315 (May 10, 2025: "I don't think I have it in me anymore", MMA Fighting) and made it official by laying his gloves in the center of the octagon at UFC Fight Night in Rio de Janeiro on Oct 11, 2025 (MMA Fighting; Getty photo via Sherdog). It is his second retirement: he first stepped away in Sep 2022 and came back in May 2024.
- **Since then:** a 10-minute submission-only grappling match with Marlon Vera at Hype FC in Quito, Ecuador, on Oct 4, 2026, ended in a draw (MMA Fighting); Vera hunted takedowns, Aldo pulled guard and escaped a heel hook. No MMA, boxing or grappling booking found as of Nov 6, 2026. ESPN's profile still lists him as active, which looks stale.
- **Ranking:** none. He is not in either UFC ranking list (retired fighters are not eligible).
- **Pro record:** 32-10 in MMA (17 KO/TKO, 1 submission, 14 decisions; losses 4 KO/TKO, 1 submission, 5 decisions) per Sherdog, ESPN, MMA Fighting and Wikipedia. Pro boxing 1-0-1 (Wikipedia).
- **Honours:** UFC Hall of Fame (modern wing, class of 2023); inaugural UFC featherweight champion with seven straight defences.
- **Injuries:** none reported. UFC 315 moved to featherweight on weigh-in day after he could not make bantamweight (Wikipedia).
- **Age and frame:** 40 (born Sep 9, 1986), 5'7" with a 70" reach, orthodox.
- **Division:** listed at bantamweight for his recent run, but his last bout was at featherweight.
- **UFC record:** 14-9 (4 KO/TKO, 0 submissions, 10 decisions; losses 4 KO/TKO, 5 decisions).
- **Title history:** 11 UFC featherweight title fights (8-3) from 2011 to 2017, plus a 2020 bantamweight title loss to Petr Yan.
- **Last fight:** a 28-29 x3 decision loss to Aiemann Zahabi at UFC 315 (May 10, 2025), after which he retired.

## Style

In the UFC data Aldo is a patient counter-striker.

- **Volume:** opponents attempt 10.1 sig strikes per minute against his 7.9, but he lands 45% and held opponents to 41.6% accuracy over his career.
- **Body attack:** 20.7% of his landed strikes go to the body, and he KO'd Jeremy Stephens with body punches in 2018.
- **Kicks and knees:** his older low-kick game is barely present here (6% of attempts to the legs). His clinch knee finished Chad Mendes in 2012.
- **Takedown defence:** elite.

## Strengths

- **Takedown defence:** 91.6% career and 96% time-decayed. Only 9 of 107 UFC attempts against him succeeded.
- **Body work:** accurate body punching.
- **Power:** knockdowns in two of his last five fights (Font, Zahabi).

## Weaknesses

- **Output:** he is out-thrown, and his decayed striking is negative (4.07 landed vs 4.94 absorbed per minute).
- **Cage pressure:** wrestlers can pin him on the fence without completing takedowns.
- **Age:** he is 40 and has lost his last two.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** four UFC KO/TKO losses (McGregor in 13 seconds in 2015, Holloway twice in 2017, Yan in 2020), and three knockdowns absorbed. His time-decayed knockdowns absorbed are low (0.17) because his recent fights went the distance.
- **Cardio:** in 5-round fights his output by round is 1.00, 1.13, 1.07, 0.72 and 0.56 of round 1, a clear late fade.

## What the numbers say

He is retired, so he only appears in the sim in hypothetical or historical matchups. The engine should give Aldo near-immunity to takedowns, a body-heavy target split (17% body attempts, decayed) and decent single-shot power. It should also give him below-average volume and a late-round decline. His decayed Elo is 1645. Against a high-pace opponent he should drop decisions on volume, as the data shows against Zahabi, Merab and Bautista.
