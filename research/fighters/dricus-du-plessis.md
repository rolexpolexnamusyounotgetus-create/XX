## Current status
- **Former UFC middleweight champion.** He won the title from Sean Strickland (SD, UFC 297, Jan 20, 2024). He defended it against Israel Adesanya (RNC R4, UFC 305, Aug 17, 2024) and Strickland again (UD, UFC 312, Feb 8, 2025). He lost it to Khamzat Chimaev (UD 50-44 x3, UFC 319, Aug 16, 2025).
- **Last fight:** a UD over Kamaru Usman (50-45, 49-46, 49-46), the five-round main event of UFC Fight Night 281 in Oklahoma City on Jul 18, 2026 (Fight of the Night).
- **Record:** 24-3 pro (ESPN, Wikipedia, MMA Junkie); UFC 10-1 (4 KO/TKO, 2 SUB, 4 DEC).
- **Ranking:** #2 middleweight on the UFC media panel (Nov 3-5, 2026) and in the Meta UFC Rankings (Oct 31, 2026).
- **Next:** nothing booked. His coach Morne Visser said in September 2026 that he will not fight again this year and plans to return early in 2027; Visser expects champion Strickland to beat Nassourdine Imavov and a Du Plessis-Strickland trilogy to follow, and also named Chimaev as an option (MMA Sucka, Sep 17; MMA Junkie via Yahoo, Sep 22). Brendan Allen called him out after beating Christian Leroy Duncan on Oct 10, 2026: "where's Dricus du Plessis? He's been running for a little while" (Yahoo).
- **Injuries:** none reported in the coverage checked.

## Recent fights
- **W Usman (UD, Jul 2026):** He out-landed Usman in four of five rounds and 136-92 overall, almost all of it at distance. Usman went 0 for 7 on takedowns. Usman's best work went to the body (37 of 58 landed).
- **L Chimaev (UD, Aug 2025):** Chimaev took him down 12 times in 17 attempts and held him for 21:40. Du Plessis landed 13 significant strikes in five rounds, with one takedown and one submission attempt, both in R5.
- **W Strickland 2 (UD, Feb 2025):** He out-landed Strickland 147-128 by changing targets: 52 of 62 leg strikes and 45 of 59 body strikes landed.
- **W Adesanya (RNC R4, Aug 2024):** He trailed 90-97 in significant strikes but went 4 for 9 on takedowns, had 3:39 of control and finished from the back.
- **W Strickland 1 (SD, Jan 2024):** He was out-landed 137-173 but landed 6 of 11 takedowns and took two of the three cards 48-47.
- **W Robert Whittaker (KO R2, Jul 2023):** He dropped Whittaker and out-landed him 62-31.

## Style
He is a pressure fighter who switches stance, throws in volume and keeps coming forward. He attempts 9.9 significant strikes per minute (the middleweight average is 8.1) at 48% accuracy. He adjusts his targets to the opponent. Against Usman he went mostly to the head. Against Strickland he kicked the legs and body. He also wrestles and takes the back: a neck crank from back control finished Darren Till, and a rear-naked choke finished Adesanya.

**Strengths:** volume and pressure that rise late, punching power at distance (Perez, Giles and Whittaker were all finished by punches), changing targets mid-fight, back takes, durability.

**Weaknesses:** sustained chain wrestling. His takedown defense is 54.8% decayed and 50% career, against a middleweight average near 64%. Opponents control him 19% of the time. He also absorbs about the league average of significant strikes (4.0 per minute).

## Durability and cardio
He has **never been knocked down in 11 UFC fights** and has no stoppage losses. Over his five five-round fights, his output by round is 1.00, 1.19, 1.40, 1.19 and 1.45 times R1. Against the middleweight curve (1.26, 1.20, 1.28, 1.31 for R2-R5), his R3 and R5 sit above it and his R2 and R4 below.

## What the numbers say (decayed, 2-year half-life)

- Target split of attempts: 76.8% head, 11.8% body, 11.4% leg (middleweight average 77/14/9). 89% of his landed strikes come at distance.
- 1.39 takedowns per 15 minutes at 47% accuracy. Control share is 7% for him and 19% for his opponents.
