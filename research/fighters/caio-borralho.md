## Style
A **southpaw all-rounder who now leads with striking**. 86.5% of his decayed sig strikes land at distance, up from 82% career. He works the body (17.6% of attempts) and the legs (11.6%) more than the league average of 14% and 9%. He keeps a real grappling threat (an RNC win, 19% control share) but has used it less lately: 0.75 takedowns per 15 minutes decayed against 1.08 career.

**Strengths:** defense (opponents land only 40.5% of sig strikes against him), durability, body and leg work, and late-round momentum.
**Weaknesses:** slow starts, low volume (6.7 attempts per minute against about 8.1 for the league), and trouble with a longer jab-first striker (Imavov).
