# Caio Borralho ("The Natural")

As of Nov 6, 2026. Southpaw middleweight, age 33 (born Jan 16, 1993), 6'1" in the dataset (Sherdog lists 6'2") with a 75" reach. Fights out of The Fighting Nerds (Sherdog, ESPN). Dataset: `data/derived/fighters.json`, snapshot of Oct 31, 2026. This replaces the thin first pass, which was written without page access.

## Current status
- **Ranked #5** at middleweight on the UFC media rankings updated Nov 3, 2026 (CBS Sports; ufcalendar.com mirror); #6 on the UFC/Meta rankings (Oct 31).
- **Record:** 18-2 (1 NC) pro (Sherdog, Wikipedia).
- **Last fight:** Mar 7, 2026, a **unanimous decision over Reinier de Ridder** (30-27 x3) at UFC 326.
- **Injury:** He **fractured a rib in training about two weeks before UFC 326** and fought through it. The rib did not heal and became displaced, which he said could have punctured a lung, so he **had surgery in June 2026** and pulled out of an unannounced UFC 330 bout with Joe Pyfer (Aug 15, Philadelphia) (EssentiallySports, quoting his Instagram, Jun 6; Bloody Elbow via Wikipedia).
- **Next (booked):** **Yousri Belgaroui (11-3) at UFC 334, Madison Square Garden, New York, Nov 14, 2026**, a three-round middleweight bout. It is on ESPN's schedule and Sherdog's fight card.

## Recent fights
- **W de Ridder (UD, Mar 2026):** He kept it standing and won the range battle 57-55 in sig strikes on far fewer attempts (98 vs 141). He landed 16 of 17 leg strikes and stopped 6 of de Ridder's 8 takedown attempts, all on a cracked rib.
- **L Nassourdine Imavov (UD 50-45, 49-46, 49-46, Sep 2025):** This was his first loss in about a decade. He started very slowly, landing 6 and 7 sig strikes in R1-R2, and went 0 for 5 on takedowns. He rallied late (24 sig in R5) but lost 66-81 overall.
- **W Jared Cannonier (UD, 5 rds, Aug 2024):** He outlanded Cannonier 153-83 with a knockdown, building from 15 sig in R1 to 50 in R5.
- **W Paul Craig (KO R2, May 2024)** with punches at distance. **W Abus Magomedov (UD, Nov 2023). W Michal Oleksiejczuk (RNC R2, Apr 2023).**

## Style
A **powerful, explosive southpaw all-rounder who now leads with striking**. Sherdog's UFC Paris preview (Ben Duffy, Sep 2025) describes "hard one-twos, huge hooks and a variety of hard kicks to all levels"; wrestling that is "more effective and opportunistic than technical"; and, once he has an opponent down, heavy ground-and-pound while advancing position, then a quick back take for chokes. 86.5% of his decayed sig strikes land at distance, up from 82% career. He works the body (17.6% of attempts) and legs (11.6%) more than the league average of 14% and 9%. He has used his grappling less lately: 0.75 takedowns per 15 minutes decayed against 1.08 career.

**Strengths:** durability, defense by the numbers (opponents land only 40.5% of sig strikes against him), body and leg work, power, and late-round momentum.
**Weaknesses:** Sherdog calls his head "very hittable for an elite fighter," as Cannonier showed. Slow starts and low volume (6.7 attempts per minute against about 8.1 for the league). Trouble with a longer, jab-first striker (Imavov). He is returning from rib surgery after eight months out.

## Durability and cardio
He has **no knockdowns absorbed and no KO losses** in 9 UFC fights. The sources disagree on his cardio. Sherdog's preview says his muscular build and pace leave him "visibly slowed in Round 3 and later." The round-by-round numbers point the other way: across his two five-rounders, his R3 output is 2.57x his R1 output and R5 is 3.52x (Cannonier 15, 21, 37, 30, 50; Imavov 6, 7, 17, 12, 24). That is a small sample, so read it as rising output, not proof of fresh legs.
