## Current status

- **Title:** former UFC bantamweight champion. He won the belt from Aljamain Sterling (UFC 292, Aug 19, 2023) and defended it against Marlon Vera (UFC 299, Mar 9, 2024).
- **Since the belt:** he lost twice to Merab Dvalishvili, then won twice. He beat Song Yadong at UFC 324 (Jan 24, 2026) and KO'd Aiemann Zahabi at UFC Freedom 250 in Washington, DC (Jun 14, 2026).
- **Title picture:** on Oct 25, Dana White named O'Malley and Song as the contenders for champion Petr Yan (Bloody Elbow). Headlines in Sherdog and Boxing News had Yan favoured in that rematch. O'Malley beat Yan by split decision in 2022.
- **Age and frame:** 32 (born Oct 24, 1994), 5'11", 72" reach, switch stance. Nickname "Suga".
- **Booking:** none found.

## Style

O'Malley is a rangy switch-stance counter striker.

- **Range:** 96% of his attempts come at distance.
- **Accuracy:** 60% career sig accuracy and 54% to the head, against league figures of about 46% and 37%.
- **Body attack:** 22% of his decayed attempts go to the body, the 4th-highest share among 51 active bantamweights.
- **Finishing:** he hits hard enough to end fights with clean shots. He has 7 KO/TKO wins, including Sterling for the title.

## Strengths

- **Chin and defence:** he has never been knocked down in 16 UFC fights, and opponents land only 27.7% of head attempts on him.
- **Power:** 7 knockdowns, 0.55 per 15 min career against a 0.31 prior.
- **Late rounds:** he took over late against Vera and Song.

## Weaknesses

- **Wrestling:** takedown defence is 60%, against a 65.7% league average. Dvalishvili took him down 11 times over their two fights, held 16:32 of control and submitted him.
- **Leg kicks:** opponents throw 24% of their decayed attempts at his legs (league 9.7%), and 42% of what lands on him is leg strikes. Zahabi, Munhoz and Song all found the legs, and Vera's 2020 TKO came after 9 of 10 leg strikes landed.
- **No offensive wrestling:** 3 takedowns landed in 16 UFC fights.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** one KO/TKO loss (Vera, 2020, elbows from guard after leg damage) and no knockdowns absorbed.
- **Cardio:** he has 4 five-round fights. His pace looks strong late (1.00 / 1.90 / 1.03 / 2.03 / 2.36), but the Vera fight dominates that small sample.

## What the numbers say

Decayed rates: he lands 4.75 per minute and absorbs 3.25. His attempt split is 71.5% head, 22.4% body and 6.1% leg. He makes almost no takedown attempts, and opponents take him down 2.3 times per 15 min. His Elo is 1714. In the engine he should win most striking exchanges at range and carry above-average knockdown power. His main losing paths should be takedowns and leg-kick accumulation, so wrestlers and kickers deserve a real edge against him.
