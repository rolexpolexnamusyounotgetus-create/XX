# Sean O'Malley: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. "Dataset" = `data/derived/fighters.json` (ufcstats snapshot to Oct 31, 2026). Fight numbers re-checked against raw ufcstats rows on Nov 6.

**Research note (updated Nov 6, 2026):** the network was open for this pass. Status, ranking, record, bookings, injuries and appearance were re-checked on the web (pages listed under Sources, all fetched on Nov 6 unless marked as a search summary). ufc.com returned 403, so the official rankings were read through two mirrors that agree. Fight numbers still come from the ufcstats snapshot of Oct 31, 2026.

## Current status

- **Title:** former UFC bantamweight champion. He won the belt from Aljamain Sterling (UFC 292, Aug 19, 2023) and defended it against Marlon Vera (UFC 299, Mar 9, 2024).
- **Ranking:** #2 bantamweight in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026) and #3 in the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31, 2026).
- **Since the belt:** he lost twice to Merab Dvalishvili, then won twice. He beat Song Yadong at UFC 324 (Jan 24, 2026) and KO'd Aiemann Zahabi at UFC Freedom 250 in Washington, DC (Jun 14, 2026).
- **Title picture:** on Oct 25, Dana White named O'Malley and Song as the contenders for champion Petr Yan (Bloody Elbow). O'Malley told Daniel Cormier "me versus Petr is happening next" (MMA Fighting, Oct 28). Headlines in Sherdog and Boxing News had Yan favoured in that rematch. O'Malley beat Yan by split decision in 2022.
- **Record:** 20-3 (1 NC) pro per Wikipedia (UFCalendar shows 20-3); 12-3 (1 NC) UFC per ufcstats.
- **Age and frame:** 32 (born Oct 24, 1994), 5'11", 72" reach, switch stance. Nickname "Suga".
- **Booking:** none on Nov 6 (UFCalendar).
- **Injuries:** no current injury reported. He had hip labrum surgery in 2024 and fought Dvalishvili at UFC 316 about seven months later, which he calls his biggest regret. On Oct 19 he described about three years of lower-back discomfort that has been improving with a regular exercise routine (his YouTube channel, via MiddleEasy).

## Style

- **Range:** 96% of attempts come at distance.
- **Accuracy:** 60% career sig accuracy and 54% to the head, against league figures of about 46% and 37%.
- **Body attack:** 22% of his decayed attempts go to the body, the 4th-highest share among 51 bantamweights with 30+ decayed minutes in the dataset.
- **Finishing:** 7 KO/TKO wins, including Sterling for the title.

## Strengths

- **Chin and defence:** never knocked down in 16 UFC fights; opponents land just 27.7% of head attempts.
- **Power:** 7 knockdowns, 0.55 per 15 min career against a 0.31 prior.

## Weaknesses

- **Wrestling:** takedown defence is 60%, against a 65.7% league average. Dvalishvili took him down 11 times over their two fights, held 16:32 of control and submitted him.
- **Leg kicks:** opponents throw 24% of their decayed attempts at his legs (league 9.7%), and 42% of what lands on him is leg strikes. Zahabi, Munhoz and Song all chopped them.
- **No offensive wrestling:** 3 takedowns landed in 16 UFC fights.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** one KO/TKO loss (Vera, 2020: elbows from guard after Vera landed 9 of 10 leg strikes) and no knockdowns absorbed.
- **Cardio:** 4 five-round fights; late pace looks strong (1.00 / 1.90 / 1.03 / 2.03 / 2.36), but the Vera fight dominates that sample.

## What the numbers say

Decayed: lands 4.75 per minute, absorbs 3.25; attempt split 71.5% head, 22.4% body, 6.1% leg; taken down 2.3 times per 15 min. Elo 1714. In the engine he should win most striking exchanges at range and carry above-average knockdown power. His losing paths are takedowns and leg-kick accumulation, so wrestlers and kickers deserve a real edge.

- **Hair:** dyed bright magenta-pink and curly, with red on his right side and blue on his left (ESPN, weigh-in), worn in tight magenta and blue cornrows on fight night. He changes colour between fights, and no photo after June 2026 was checked, so treat the colour as dated.
- **Facial hair:** moustache and short chin beard (goatee) over light stubble.
- **Skin:** light.
- **Tattoos:** heavily tattooed. A large winged bird across the chest, "doingwell" script below the collarbones, full sleeves on both arms, script on the ribs, neck pieces, and small face tattoos (a star beside the left eye, a heart and "love" on the right cheek, script at the hairline).
- **Kit:** compression-length white UFC Freedom 250 shorts with blue pinstripes and red-and-white stripes at the White House card; navy shorts at the weigh-in. Corner colour wins in the sim.
