## Style breakdown

A switch-stance boxer. 88% of his landed strikes come at distance. He attacks the body more than most welterweights (19.5% of attempts vs 13.9%), and a punch to the body knocked out Ramazan Emeev. He starts fast in three-round fights, landing 6.89 per minute in round 1, and finished each of his first four UFC opponents in round 1. In five-round fights he finishes strong: his 53 landed in round 5 against Muhammad is the clearest example. He kicks the legs a little (7% of attempts) and seldom clinches.

## Strengths

- Boxing volume and accuracy: 51.7% decayed (division 47.7%), with opponents landing only 42.7% on him.
- Body work, plus late-fight output.
- Power early in his UFC run: 0.76 knockdowns per 15 min career. That has dropped to 0.19 over the last 3 years.

## Weaknesses

- Wrestling defense: 41.7% takedown defense over the last 3 years, and 331 s per 15 min spent controlled (division 167). Makhachev, Burns and Hafez all held him down for long spells.
- Almost no takedowns of his own (0.48 per 15 decayed).
- First KO loss and three knockdowns absorbed in the Prates fight.

## Durability and cardio

Never knocked down in his first 105 UFC minutes, then dropped three times by Prates. It is too early to call that a chin trend, but it is a real data point. His cardio is good: output holds or rises across five rounds.
