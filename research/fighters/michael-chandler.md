# Michael Chandler: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Orthodox, 5'8", 71-inch reach, born 1986-04-24 (age 40) in High Ridge, Missouri. Nickname "Iron". American; trains with Kill Cliff FC (ESPN) and owns the Training Camp gym in Nashville (Wikipedia). Pro record 23-11-0 (ESPN, UFCalendar); UFC record 2-6 in fighters.json (since his Jan 2021 debut). His earlier Bellator career, with three Bellator lightweight titles, is not in the dataset.*

> Research note: this file was first written without web access. On Nov 6, 2026 the status, ranking, record and appearance were checked on the web (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org, MMA Mania, MiddleEasy, MMA Sucka, Cageside Press, Wikipedia via its API) and against the photo-based likeness sheet data/likeness/michael-chandler.json. ufc.com returned 403 and MMA Junkie returned 402 to scripts, so those pages were seen only as search snippets.

## Current status
**Active, unranked and unbooked.** At UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn (**Jun 14, 2026**), Mauricio Ruffy knocked him down twice and stopped him with ground punches at 4:29 of round 1. Reports credit spinning kicks for the damage (see the Ruffy dossier). That made four straight losses:
- **Dustin Poirier**, RNC, Nov 2022
- **Charles Oliveira**, decision, Nov 2024
- **Paddy Pimblett**, TKO, Apr 2025
- **Ruffy**, TKO, Jun 2026

- **Retirement:** not retired. Despite public calls to retire (Din Thomas, Dustin Poirier, Arman Tsarukyan), he said he plans to keep fighting (MMA Junkie, Sep 18, 2026; BJPenn). ESPN lists him as active.
- **Ranking:** none. He is outside the lightweight top 15 on both the media-panel board (Nov 3, 2026) and the UFC/Meta board (Oct 31, 2026), per UFCalendar and ufcrankings.org.
- **Next fight:** none booked (ESPN, UFCalendar on Nov 6). A fight with King Green has been floated: Green called him out, and Jorge Masvidal discussed it on a podcast (MMA Sucka, Oct 27). Nothing is official.
- **Other:** on Nov 4, 2026 he was a special guest referee on WWE NXT. Cageside Press sees no clear sign of a pro wrestling move.
- **Medical:** a 45-day mandatory suspension after the Ruffy TKO, long expired (MMA Mania). No other injury was reported.

The 24-month gap between the Poirier and Oliveira fights is in the dataset (the cancelled Conor McGregor fight at UFC 303).

## Recent fights
- **L Ruffy, Freedom 250 (Jun 2026), TKO R1 4:29.** He landed 3 significant strikes to Ruffy's 19 and went 0 of 3 on takedowns. He was bleeding from the nose by mid-round, and spinning kicks hurt and dropped him before the finish (MiddleEasy).
- **L Pimblett, UFC 314 (Apr 2025), elbows from mount R3 3:07.** He went 4 of 7 on takedowns early, then was outlanded 80-11. The Pimblett dossier cites CBS for a cut opened by a knee.
- **L Oliveira, UFC 309 (Nov 2024), 45-49, 46-49, 46-49.** Oliveira controlled him for 14:52. In round 5 Chandler landed 40 of 47 significant strikes with 1:59 of control.
- **L Poirier, UFC 281 (Nov 2022), RNC R3.** He went 3 of 7 on takedowns with 5:39 of control before Poirier took his back.
- **W Tony Ferguson, UFC 274 (May 2022), front-kick KO 17 seconds into R2.**
- **L Justin Gaethje, UFC 268 (Nov 2021), decision.** He landed 103 significant strikes, 28 of them to the body.
- **W Dan Hooker, UFC 257 (Jan 2021), KO R1 2:30.**

## Style
He is an explosive wrestle-boxer who looks for an early finish. He leads with power punches (75% of attempts to the head) and surprises opponents with front kicks. He shoots early (1.6 takedowns per 15 minutes at 36%) and follows with ground strikes, which make up 24% of what he lands. He trades readily and accepts return fire.

## Strengths
- **Early danger:** stoppages of Hooker in round 1 and Ferguson in round 2; KO power with punches and kicks.
- **Wrestling entries:** takedowns against Poirier and Pimblett, two well-rounded opponents.
- **Keeps pushing when behind:** the round-5 surge against Oliveira after four lopsided rounds.

## Weaknesses
- **Defence:** 41% significant-strike defence, third-lowest of 120 lightweights with 30+ UFC minutes. He absorbs 4.8 per minute (median about 4.1).
- **Chin:** 5 knockdowns absorbed in 82.7 minutes (0.91 per 15; median about 0.24) and 3 KO/TKO losses in the UFC.
- **Grappling defence:** 56% takedown defence, and opponents control him 29-35% of the time.

## Durability and cardio
His damage has built up: three of his last four losses ended inside the distance. Output falls after the first round in his five-round fights (rounds 2-4 at 0.78 / 0.63 / 0.59 of round 1). The round-5 value of 2.93 is one round of late output against Oliveira, not a pattern.

## What the numbers say
Elo 1499, 73rd among the 100 lightweights who have fought since May 2025, and near the starting value of 1500. Recency-weighted, he lands 2.9 significant strikes per minute and absorbs 4.6. That is a fighter who is still dangerous early but loses the exchanges. For a "prime" version, the 2021-22 fights (Hooker, Ferguson, Gaethje) are the best guide.
