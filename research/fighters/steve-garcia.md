# Steve Garcia: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Southpaw, 6 ft 0 in, 75 in reach, born 1992-05-22 (age 34). Fights out of Jackson-Wink MMA, Albuquerque, New Mexico. Nickname "Mean Machine". UFC record 8-3 (ufcstats); pro record 19-6.

## Current status

- **Last fight:** KO loss to Diego Lopes at 2:42 of round 2, UFC Freedom 250, on the White House South Lawn, Jun 14, 2026. It ended a seven-fight winning streak (six stoppages).
- **Next:** vs former interim champion Yair Rodriguez at UFC 335, Dec 12, 2026, T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas. Garcia confirmed the bout on Instagram and MMA Mania reported it. Rodriguez has not fought since April 2025. Boxing News says a win would likely move Garcia into the featherweight top 10. Before the booking, Garcia said he wanted Jean Silva at Madison Square Garden.
- **Ranking:** #14 featherweight (cited by MMA Mania). Other sites list him anywhere from #9 to #14.
- **Injuries:** None reported in the sources found.

## Style breakdown

A long southpaw pressure fighter. He walks opponents to the fence and throws a lot, with the straight left as his best punch. Decayed, 85% of his significant attempts go to the head (featherweight league 76%). He goes to the body with punches more than kicks (the 2023 body-shot KO of Nuerdanbieke, 23 body strikes vs Kattar). Once an opponent is hurt he follows them to the mat with punches and elbows. Four of his UFC finishes came on the ground. He rarely wrestles. His four UFC takedowns all came in 2020-21.

## Strengths

- Power: 3.15 KD per 100 head strikes landed, over three times the featherweight rate. 7 of 8 UFC wins by KO/TKO.
- Output: about 10.6 significant attempts per minute career and 13.3 decayed.
- Fast finishes: four first-round stoppages in the UFC.
- Takedown defense: 81% decayed (league 62%).

## Weaknesses

- Chin: 6 knockdowns absorbed and 2 KO losses in 11 UFC fights. That is 5.45 KD per 100 head strikes absorbed, against a 0.94 league rate.
- Defensive lapses in exchanges. He was winning the Lopes fight until a hook exchange.
- Limited wrestling offense. He was held down for 14 minutes by Luis Pena in his 2020 debut, but has largely kept fights standing since.
- Weight-making was a recurring problem early in his career (Albuquerque Journal, 2020).

## Durability and cardio

Durability is the main question: six knockdowns in 68 UFC minutes. On cardio he kept a very high pace for 15 minutes against Kattar (250 attempts). He has no rounds 4-5 on record, since his only five-round booking ended in round 1.
