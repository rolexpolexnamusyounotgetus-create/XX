# Quillan Salkilld: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Orthodox, 6'0", 75-inch reach, born 1999-12-28. Australian, fights out of Luistro Combat Academy in Perth. 13-1 pro, 6-0 in the UFC.*

## Current status
**Unbeaten in the UFC and booked for a headliner.** On **Aug 8, 2026** he submitted Mateusz Gamrot with a rear-naked choke at 4:25 of round 1 in the UFC Vegas 120 main event. He was the second man to submit Gamrot in the UFC, after Charles Oliveira (rear-naked choke, Oct 11, 2025, per fighters.json). On **Oct 27, 2026** the UFC announced him against **Benoit Saint Denis** as the five-round main event of UFC Fight Night Sydney at Afterpay Arena on **Feb 7, 2027** (Feb 6 US time). Rankings in early November disagree: he is #6 on ufcalendar.com (Nov 3) and #7 on Home of Fight (Nov 5). He has no reported injuries.

## Recent fights
- **W Gamrot, Vegas 120 (Aug 2026), RNC in round 1.** He opened with jabs and kicks, took Gamrot down with about two minutes left in the round, defended a leg-lock attempt in a scramble and took the back.
- **W Beneil Dariush, UFC Perth (May 2, 2026), TKO in round 1 at 3:29.** An early Dariush jab hurt him (reported as a knockdown; UFC Stats did not score one). He fought off repeated takedown attempts (Dariush completed 2 of 5), dropped Dariush with a right hook and finished with ground strikes.
- **W Jamie Mullarkey, UFC 325 (Jan 31, 2026), neck crank from back control in round 1.**
- **W Nasrat Haqparast, UFC 321 (Oct 25, 2025), head-kick KO in round 1.**
- **W Yanal Ashmouz, UFC 316 (Jun 2025), unanimous decision.** He landed 79 significant strikes, completed 8 of 23 takedowns and controlled Ashmouz for 7:35.
- **W Anshul Jubli, UFC 312 (Feb 2025), KO in 19 seconds** with a straight right.
- He earned his contract with a decision win on Dana White's Contender Series in Sep 2024.

## Style
He is a long, powerful all-rounder. The jab and straight right lead, with kicks to the body and head off them. When he hurts someone, or when a scramble opens, he takes the back and chokes. Profile pages describe him as a Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt, but I could not confirm that from a primary source. Against Ashmouz he showed he can grind out three rounds of takedowns and top control.

## Strengths
- **Finishing:** 5 of 6 UFC wins in round 1; knockdowns at 1.5 per 15 minutes (lightweight median about 0.31).
- **Finishes both ways:** two KO/TKOs by punches, one head-kick KO and two back-take submissions.
- **Takedown defence:** 77% (10 of 13).
- **Takes little damage:** about 2.0 significant strikes absorbed per minute (median about 4.1).

## Weaknesses
- **Tiny sample:** 28.8 UFC minutes. He has never gone past three rounds, and only one UFC fight has passed round 1.
- **Defence:** striking defence about 50%, below the median of about 54%. Dariush hurt him early.
- **Takedown accuracy** is only 33%.

## Durability and cardio
He has not been knocked down in the UFC according to UFC Stats, though the Dariush jab clearly hurt him. His cardio over 25 minutes is **unknown**: his only five-round booking ended in round 1, so fighters.json has pace data for round 1 only.

## What the numbers say
He lands 4.4 significant strikes per minute at 58% accuracy (median 4.1 at 47%) and controls 37% of fight time (median 17%). His Elo of 1690 ranks 8th among active lightweights, level with Pimblett. Every rate rests on a very small sample, so his profile should carry wider uncertainty than veterans with similar numbers.
