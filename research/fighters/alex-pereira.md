# Alex Pereira: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Poatan". Orthodox, 6 ft 4 in, 79 in reach, born 1987-07-07 (age 39). UFC record 10-3 (ufcstats); pro record 13-4 (ESPN, Sherdog). Trains at Teixeira MMA & Fitness (ESPN, Sherdog). Re-checked on Nov 6, 2026 with direct page fetches (ESPN schedule, Sherdog, CBS Sports, MMA Mania) and the ESPN/UFC studio photos.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He vacated the light heavyweight belt, ending his second reign, to fight Ciryl Gane for the interim heavyweight title at UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn (Jun 14, 2026). Gane stopped him at 1:27 of round 2.
- **Next (booked):** Sergei Pavlovich at UFC 335, Dec 12, 2026, T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas: a five-round heavyweight co-main event under Oliveira vs Lopes, announced by Dana White on the UFC 332 broadcast (Oct 3, 2026) and on the ESPN and Sherdog schedules. Pereira had pushed for an immediate Gane rematch, but Gane was booked against Josh Hokit (UFC 334, Nov 14) instead. Pereira said in late October that he will go back to light heavyweight if he loses to Pavlovich (MMA Mania, Oct 22).
- **Ranking:** the media panel still has him **#3 at light heavyweight** (UFC media rankings updated Nov 3, 2026, per CBS Sports and ufcalendar.com); he is not in the media heavyweight top 15. On the UFC/Meta algorithmic rankings (Oct 31) he is #1 at light heavyweight and #3 at heavyweight.
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style breakdown

A kickboxer through and through: a former Glory champion at middleweight and light heavyweight who beat Israel Adesanya in kickboxing before beating him again for the UFC middleweight title (UFC 281, TKO R5). He walks forward behind a high guard, jabs and chops the lead leg with rear-leg low kicks, and waits for the opponent to lead so he can land the left hook. He finishes hurt opponents on the ground with elbows. He has also scored with a left head kick (Prochazka) and a flying knee (Michailidis).

## Strengths

- One-shot power: 8 of 10 UFC wins by KO/TKO, 2.0 knockdowns per 100 significant head strikes landed (decayed).
- Low kicks: 82% leg accuracy decayed; 30% of his landed strikes go to the legs.
- Five-round output rises late (round 4-5 pace 1.32 and 1.35 of round 1).
- Takedown defense of 86% (decayed).

## Weaknesses

- Little offensive wrestling (0.07 takedowns per 15 min decayed). Opponents get about 170 s of control per 15 min decayed, mostly cage clinch work.
- KO/TKO losses to Adesanya (UFC 287, round 2) and Gane.
- At heavyweight his size edge is gone: Gane out-landed him 54-12.

## Durability and cardio

Two knockdowns absorbed in 13 UFC fights. He has been finished twice, both times in round 2. He has gone 25 minutes once (Ankalaev 1) and closed strongly in five-round fights.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums in fighters.json: 8.0 significant attempts and 4.8 landed per minute at 60% accuracy; absorbs 4.0 per minute. Of his attempts, 60% go to the head, 17% to the body and 22% to the legs. Opponents land 49% against him. Elo 1716. Of his 13 UFC fights, 9 were five-rounders, and only the Gane fight was at heavyweight, so his heavyweight inputs should lean on the division prior.
