## Style

She is a BJJ specialist: a former world No. 1 IBJJF competitor and an ADCC and no-gi world champion (Wikipedia). The dataset backs the label: she has five submission wins (armbar from mount three times, a kneebar from bottom guard and a rear-naked choke), and time-decayed control of 278 s per 15 min against a division mean of 185. On the feet she is a head-hunter: 86% of her attempts go to the head. She shoots often but rarely finishes the takedown cleanly. Her career takedown accuracy is 22%, so she reaches the mat through scrambles, clinches and opponents' mistakes as much as through wrestling.

## Strengths

- Submission threat: 1.01 attempts per 15 min career (division 0.40). Opponents who end up underneath her are in danger, as Ribas found with 4 seconds left.
- Long top-control spells in title fights: 10:48 vs Robertson and 13:12 vs Hill.
- Five-round experience: 6 fights scheduled for five rounds, and she won both title bouts.

## Weaknesses

- Below-division striking. She lands 3.38 significant strikes per minute (division 4.06) and was out-struck in all four decision losses (Ribas, Rodriguez, Yan and Lemos).
- Takedown entries: 18 of 82 career.
- Takedown defence: 41% career (division 64%). Jandiroba and Robertson both held her down.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** six knockdowns absorbed and one KO loss. That is 1.17 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed (division 0.33). This is the clearest exploitable hole.
- **Cardio:** her 5-round output by round is 1.00, 0.70, 1.22, 1.06 and 1.07 of round 1 (division 1.00, 0.88, 1.15, 1.21, 1.15). She finished strongly against Jandiroba and Ribas. She faded against Robertson in R5, but by being held down, not on volume.

## What the numbers say

Her Elo of 1657 is third among active strawweights, behind Zhang (1706) and Tatiana Suarez (1703). The engine should give her below-average striking volume and defence, a weak chin, low knockdown power (2 knockdowns, 0 KO wins), poor takedown accuracy and poor takedown defence. It should also give her an above-average control rate once a fight hits the mat and a high submission conversion. Expect grinding decisions and late submissions, with a real KO path for a hard-hitting striker.
