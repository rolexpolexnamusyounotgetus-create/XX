# Jiri Prochazka: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Czech, known as "BJP". Orthodox base with frequent stance switches, 6 ft 3 in, 80 in reach, born 1992-10-14 (age 34). UFC record 6-3 (ufcstats); pro record 32-6-1.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He won the light heavyweight belt from Glover Teixeira at UFC 275 (Jun 11, 2022, RNC R5 4:32) and vacated it in November 2022 with a shoulder injury. He then lost two title fights to Alex Pereira (UFC 295, UFC 303) and the vacant-title fight to Carlos Ulberg (UFC 327, Apr 11, 2026).
- **Next:** Navajo Stirling (11-0, six straight UFC wins, most recently a sub-three-minute stoppage of Jan Blachowicz in August) on Nov 21, 2026, headlining UFC Fight Night 294 at ABHA Arena, Doha, Qatar; five rounds.
- **Ranking:** #3 light heavyweight on the Oct 27, 2026 list as mirrored by ufcalendar.com and kalshi.com (not checked on ufc.com).
- **Injuries:** none current reported.

## Style breakdown

A wide, hands-low stance by design: he told Joe Rogan that hands down is "your natural posture." He switches stance and twitches with feints on the outside, then throws long punches from strange angles. In close he adds knees and elbows, and he has a spinning back elbow KO (Reyes). He relies on head movement, timing and reflexes rather than a guard.

## Strengths

- Volume and pressure: 9.9 significant attempts per minute decayed.
- Gets stronger as fights go on: in five-round fights his round-3 output was 2.24 times round 1, though rounds 3-5 of that curve come from the Teixeira fight alone. Both of his 2025 wins were third-round finishes of three-round fights.
- Varied finishing: 5 KO/TKO and 1 submission in the UFC.

## Weaknesses

- Defense: absorbs 5.7 significant strikes per minute decayed, 3.9 to the head; 4 knockdowns absorbed and 3 KO losses in 9 UFC fights.
- Over-commits when he senses a finish (UFC 327) and admits he was "too courageous" against Pereira.
- Disciplined kickers can win early rounds: Rountree's inside leg kicks disrupted his movement.
- Takedown defense 69% decayed; Teixeira controlled him for 587 s.

## Durability and cardio

Cardio is a strength: he went 25 minutes with Teixeira and finished in the last minute. Durability is now the question. All three KO losses came between November 2023 and April 2026.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums: 5.5 landed and 5.7 absorbed per minute, 56% accuracy. Of his attempts, 79% go to the head, 13% to the body and 8% to the legs. Elo 1642.
