# Brendan Allen ("All In")

As of Nov 6, 2026. Orthodox middleweight, age 30 (born Dec 28, 1995), 6'2" with a 75" reach. He is a BJJ black belt under Daniel Wanderley and a former LFA middleweight champion: he lost two LFA title fights (Eryk Anders, 2017; Anthony Hernandez, Jan 2018), won the vacant belt at LFA 50 (Sep 2018) and defended it once at LFA 61 (Feb 2019), so "two-time champion" was wrong (Wikipedia, record table). ESPN and Sherdog list his gym as Kill Cliff FC. Dataset: `data/derived/fighters.json`, snapshot of Oct 31, 2026. Re-checked on Nov 6, 2026 with direct page fetches (Sherdog, ESPN, CBS Sports, MMA Fighting, MiddleEasy, Wikipedia) and the ESPN/UFC studio photos.

- **Ranked #4** at middleweight on the UFC media rankings updated Nov 3, 2026 (CBS Sports; ufcalendar.com mirror) and on the UFC/Meta rankings (Oct 31).
- **Record:** 28-7 pro (ESPN, Sherdog, CBS, Wikipedia).

## Recent fights
- **W Duncan (RNC R5, Oct 2026):** Duncan was on a five-fight streak and outlanded Allen 106-51 in sig strikes, including 46 in R4. Duncan was also docked a point for an eye poke in R2. Allen kept going to body-lock takedowns (4 of 11, 8:39 control), took the back in R5 and finished.
- **W Edmen Shahbazyan (UD 30-27, 30-27, 29-28, Jun 2026):** a bloody fight that Allen led with kicks, right hands and clinch work. The sig count was 89-90; Shahbazyan stopped several takedowns and landed a late counter right.
- **W Reinier de Ridder (corner stoppage after R4, Oct 2025):** He stepped in on short notice and dominated on the mat with 11:39 of control and 19 ground sig strikes. De Ridder's corner stopped it after R4.
- **W Marvin Vettori (UD, Jul 2025).**
- **L Anthony Hernandez (UD 29-28 x3, Feb 2025):** Allen was out-grappled and controlled for 10:22.
- **L Nassourdine Imavov (UD 29-28 x3, Sep 2024):** He scored one early slam, then went 1 for 10 on takedowns and lost the striking 30-57.

## Style
A **submission grappler with pressure**. He forces clinches, wrestles along the fence with body locks, and moves to the back. Most of his submission wins are rear-naked chokes; Wikipedia credits him with the second-most submissions in UFC middleweight history (7). On top he is heavy and lands ground strikes, enough to break de Ridder. On the feet he walks forward behind punches and kicks, but his defense is loose.

**Strengths:** clinch entries, back takes, the RNC, top control, late-fight finishing, and the confidence to scramble off his back.
**Weaknesses:** He loses striking exchanges to good strikers. His takedowns can be stuffed by strong counter-wrestlers (Imavov). He can be out-wrestled and held down (Hernandez).

## Durability and cardio
He has two TKO losses, both early in his UFC run: Sean Strickland in 2020 and Chris Curtis (knees to the body) in 2021. He has two UFC knockdowns absorbed and has not been stopped since 2021. His cardio holds up over five rounds: he finished Duncan in R5 after taking heavy R4 volume, while on antibiotics. Pace in his four five-round fights is uneven (R5 0.78x R1).
