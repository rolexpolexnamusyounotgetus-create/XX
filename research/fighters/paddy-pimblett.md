# Paddy Pimblett: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Orthodox, 5'10", 73-inch reach, born 1995-01-03. From Liverpool. 24-4 pro, 8-1 in the UFC.*

## Current status
He is **#5 at lightweight** in the UFC media rankings (Nov 3, 2026) and has **no fight booked**. On **Jul 11, 2026** at UFC 329 he submitted Benoit Saint Denis in 52 seconds. In late **October 2026** he said he had stem-cell injections in his **left hand**, which keep him out of hand-heavy training, and that he is aiming for early 2027. He says he would rather have a title rematch with **Justin Gaethje**, who beat Ilia Topuria for the undisputed title on Jun 14, 2026. **Topuria** said on Nov 5, 2026 that he wants Pimblett for his return in April 2027. Pimblett also says he suffered a scratched cornea in the Gaethje loss.

## Recent fights
- **W Saint Denis, UFC 329 (Jul 2026), D'Arce choke in round 1 at 0:52.** He blocked a high kick and sprawled on the takedown that followed. He then locked a front-headlock choke he described as part D'Arce and part Peruvian necktie, sat to his hip, and Saint Denis went unconscious rather than tap.
- **L Gaethje, UFC 324 (Jan 24, 2026), unanimous decision for the interim title (46-49, 46-49, 47-48).** Gaethje dropped him twice and went 3 for 3 on takedowns. He outlanded Gaethje 156-144 in significant strikes, but by UFC Stats 83 of those went to the head against 120 of Gaethje's.
- **W Michael Chandler, UFC 314 (Apr 2025), TKO in round 3 by elbows from mount.** He landed 80 strikes to 11 after surviving Chandler's early takedowns; a knee opened a cut along the way.
- **W King Green, UFC 304 (Jul 2024), triangle from bottom guard.**
- **W Tony Ferguson (Dec 2023) and Jared Gordon (Dec 2022), decisions.**

## Style
He is a forward-moving, high-volume puncher who will brawl, and an excellent submission grappler in scrambles. He throws jab-cross combinations in volume, kicks the legs more than most lightweights (19-21% of his landed strikes vs a median of about 15%), and opens fights with low kicks. His body share is about average, though he landed 48 body strikes on Gaethje. His finishes come from the back (RNC), from guard (triangle), from the front headlock and from mount (elbows).

## Strengths
- **Output:** 5.5-5.8 significant strikes landed per minute (median 4.1).
- **Submissions:** 1.36 attempts per 15 minutes (median about 0.28). A failed takedown against him is dangerous for the opponent.
- **Chin and recovery:** never stopped in the UFC; he survived two Gaethje knockdowns and finished all five rounds.

## Weaknesses
- **Defence:** about 43% significant-strike defence (median about 54%).
- **Wrestling defence:** 46% career and 42% recent. Gordon, Chandler and Gaethje all took him down, and opponents control him more than he controls them (24-27% against 19-21%).
- **Takedown accuracy:** 21% career, 0 for 5 against Gaethje.
- The left hand is a current concern.

## Durability and cardio
He has 0 KO losses and 2 knockdowns absorbed in 9 UFC fights. In two five-round fights his significant-strike rate by round, relative to round 1, ran 1.00 / 1.06 / 1.65 / 1.32 / 0.79: he picks up in the middle rounds and dips in the fifth, on a small sample.

## What the numbers say
His Elo is 1690 (9th among active lightweights). The profile is high volume with average accuracy (53%) and poor defence, a strong submission rate and weak wrestling defence. In the simulator, his best paths should be chokes in scrambles and late volume. His worst matchups are clean, powerful strikers (the Gaethje knockdowns) and patient control wrestlers who avoid giving up the front headlock.
