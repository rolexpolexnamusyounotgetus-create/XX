## Style

He is a southpaw counter-striker with one-punch power: 13 knockdowns in 15 UFC fights, 3.1 per 100 head strikes landed, about 3.5 times the lightweight rate. Eight of his ten UFC wins are KO/TKO, and five came in round 1. The knockout of Jose Aldo took 13 seconds. The left hand, often thrown as a counter, is his main weapon (*recalled*). A 74-inch reach on a 5 ft 9 in frame backs it up. He hit the body hard at featherweight (15 of 30 vs Mendes) and kicked Diaz's lead leg in the rematch (40 of 45 leg strikes).

## Strengths

- Knockdown power and head accuracy: 44% on head strikes career (league 39%).
- Durability on the feet: one knockdown absorbed in 15 UFC fights.

## Weaknesses

- Wrestling: opponents average 250 s of control per 15 min against him (league 171). Khabib and Mendes both held him down for long spells.
- Calf kicks: Poirier landed 18 leg strikes in their second fight.
- Submitted twice by rear-naked choke (Diaz R2 2016, Khabib R4 2018).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** his chin has held up (0.27 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed), but his legs have not.
- **Cardio:** a long-standing question. Diaz submitted him in R2 (UFC 196) after he slowed (*recalled*), and Khabib finished him in R4. His 5-round pace numbers (1.00, 1.10, 1.19, 1.28, 0.95 of round 1) beyond R2 rest only on Diaz II and Khabib.

## What the numbers say

His career numbers (5.27 landed per minute, 49.5% accuracy) date from 2013-2021; his time-decayed sums hold only 5.8 minutes. His Elo of 1668 is 15th among active welterweights. fighters.json files him at welterweight (UFC 329), though most of his career was at 145 and 155. The engine should give him elite early knockdown power, average takedown defence with long control spells once he is taken down, and a heavy discount for inactivity, age and injuries.
