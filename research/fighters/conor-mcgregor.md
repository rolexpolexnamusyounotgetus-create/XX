# Conor McGregor ("The Notorious"): scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats: `data/derived/fighters.json` and `data/raw/` (ufcstats snapshot to Oct 31, 2026). Status, record, ranking and appearance were re-checked on the web on Nov 6, 2026 (sources at the end). *Recalled* marks the few style details that are still unchecked.

## Current status

- **Last fight:** TKO loss to Max Holloway at 1:09 of R1, UFC 329 (Jul 11, 2026, Las Vegas, filed at welterweight). He tore the ACL and meniscus in his right knee landing from a jumping kick in the first exchange, and he landed 0 of 6. He says it is the same injury he had in the first Holloway fight in 2013, on the other leg ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/conor-mcgregor-confirms-injury-diagnosis-161428138.html)).
- **Recovery:** knee surgery in early August 2026 (statement Aug 7, [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/sports/conor-mcgregor-surgery-torn-acl-vows-comeback-knee-repaired-thank-you-god)). Dana White expects him out until about July 2027 ([The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/ufc/conor-mcgregor-next-fight-injury-record-2027-b3022382.html)).
- **Plans:** on Nov 5, 2026 he said he has one fight left on his UFC contract, that it will be his last UFC fight, and that he will then move to bare-knuckle fighting rather than retire; he bought a stake in BKFC in 2024 ([Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Conor-McGregor-plans-bareknuckle-move-after-final-UFC-contract-fight-203507)). No opponent or date is booked ([UFCalendar](https://www.ufcalendar.com/fighters/conor-mcgregor)); he and Holloway have both talked about a third fight.
- **Layoff before UFC 329:** five years, after a doctor's stoppage at the end of R1 vs Poirier at UFC 264 (Jul 10, 2021), when he rolled his ankle and broke his lower tibia (Wikipedia). An 18-month suspension for three missed anti-doping whereabouts filings in 2024 ran until March 2026 (Wikipedia).
- **Titles and ranking:** none now; unranked (not in the welterweight or lightweight top 15 of Nov 3, 2026). He won the featherweight title (UFC 194, Dec 12, 2015) and the lightweight title (UFC 205, Nov 12, 2016).
- **Record:** 22-7 pro (Wikipedia, UFCalendar); 10-5 in the UFC. He is 38.

## Style

He is a southpaw counter-striker with one-punch power: 13 knockdowns in 15 UFC fights, 3.1 per 100 head strikes landed, about 3.5 times the lightweight rate. Eight of his ten UFC wins are KO/TKO, and five came in round 1. The knockout of Jose Aldo took 13 seconds. The left hand is his main weapon: Wikipedia gives the Aldo knockout as a left hook (that he often throws it as a counter is *recalled*). A 74-inch reach on a 5 ft 9 in frame backs it up. He hit the body hard at featherweight (15 of 30 vs Mendes) and kicked Diaz's lead leg in the rematch (40 of 45 leg strikes).

## Strengths

- Knockdown power and head accuracy: 44% on head strikes career (league 39%).
- Durability on the feet: one knockdown absorbed in 15 UFC fights.

## Weaknesses

- Wrestling: opponents average 250 s of control per 15 min against him (league 171). Khabib and Mendes both held him down for long spells.
- Calf kicks: Poirier landed 18 leg strikes in their second fight.
- Submitted twice by rear-naked choke (Diaz R2 2016, Khabib R4 2018).

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** his chin has held up (0.27 knockdowns per 100 head strikes absorbed), but his legs have not: a torn ACL in 2013, a broken tibia in 2021, and a torn ACL and meniscus in 2026.
- **Cardio:** a long-standing question. Diaz submitted him in R2 (UFC 196) after he slowed (*recalled*), and Khabib finished him in R4. His 5-round pace numbers (1.00, 1.10, 1.19, 1.28, 0.95 of round 1) beyond R2 rest only on Diaz II and Khabib.

## What the numbers say

His career numbers (5.27 landed per minute, 49.5% accuracy) date from 2013-2021; his time-decayed sums hold only 5.8 minutes. His Elo of 1668 is 15th among active welterweights. fighters.json files him at welterweight (UFC 329), though most of his career was at 145 and 155. The engine should give him elite early knockdown power, average takedown defence with long control spells once he is taken down, and a heavy discount for inactivity, age and injuries.

## Appearance

Checked against ESPN studio photos (headshot and cutout dated Jul 9, 2026; cutout dated Jul 8, 2021) and ESPN's lead photo from UFC 329. The McGREGOR lettering reads correctly, so left and right are his own. No likeness sheet exists for him.

- **Hair:** short and brown, faded at the sides and longer on top, swept back in the Jul 2026 headshot. At UFC 329 the sides were clipped close under a short spiky top. He is receding at the temples.
- **Facial hair:** a full beard of medium length, reddish brown with lighter ginger tones.
- **Skin:** light.
- **Tattoos:** a large chest piece of a crowned gorilla eating a heart (black and grey with red, with spiky black rays out to both shoulders); a crown at the base of the throat; McGREGOR in large capitals across the upper abdomen, with a tiger below it on the stomach; a dagger through a heart, other traditional-style pieces and script on the left forearm. The back was not visible.
- **Kit:** custom green UFC Venum shorts, a long compression cut with a Celtic-knot print and Irish-tricolour stripes on one leg (Jul 2026, worn at UFC 329). In 2021 he wore green Venum shorts with CONOR on the leg.
- **Build:** 5 ft 9 in with a 74 in reach (ufcstats). Lean and muscular in Jul 2026.
