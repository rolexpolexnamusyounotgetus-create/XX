# Tom Aspinall: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 6 ft 5 in, 78 in reach, listed 256 lb, born 1993-04-11 (age 33). UFC record 8-1, 1 NC (ufcstats); pro record 15-3 (1 NC) (ESPN, Wikipedia).

**Re-check, Nov 6, 2026:** status, ranking, record, injuries and appearance were re-verified on the open web (sources at the end). Rankings come from ufcalendar.com's mirror because ufc.com returned 403.


## Current status

- **Title:** none since Sep 14, 2026. He won the interim belt by beating Pavlovich (UFC 295, Nov 11, 2023), defended it against Blaydes (UFC 304, Jul 27, 2024), and was promoted to undisputed champion when Jon Jones's retirement was announced on Jun 21, 2025.
- **Last fight:** UFC 321, Oct 25, 2025, Abu Dhabi, against Ciryl Gane. The fight was ruled a no contest at 4:35 of round 1 after Gane poked him in both eyes (ufcstats: "Eye poke by Gane"). Aspinall was criticised for not continuing, and Alexander Volkanovski publicly defended him in Feb 2026 (Bloody Elbow).
- **Eye injuries:** he announced on Dec 29, 2025 that he would have multiple eye surgeries (ESPN). By September 2026 he had agreed a fight date and was back sparring when "an accident caused further damage to my right eye". He wrote that "ongoing complications with my eyes mean I cannot get medically cleared to fight right now. I've had multiple surgeries, accidents, and infections," and vacated the title on Sep 14, 2026, adding "I'll be back as soon as I'm medically able to be" (Sherdog; Sky Sports; Heavy).
- **Since then:** Gane was made undisputed champion and defends against Josh Hokit at UFC 334 (Nov 14, 2026). Aspinall posted training photos on Oct 30, 2026, but no return date or medical clearance has been announced (DAZN, Nov 5, 2026).
- **Ranking:** #1 heavyweight in both the Meta UFC rankings (Oct 31, 2026) and the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026), and #8 in the media pound-for-pound list (Nov 3), per ufcalendar.com.
- **Next fight:** none. **Injuries:** the eye injuries above, and a knee injury in 2022.

## Style breakdown

A grappler who also boxes very well. His hand speed at heavyweight is his calling card: he finishes with sharp single punches as opponents close the distance, then follows them to the mat. Off his back or on top he is a real submission threat (armbar on Volkov, rear-naked choke on Arlovski). He has the footwork to stay at range, and he used leg and body kicks early against Gane.

## Strengths

- One-shot power: 4.2 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed.
- Seven of eight UFC wins came in round 1.
- Finishes on the ground and from submissions as well as on the feet.
- Fast starter; no knockdowns absorbed.

## Weaknesses

- Untested late: only 22.9 UFC minutes in total, and he has never fought past round 2.
- Injury history: a knee injury in 2022 and the 2025 eye injury.
- More than a year out of action, with multiple eye surgeries and no medical clearance as of Nov 2026.

## Durability and cardio

No knockdowns absorbed. Cardio is unknown, because he has not had to show it in the UFC. Inputs for rounds 3 to 5 should come from the division prior.

## What the numbers say

Career: 7.6 significant strikes landed per minute at 68% accuracy, and he absorbs 3.6 per minute. Of his attempts, 77% go to the head, 10% to the body and 13% to the legs. On the ground: 4 of 5 takedowns and about 200 s of control per 15 min. With so few minutes, and the decayed numbers dominated by the short Gane fight, every rate should be shrunk toward the heavyweight prior. Elo 1711.
