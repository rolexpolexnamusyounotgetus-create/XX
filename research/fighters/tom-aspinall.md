# Tom Aspinall: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 6 ft 5 in, 78 in reach, listed 256 lb, born 1993-04-11 (age 33). UFC record 8-1, 1 NC (ufcstats).

## Current status

- **Title:** UFC heavyweight champion. He won the interim belt by beating Pavlovich (UFC 295, Nov 11, 2023), defended it against Blaydes (UFC 304, Jul 27, 2024), and was promoted to undisputed champion when Jon Jones's retirement was announced in June 2025.
- **Last fight:** UFC 321, Oct 25, 2025, Abu Dhabi, against Ciryl Gane. The fight was ruled a no contest at 4:35 of round 1 after an eye poke by Gane (ufcstats: "Eye poke by Gane"). Aspinall was criticised for not continuing, and Alexander Volkanovski publicly defended him in Feb 2026 (Bloody Elbow).
- **Since then:** Gane won an interim title against Alex Pereira at UFC Freedom 250 (Jun 14, 2026) and was booked against Josh Hokit (ESPN, Oct 2026), so Aspinall still holds the undisputed belt but had no confirmed return date as of October.
- **Injuries:** the eye injury from UFC 321 (treatment details not verified), and a knee injury in 2022.
- **Next fight:** none found.

## Style breakdown

A grappler who also boxes very well. His hand speed at heavyweight is his calling card: he finishes with sharp single punches as opponents close the distance, then follows them to the mat. Off his back or on top he is a real submission threat (armbar on Volkov, rear-naked choke on Arlovski). He has the footwork to stay at range, and he used leg and body kicks early against Gane.

## Strengths

- One-shot power: 4.2 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed.
- Seven of eight UFC wins came in round 1.
- Finishes on the ground and from submissions as well as on the feet.
- Fast starter; no knockdowns absorbed.

## Weaknesses

- Untested late: only 22.9 UFC minutes in total, and he has never fought past round 2.
- Injury history: a knee injury in 2022 and the 2025 eye injury.
- More than a year out of action.

## Durability and cardio

No knockdowns absorbed. Cardio is unknown, because he has not had to show it in the UFC. Inputs for rounds 3 to 5 should come from the division prior.
