# Jorge Masvidal: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Gamebred". Orthodox, 5 ft 11 in, 74-inch reach, born 1984-11-12 (age 41). UFC record 12-10 in fighters.json (6 KO/TKO, 1 submission, 5 decisions).*

**Research note:** UFC facts: UFC Stats snapshot of 2026-10-31. Status, record and appearance were re-checked on the web on 2026-11-06 (Wikipedia, Sherdog, UFCalendar, news reports; sources at the end).

## Current status
**Retired from MMA.** He announced his retirement in the cage after losing a unanimous decision to Gilbert Burns at UFC 287 in Miami on **Apr 8, 2023** (27-30, 27-30, 28-29). On **Jul 6, 2024** he lost a boxing match to Nate Diaz by majority decision, and in **March 2026** he pulled out, for undisclosed reasons, of a submission-grappling match with Sharabutdin Magomedov at Hype Fighting Brazil (Wikipedia). He founded the Gamebred Fighting Championship bare-knuckle MMA promotion in 2021. Sherdog, Wikipedia and UFCalendar show no MMA bout since UFC 287; pro record 35-17. In **October 2026** he said he wants to fight in the UFC again and is talking to the promotion, hinting at next year ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/jorge-masvidal-provides-latest-ufc-202205569.html), [MMA Sucka](https://mmasucka.com/news/next-year-jorge-masvidal-teases-ufc-return-after-revealing-white-house-card-fallout/)), but no MMA bout is booked as of Nov 6, 2026. On **Nov 2, 2026** ALF League announced him against **Khamzat Chimaev in a submission-only grappling match** headlining its first event on **Dec 4, 2026** at The Agenda in Dubai ([MMA Fighting](https://www.mmafighting.com/ufc/519052/khamzat-chimaev-faces-jorge-masvidal-in-submission-only-grappling-match-for-alf-league)). Sherdog notes that the announcement post was later taken down ([Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Khamzat-Chimaev-vs-Jorge-Masvidal-grappling-match-reportedly-set-203461)). It is not an MMA fight. No title, ranking or MMA booking.

## Recent UFC fights
- **L Gilbert Burns, UFC 287 (Apr 2023), decision.** Burns took him down 4 times in 6 attempts and controlled him for 5:41. His best work: leg kicks (17 of 19).
- **L Colby Covington, UFC 272 (Mar 2022), decision.** He was controlled for 16:14. He landed 31 significant strikes in round 2 and 1 in round 5.
- **L Kamaru Usman, UFC 261 (Apr 2021), KO R2 1:02, title fight.** A right hand at distance.
- **L Kamaru Usman, UFC 251 (Jul 2020), decision, title fight.** Usman held him for 16:38 and landed 49 body strikes.
- **W Nate Diaz, UFC 244 (Nov 2019), doctor stoppage after R3.** He scored two knockdowns and landed 112-43, with 40 ground strikes.
- **W Ben Askren, UFC 239 (Jul 2019), flying knee at 0:05 of R1.** Widely cited as the fastest KO in UFC history.

## Style
He is an orthodox boxer-striker with power and a broad toolkit. His jab, cross and hook do most of the work at distance (69% of his landed significant strikes), and he throws body punches and body kicks. Late in his career he also used low kicks. In the clinch he uses elbows and knees: he knocked out Cezar Ferreira with a clinch elbow in 2015. He counters well and finishes hurt opponents on the ground.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 0.46 knockdowns per 15 minutes (welterweight median 0.35). He has KO wins with a punch (Till), a flying knee (Askren), body punches (Cerrone) and an elbow (Ferreira).
- **Body work:** 24% of his landed significant strikes go to the body (league about 21%).
- **Striking defence:** he absorbs 3.07 significant strikes per minute (median 3.69). His takedown defence is 70% (median 66%).

## Weaknesses
- **Elite wrestlers:** in his last three fights, Usman, Covington and Burns held him down for long stretches. Opponents controlled him for 41% of his recency-weighted minutes.
- **Late-fight output:** his five-round pace drops to 0.61 in round 4 and 0.23 in round 5. Against Usman he landed 24, 20, 12, 6 and 4 by round.
- **Close decisions:** 9 of his 10 UFC losses went to the judges, including four split decisions (Iaquinta, Henderson, Larkin, Maia).

## Durability and cardio
One KO loss by strikes (Usman) in 22 UFC fights and 6 knockdowns absorbed in 294 minutes: durable. He fades in five-round fights, partly from being held down.
