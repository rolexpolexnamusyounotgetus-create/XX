## Current status
**Retired from MMA.** He announced his retirement in the cage after losing a unanimous decision to Gilbert Burns at UFC 287 in Miami on **Apr 8, 2023** (27-30, 27-30, 28-29). On **Jul 6, 2024** he lost a majority decision to Nate Diaz in a boxing match. He founded the Gamebred bare-knuckle MMA promotion (widely reported; not re-opened). Sherdog's record, fetched Nov 6, 2026, shows no MMA bout since UFC 287 (pro record 35-17). On **Nov 4, 2026** Sherdog reported that he is scheduled to face **Khamzat Chimaev in a submission-only grappling match on Dec 4, 2026** at The Agenda in Dubai. That is not an MMA fight. He has no title, no ranking and no MMA bout booked.

## Recent UFC fights
- **L Gilbert Burns, UFC 287 (Apr 2023), decision.** Burns took him down 4 times in 6 attempts and controlled him for 5:41. His best work was leg kicks (17 of 19).
- **L Colby Covington, UFC 272 (Mar 2022), decision.** He was controlled for 16:14. He landed 31 significant strikes in round 2 and 1 in round 5.
- **L Kamaru Usman, UFC 261 (Apr 2021), KO R2 1:02, title fight.** He was knocked out by a right hand at distance.
- **L Kamaru Usman, UFC 251 (Jul 2020), decision, title fight.** Usman held him for 16:38 and landed 49 body strikes.
- **W Nate Diaz, UFC 244 (Nov 2019), doctor stoppage after R3.** He scored two knockdowns and landed 112-43, with 40 ground strikes.
- **W Ben Askren, UFC 239 (Jul 2019), flying knee at 0:05 of R1.** This is widely cited as the fastest KO in UFC history.

## Style
He is an orthodox boxer-striker with power and a broad toolkit. His jab, cross and hook do most of the work at distance (69% of his landed significant strikes), and he throws body punches and body kicks. Late in his career he also used low kicks. In the clinch he uses elbows and knees: he knocked out Cezar Ferreira with a clinch elbow in 2015. He counters well and finishes hurt opponents on the ground.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 0.46 knockdowns per 15 minutes (welterweight median 0.35). He has KO wins with a punch (Till), a flying knee (Askren), body punches (Cerrone) and an elbow (Ferreira).
- **Body work:** 24% of his landed significant strikes go to the body (league about 21%).
- **Striking defence:** he absorbs 3.07 significant strikes per minute (median 3.69). His takedown defence is 70% (median 66%).

## Weaknesses
- **Elite wrestlers:** in his last three fights, Usman, Covington and Burns held him down for long stretches. Opponents controlled him for 41% of his recency-weighted minutes.
- **Late-fight output:** his five-round pace drops to 0.61 in round 4 and 0.23 in round 5. Against Usman he landed 24, 20, 12, 6 and 4 by round.
- **Close decisions:** 9 of his 10 UFC losses went to the judges, including four split decisions (Iaquinta, Henderson, Larkin, Maia).

## Durability and cardio
He has one KO loss by strikes (Usman) in 22 UFC fights and absorbed 6 knockdowns in 294 minutes, so he is durable. He fades in five-round fights. Part of that fade comes from being held down.
