# Jamahal Hill: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Sweet Dreams". Southpaw, 6 ft 4 in, 79 in reach, born 1991-05-19 (age 35). UFC record 6-4 with 1 no contest (ufcstats); pro record 12-4 (1 NC).

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He won the vacant light heavyweight title over Glover Teixeira at UFC 283 (Jan 21, 2023) and vacated it on Jul 13, 2023 after rupturing his Achilles tendon. MMA Fighting, as cited by Fightful, reported the injury came in a pickup basketball game during International Fight Week.
- **Form:** three straight losses since his return (Pereira, Prochazka, Rountree). Last fight Jun 21, 2025.
- **Next:** none booked. In June 2026 he announced on his *What You Think?* podcast that he is moving to heavyweight, citing a hard weight cut. He named Josh Hokit for his debut and Sergei Pavlovich as a fallback. Hokit has since been booked against interim heavyweight champion Ciryl Gane (per ESPN, Oct 2026), and no Hill bout had been announced in the sources found.
- **Ranking:** none found.

## Style breakdown

A long southpaw who works at range: 90% of his decayed attempts come at distance, led by the jab and straight left, with punches to the body (23% of career landed strikes). He throws a lot, 11.7 significant attempts per minute over his career. He almost never kicks (6% of attempts to the legs) and has never attempted a UFC takedown. When he hurts someone he follows them down with ground strikes and elbows (Santos).

## Strengths

- Five-round volume and range control at his best (UFC 283).
- Body punching.
- Defended 15 of 17 takedowns from Teixeira and 14 of 20 from Santos.

## Weaknesses

- Durability: 2.3 knockdowns absorbed per 100 head strikes taken (decayed), against a light heavyweight league rate of 1.3; three KO/TKO losses.
- Kicks: Rountree took his legs away.
- Opponents land 55-57% of their significant strikes on him (league 50%).
- Low power per strike: 0.44 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed (decayed).

## Durability and cardio

Cardio over five rounds was strong against Teixeira: he landed 46, 51, 44, 79 and 12 significant strikes by round. Against Rountree he picked up in round 5 (26 landed after 6 and 8 in rounds 3-4). Durability and mobility are the concerns since the Achilles: after Baku he said an Achilles tear is a career-ender for an athlete.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums: 5.1 landed and 4.5 absorbed per minute, 49% accuracy. Of his attempts, 81% go to the head, 13% to the body and 6% to the legs. Takedown defense is 76% decayed. Elo 1578, the lowest of these five. Every stat comes from 205 lb fights; a heavyweight debut after 17+ months out has no data behind it.
