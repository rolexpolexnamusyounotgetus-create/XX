# Jamahal Hill: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "Sweet Dreams". Southpaw, 6 ft 4 in, 79 in reach, born 1991-05-19 (age 35). UFC record 6-4 with 1 no contest (ufcstats); pro record 12-4 (1 NC) (ESPN, Wikipedia).

**Re-check, Nov 6, 2026:** status, ranking, record, injuries and appearance were re-verified on the open web (sources at the end). Rankings come from ufcalendar.com's mirror because ufc.com returned 403.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He won the vacant light heavyweight title over Glover Teixeira at UFC 283 (Jan 21, 2023) and vacated it on Jul 13, 2023 after rupturing his Achilles tendon. MMA Fighting, as cited by Fightful, reported the injury came in a pickup basketball game during International Fight Week.
- **Form:** three straight losses since his return (Pereira, Prochazka, Rountree). Last fight Jun 21, 2025, so more than 16 months out.
- **Injuries:** in October 2025 he had knee surgery, a cartilage transplant: "the cartilage in my knee was basically non-existent. There was some pretty bad tears in there." He said the knee had stopped him "hopping, bouncing, or really throwing kicks" in his last fights and that he was "looking at [returning] maybe end of next year" (Heavy, Oct 25, 2025; MMA Junkie headline, Oct 28, 2025). No medical clearance has been reported.
- **Next:** none booked. In June 2026 he announced on his *What You Think?* podcast that he is moving to heavyweight, citing a hard weight cut. In August he said he wants a top-five heavyweight and "Josh Hokit first fight" (Sherdog, Aug 12, 2026). Hokit is instead fighting Ciryl Gane for the heavyweight title at UFC 334 on Nov 14. Hill is on no UFC card in the ESPN schedule through Feb 2027, and MMA Sucka (Oct 22, 2026) noted there is still no date for his heavyweight debut.
- **Ranking:** unranked. He is not in the UFC light heavyweight top 15 of either the Meta rankings (Oct 31, 2026) or the media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026), per ufcalendar.com, and Sherdog dropped him from its light heavyweight top 15 for inactivity on Nov 2, 2026. He has no heavyweight ranking.

## Style breakdown

A long southpaw who works at range: 90% of his decayed attempts come at distance, led by the jab and straight left, with punches to the body (23% of career landed strikes). He throws a lot, 11.7 significant attempts per minute over his career. He almost never kicks (6% of attempts to the legs) and has never attempted a UFC takedown. When he hurts someone he follows them down with ground strikes and elbows (Santos).

## Strengths

- Five-round volume and range control at his best (UFC 283).
- Body punching.
- Defended 15 of 17 takedowns from Teixeira and 14 of 20 from Santos.

## Weaknesses

- Durability: 2.3 knockdowns absorbed per 100 head strikes taken (decayed), against a light heavyweight league rate of 1.3; three KO/TKO losses.
- Kicks: Rountree took his legs away.
- Opponents land 55-57% of their significant strikes on him (league 50%).
- Low power per strike: 0.44 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed (decayed).

## Durability and cardio

Cardio over five rounds was strong against Teixeira: he landed 46, 51, 44, 79 and 12 significant strikes by round. Against Rountree he picked up in round 5 (26 landed after 6 and 8 in rounds 3-4). Durability and mobility are the concerns since the Achilles: after Baku he said an Achilles tear is a career-ender for an athlete, and in Oct 2025 he needed a knee cartilage transplant.

## What the numbers say

Decayed sums: 5.1 landed and 4.5 absorbed per minute, 49% accuracy. Of his attempts, 81% go to the head, 13% to the body and 6% to the legs. Takedown defense is 76% decayed. Elo 1578, the lowest of these five. Every stat comes from 205 lb fights; a heavyweight debut after 17+ months out and knee surgery has no data behind it.

## Appearance

- **Hair:** short, close-cropped black hair (ESPN, Jan and Jun 2025).
- **Facial hair:** thin moustache and a short chin beard or goatee with light stubble on the jaw. Not a full beard.
- **Skin:** dark.
- **Tattoos:** "Northside" in script across the upper chest between the collarbones, flanked by a thumbs-up hand on each side of the chest; numerals ("22" or "122") on the outer right upper arm; small script on the left shoulder; a small tattoo on top of one foot (ESPN full-body cutouts, Jan and Jun 2025).
- **Build now:** long and lanky in the 2025 cutouts, a little soft through the midsection; 2026 heavyweight-move footage shows him much heavier (MMA Fighting headline, Jul 30, 2026). No 2026 studio photo was found.
- **Kit:** black UFC shorts with a grey-and-white slash pattern, as fight shorts (Jun 2025) and compression shorts with a Venum logo (Jan 2025). The sim uses corner colour.
