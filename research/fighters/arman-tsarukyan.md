## Style

He is a wrestle-boxer. He shoots 9.4 times per 15 minutes (league 4.3) and keeps re-shooting after misses: 7 of 21 against Ismagulov, 1 of 10 against Ruffy. Once on top he stays there. Control runs 387 seconds per 15 minutes career and 541 over the last three years (league 174). Ground strikes are 31% of what he lands career and 44% lately. Standing, he throws mostly to the head (79% of attempts) with few kicks, and he has real power. He has 4 knockdowns in 13 fights, and his decayed rate is 1.63 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed (league 0.95).

**Strengths:** relentless takedown volume, heavy top control, ground strikes, first-round power, and a very low absorption rate (1.77 significant strikes per minute career).

**Weaknesses:** low takedown accuracy (35% career, 28% over three years). Opponents throw a lot of submissions at him: Oliveira 4, Hooker 2, a 3-year rate of 3.0 per 15 minutes on a small sample. His only losses came against grapplers who matched his wrestling.

## Durability and cardio

He has never been knocked down or finished in 13 UFC fights. Opponents land 1.2 significant strikes per minute on him over three years. That chin sample is small (168 head strikes absorbed), so the engine should shrink it toward the league. Cardio evidence is thin. His one 25-minute fight (Gamrot) shows output rising to 1.53 times round 1 in round 5, but he lost it.
