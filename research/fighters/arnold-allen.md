## Style

Allen is a southpaw boxer. He works at range (89% of his landed significant strikes come at distance) and aims high (82% of attempts at the head, league 76%). He rarely clinches (3% of landed strikes). He has two guillotine wins, Omer (2015, on the ground) and Burnell (2018, standing). Lately he has added top position: against Costa he took him down at will in R5 (4 of 4, 4:03 of control).

**Strengths:** durability; takedown defense (73% career); accuracy over the last three years (52%, league 47%); takedowns when he chooses (84% accuracy decayed).

**Weaknesses:** his output is below the division average (3.52 landed per minute career, league 4.12), and high-volume strikers have out-landed him (Holloway, Silva). He absorbs leg kicks (0.84 per minute decayed, league 0.66). All three losses went the distance; the Evloev and Silva cards were close. He has also had long layoffs.

## Durability and cardio

In 15 UFC fights he has never been knocked down or stopped, after absorbing 378 head strikes. His power is modest: 4 knockdowns, 0.71 per 100 head strikes landed (league 0.94).
