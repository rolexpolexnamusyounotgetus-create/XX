# Dustin Poirier: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Southpaw, 5'9", 72-inch reach, born 1989-01-19. UFC record 22-9 (1 NC) in fighters.json; 30-10 (1 NC) as a pro.*

## Current status
**Retired.** The former interim UFC lightweight champion retired after his trilogy fight with Max Holloway at UFC 318 in Louisiana on **Jul 19, 2025**, which he lost by unanimous decision (46-49, 46-49, 47-48). In **May 2026** he said on a podcast that he would consider a return for a Diaz fight. A French report on **Jul 22, 2026** was headlined with a "95%" remark about a possible return (this check could not reopen the article to confirm which way he leaned), and said he and Justin Gaethje had agreed not to fight a trilogy. In **June 2026** he was arrested at Atlanta's airport (CBS News Atlanta, Fox 8) and afterwards said publicly that he needed help. He has no fight booked and no ranking.

## Recent fights
- **L Holloway, UFC 318 (Jul 2025), decision.** Holloway outlanded him 198-109, including 64 body strikes. Each man scored one knockdown.
- **L Islam Makhachev, UFC 302 (Jun 2024), D'Arce choke in round 5.** He had moments on the feet, but Makhachev controlled him for 10:23.
- **W Benoit Saint Denis, UFC 299 (Mar 2024), KO in round 2.** He lost round 1 (12-38 in significant strikes, 3:20 controlled) and still threatened four submissions. He then landed 16 of 21 strikes in round 2 and finished it with punches.
- **L Justin Gaethje, UFC 291 (Jul 2023), head-kick KO in round 2.**
- **W Michael Chandler, UFC 281 (Nov 2022), rear-naked choke in round 3.**
- **L Charles Oliveira, UFC 269 (Dec 2021), rear-naked choke in round 3, for the title.**

## Style
He is a southpaw boxer-puncher. A right jab sets up the left straight, he stays in the pocket for hooks and uppercuts, and he will trade in wild exchanges. 80% of his landed significant strikes go to the head (lightweight median 62%). His leg kicks are mostly calf kicks, used famously against McGregor at UFC 257 (18 leg strikes landed). Off his back foot he grabs guillotines and front headlocks whenever opponents shoot.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 0.65 knockdowns per 15 minutes over his career, about twice the lightweight median; 11 KO/TKO wins in the UFC.
- **Real submission threat:** 1.08 attempts per 15 minutes (median about 0.28) and finishes over elite opponents.
- **Five-round experience:** 15 five-round UFC fights.

## Weaknesses
- He lost to the elite grapplers and wrestlers of his era (Oliveira, Makhachev) and to Gaethje's kicks.
- Late in his career he took heavy damage: 5.9 significant strikes absorbed per minute recency-weighted (median about 4.1) and striking defence near 47%.
- His takedown defence is a middling 63%.

## Durability and cardio
He has 3 KO/TKO losses in 32 UFC fights and absorbed 4 knockdowns over 348 UFC minutes (0.17 per 15 minutes, below the median of about 0.24). He went five rounds with Holloway at 36. His output holds late: his pace by round, relative to round 1, is 1.00 / 1.04 / 0.97 / 1.11 / 1.09.

## What the numbers say
The career (C) sums describe a high-output, high-power pocket boxer: 5.3 significant strikes landed per minute at 50% accuracy, with an Elo of 1715 (7th among recently active lightweights). The recency-weighted (S) sums describe a declining version: output down to 4.5 per minute and absorption up to 5.9. Simulations of a "prime" Poirier should use C. Simulations of his last form should use S.
