# Dustin Poirier: scouting dossier

*As of 2026-11-06. Southpaw, 5'9", 72-inch reach, born 1989-01-19. UFC record 22-9 (1 NC) in fighters.json; 30-10 (1 NC) as a pro (Wikipedia; ESPN shows 30-10 without the no-contest).*

## Current status
**Retired** (re-checked on the web Nov 6, 2026). The former interim UFC lightweight champion retired after his trilogy fight with Max Holloway at UFC 318 in Louisiana on **Jul 19, 2025**, which he lost by unanimous decision (46-49, 46-49, 47-48). Wikipedia now describes him as a former fighter who competed from 2009 to 2025, and Sherdog called him "retired" on Nov 4, 2026. In **May 2026** he said on a podcast that he would consider a return for a Diaz fight. The French report of **Jul 22, 2026** settles which way he leans: on Ariel Helwani's show he said he is "90%, 95%" sure he will never fight again, and that he and Justin Gaethje agreed never to fight a trilogy. In **June 2026** he was arrested for public intoxication at Atlanta's Hartsfield-Jackson airport (CBS News Atlanta, Fox 8, Sherdog) and afterwards said publicly that he needed help; the incident resurfaced in a public spat with Joaquin Buckley in early November 2026. ESPN lists no upcoming fight, he is not in the Nov 5 lightweight rankings, and no injury is reported. In early November he was working as a pundit, picking the result of Saint Denis vs. Salkilld for MMA Junkie.

## Recent fights
- **L Holloway, UFC 318 (Jul 2025), decision.** Holloway outlanded him 198-109, including 64 body strikes. Each man scored one knockdown.
- **L Islam Makhachev, UFC 302 (Jun 2024), D'Arce choke in round 5.** He had moments on the feet, but Makhachev controlled him for 10:23.
- **W Benoit Saint Denis, UFC 299 (Mar 2024), KO in round 2.** He lost round 1 (12-38 in significant strikes, 3:20 controlled) and still threatened four submissions. He then landed 16 of 21 strikes in round 2 and finished it with punches.
- **L Justin Gaethje, UFC 291 (Jul 2023), head-kick KO in round 2.**
- **W Michael Chandler, UFC 281 (Nov 2022), rear-naked choke in round 3.**
- **L Charles Oliveira, UFC 269 (Dec 2021), rear-naked choke in round 3, for the title.**

## Style
He is a southpaw boxer-puncher. A right jab sets up the left straight, he stays in the pocket for hooks and uppercuts, and he will trade in wild exchanges. 80% of his landed significant strikes go to the head (lightweight median 62%). His leg kicks are mostly calf kicks, used famously against McGregor at UFC 257 (18 leg strikes landed). Off his back foot he grabs guillotines and front headlocks whenever opponents shoot.

## Strengths
- **Power:** 0.65 knockdowns per 15 minutes over his career, about twice the lightweight median; 11 KO/TKO wins in the UFC.
- **Real submission threat:** 1.08 attempts per 15 minutes (median about 0.28) and finishes over elite opponents.
- **Five-round experience:** 15 five-round UFC fights.

## Weaknesses
- He lost to the elite grapplers and wrestlers of his era (Oliveira, Makhachev) and to Gaethje's kicks.
- Late in his career he took heavy damage: 5.9 significant strikes absorbed per minute recency-weighted (median about 4.1) and striking defence near 47%.
- His takedown defence is a middling 63%.

## Durability and cardio
He has 3 KO/TKO losses in 32 UFC fights and absorbed 4 knockdowns over 348 UFC minutes (0.17 per 15 minutes, below the median of about 0.24). He went five rounds with Holloway at 36. His output holds late: his pace by round, relative to round 1, is 1.00 / 1.04 / 0.97 / 1.11 / 1.09.

## What the numbers say
The career (C) sums describe a high-output, high-power pocket boxer: 5.3 significant strikes landed per minute at 50% accuracy, with an Elo of 1715 (7th among recently active lightweights). The recency-weighted (S) sums describe a declining version: output down to 4.5 per minute and absorption up to 5.9. Simulations of a "prime" Poirier should use C. Simulations of his last form should use S.

## Appearance
There is no likeness sheet for him (data/likeness/dustin-poirier.json does not exist), so this comes from dated photos and tattoo write-ups.
- **Hair:** very short, clipper-cut dark brown hair, slightly receding at the temples (ESPN headshot file dated Jul 21, 2025; Getty photo of the UFC 318 weigh-in face-off).
- **Facial hair:** heavy dark stubble, effectively a very short trimmed beard, in his July 2025 fight-week photos.
- **Skin:** light.
- **Tattoos:** heavily tattooed. The photos confirm "Parker" script on the right chest (his daughter, Parker Noelle), tiger stripes on the left chest, a full left-arm sleeve with a tiger and roses, and a small tattoo behind the right ear. EssentiallySports adds "bushido" over the stripes, a shoulder rose, a Japanese war mask, "Phoenix" and flaming stars, Jesus and "blood bought, paid in full" on the forearms, family names and a diamond at the wrists, a Louisiana outline with a magnolia, a fleur-de-lis on the left hand, cover-up blocks on an upper arm, a king of diamonds behind the ear and a large cross on the right thigh.
- **Kit:** not confirmed. No photo of his UFC 318 fight shorts was checked; the sim uses corner colour anyway.
