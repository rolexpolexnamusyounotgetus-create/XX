# Movsar Evloev: scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats come from `data/derived/fighters.json` and the round stats in `data/raw/` (ufcstats snapshot through Oct 31, 2026). Born Feb 11, 1994 in Ingushetia, Russia; former M-1 bantamweight champion; trains at American Top Team (ESPN). Pro record 20-1-0 (ESPN, UFCalendar, ufcrankings.org).

## Current status

- **Last fight:** TKO loss to Alexander Volkanovski for the featherweight title, R2 4:54 (punches from half guard), UFC 333, Abu Dhabi, **Oct 24, 2026**. It was his first pro loss; he entered 20-0 (AP via News4Jax). He offered no excuses and praised Volkanovski (MMA Junkie headline; SI). Paddy Pimblett and TJ Dillashaw said he gave up late in the fight (Sherdog; BJPenn), while Volkanovski said Evloev would "walk through" the rest of the division (Sherdog, Oct 27).
- **Ranking:** still **No. 1** at featherweight on both the UFC media-panel board (Nov 3, 2026, per UFCalendar; ufcrankings.org, updated Nov 5, agrees) and the UFC/Meta algorithmic board (Oct 31, 2026; UFCalendar, MMA Mania).
- **Next fight:** none booked (ESPN, UFCalendar on Nov 6). No injury or medical suspension was reported from UFC 333. Age 32.
- **Injury history:** he withdrew from a planned July 2025 fight with Aaron Pico less than two weeks out (Wikipedia). A Bloody Elbow headline (Mar 25, 2026) says he suffered a "brutal injury" about four weeks before the Murphy fight. We could not open the article, so what it was is unconfirmed. He fought as planned.

## Style

Evloev is a wrestle-boxer who wins on volume of takedowns rather than damage. He averages 9.2 takedown attempts and 4.8 takedowns per 15 minutes career (featherweight league 4.1 and 1.6). On the feet he boxes at range (88% of his landed strikes over three years came at distance). His mix is head-first, but his body work has grown: body strikes were 9% of his attempts career and 17% over three years, and 33 of his 86 landed strikes against Murphy went to the body. In UFC 333's second round, Volkanovski's underhooks and head position stopped the shots (MMA Sucka), and Evloev had no answer standing.

**Strengths:** takedown volume and accuracy (52% career); getting stronger late in fights; defense at range (opponents land 32% of head strikes, league 39%).

**Weaknesses:** no knockdown power: zero knockdowns and zero finishes in 11 UFC fights. He starts slowly: in three-round fights his landed rate in R1 is 2.78 per minute against 4.56 and 4.64 in R2 and R3. His decisions are close (split vs Lentz, majority vs Murphy). Takedown defense over three years is 30% (Sterling 6 of 8).

## Durability and cardio

Before UFC 333 he had never been knocked down in the UFC, and the dataset still records no knockdown in the Volkanovski stoppage. Cardio is a strength in three-round fights, where his output runs 1.64 and 1.67 times R1. He has only two five-round fights, but against Murphy he landed 5 of 5 takedowns in R5.

## What fighters.json says

UFC record 10-1: 10 decision wins and 1 KO/TKO loss. Elo 1742, third among active featherweights behind Volkanovski and Sterling. Decayed target split is 80/14/6 head/body/legs (landed 69/22/8). For the sim, he should win rounds on takedowns and control and almost never finish, and his biggest risk is an opponent who stuffs the shots and keeps him standing.

## Appearance (for the avatar)

No likeness sheet exists yet; reference photos are in assets/reference/movsar-evloev/. Checked against the ESPN headshot and right-stance cutout (files dated Oct 22, 2026), the left-stance cutout (Mar 19, 2026) and The National's UFC 333 grappling photo:

- **Hair:** dark brown and medium length: a heavy mop with a fringe down to the eyebrows, covering the tops of the ears.
- **Facial hair:** a short dark beard, densest on the chin and along the jaw, with a thin moustache and light cheeks. This corrects the earlier recalled "full dark beard".
- **Skin:** light.
- **Tattoos:** none visible on the chest, abdomen, arms or back. In The National's photo the tattooed right arm belongs to Volkanovski.
- **Kit:** black shorts with a dark-grey tonal pattern in both 2026 studio cutouts; white and grey Venum shorts at UFC 333. The corner colour wins in the sim.
