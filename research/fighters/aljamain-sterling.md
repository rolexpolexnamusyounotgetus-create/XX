## Style

Sterling is a wrestler with an unusual kicking game. He chains takedown attempts (7.9 per 15 minutes career, league 4.1) and rides for long stretches (298 s of control per 15, league 169). His best finishes come from the back: a rear-naked choke on Sandhagen in 88 seconds, a back-control TKO of Dillashaw and a Suloev stretch on Stamann. At range he kicks the body and legs: 36% of his attempts go below the head against 24% for the division, and he landed 50 body and 34 leg strikes on Cejudo. He also works in the clinch (15% of landed strikes, league 9%).

**Strengths:** takedown volume and control; back takes; defense (2.15 significant strikes absorbed per minute career, league 4.12; opponents land 29% of head strikes); length (71 in reach at 5 ft 7 in).

**Weaknesses:** no knockout power (0 knockdowns in 23 UFC fights). Takedown defense is 45% career. Standing output since moving up is low (2.76 landed per minute over three years). He is small for 145 and 37 years old.

## Durability and cardio

He was stopped by Marlon Moraes (knee, 2017) and O'Malley, and has absorbed 5 knockdowns, none in the last three years. His decayed chin rate is 0.71 per 100 head strikes (league 0.94). Over 7 five-round fights his striking output stays flat (1.00, 1.13, 0.95, 1.08, 0.79 of R1, below the featherweight curve), but his takedown attempts double from R1 to R5. He won both 2025-26 five-rounders comfortably.
