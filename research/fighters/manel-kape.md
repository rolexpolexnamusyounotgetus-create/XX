# Manel Kape: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Southpaw, 5 ft 5 in, 68 in reach, born 1993-11-14 (age 32). Nickname "StarBoy". Former RIZIN bantamweight champion. UFC record 8-3 (ufcstats); a 24-7 pro record cited from ESPN could not be re-checked.

## Current status

- **Next:** Challenges Joshua Van for the flyweight title, headlining UFC 336 at Scotiabank Arena, Toronto, on Jan 30, 2027. His first UFC title shot; the Houston Chronicle notes he is the only top-four flyweight Van has not beaten. Kape says Van will be "put to sleep" in round 1 (AgentMMA headline).
- **Last fight:** TKO of Kyoji Horiguchi at 2:42 of round 3, UFC Vegas 119 (Jun 20, 2026). His fourth straight knockout, it avenged a 2017 RIZIN submission loss to Horiguchi.
- **Ranking:** #2 at flyweight on ufcrankings.org and #1 on ufcalendar.com (both Nov 3, 2026).
- **Injuries:** None reported in the sources found.

## Style breakdown

A southpaw power striker. His finishes come from the lead or counter right hand (Royval, Horiguchi), punches at distance (Silva, Almabayev) and once a flying knee (Ode Osbourne, 2021). 89% of his decayed attempts come at distance. His target split is less head-heavy than most: 77% head, 13% body, 10% legs attempted; 19% body and 16% legs landed. He seldom wrestles (10 takedown attempts in 11 UFC fights) and relies on takedown defense.

## Strengths

- One-shot power: 8 knockdowns in 124 UFC minutes (0.97 per 15, against a prior of 0.32) and 6 KO/TKO wins in 8.
- Never knocked down or stopped in the UFC.
- Takedown defense of 79% (9 of 42 landed against him).

## Weaknesses

- Slow starts: three of his four straight KOs came in round 3, and Horiguchi was ahead on movement and wrestling before the finish.
- Low output when he can't find the shot. All three UFC losses are decisions (Pantoja, Nicolau, Mokaev), and he lost the debut to Pantoja while being out-landed 74-49.
- No offensive wrestling to fall back on.

## Durability and cardio

No knockdowns absorbed in 11 UFC fights. He went the distance in all three losses. In his five-round main events, his round-3 output is 2.6 times round 1 (small sample: the partial third rounds of the Almabayev and Horiguchi fights). He has not yet gone past round 3 in the UFC.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1651, essentially level with Taira for 3rd-4th among active flyweights and 68 points behind Van. Decayed: 4.38 landed per minute against 3.54 absorbed, 55% accuracy and 56% defense. The engine should read him as a high-variance striker: moderate output, high knockdown chance per exchange.
