## Style

Murphy is a long, patient kickboxer who keeps fights at his range with a 73-inch reach at 5 ft 9 in. Opponents throw only 6.7 significant strikes per minute at him (league 8.7) and land 30% of head strikes (league 39%). His target mix is close to the division's (74/16/10 head/body/legs), but more of what he lands goes low (17% to the legs), and he racks up leg strikes over long fights. He finishes with unusual weapons: a clinch knee on Makwan Amirkhani (2021) and the spinning back elbow on Pico.

**Strengths:** striking defense and range control; a strike differential of +2.0 per minute over three years; volume that builds over five rounds; several ways to finish standing.

**Weaknesses:** wrestling. His takedown defense is 44% career and 40% decayed, he is controlled 199 s per 15 minutes career (league 169), and he rarely shoots (2.2 attempts per 15; none against Evloev). Evloev showed the way to beat him: lose the early striking, then take him down late.

## Durability and cardio

He has absorbed 2 knockdowns in 11 UFC fights (Tukhugov 2019, Ige 2024) and has never been stopped. His decayed chin rate is 0.52 per 100 head strikes (league 0.94). He has gone 25 minutes three times. His output runs 1.34, 1.59, 1.67 and 1.17 times R1 in rounds 2-5, about the featherweight league curve but lower in R5.
