# Michael Morales: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 6 ft 0 in, 79 in reach, born 1999-06-24 (age 27). From Ecuador. UFC record 7-0 (ufcstats); pro record 19-0.

## Current status

- **Booked:** vs Carlos Prates for the **interim UFC welterweight title**, co-main event of UFC 336, Jan 30, 2027, Scotiabank Arena, Toronto. Champion Islam Makhachev had verbally agreed to headline that card against Prates and then backed out. The UFC made Prates vs Morales for the interim belt, and the winner is expected to face Makhachev later in 2027.
- **Before the booking:** On Oct 4, 2026, Morales said he was fighting Shavkat Rakhmonov next. He said he had agreed to a January return. The interim-title offer replaced that plan.
- **Weight:** MMA Mania and Heavy report he started camp around 220 lb, about 50 lb above the limit, with three months to cut.
- **Ranking:** #3 welterweight contender per CBS Sports and EventClock (Nov 2026).
- **Injuries:** None reported in the sources found. He has not fought since Nov 15, 2025.

## Style breakdown

A long, heavy-handed boxer-puncher. He works behind the jab at range and sets up a hard right hand. 87% of his significant attempts go to the head, against a 77% welterweight league rate. Once someone is hurt he swarms, including from back control on the mat. He kicks the legs in longer fights but barely touches the body. His size at 170 shows up in exchanges and in the clinch. Opponents have landed only 3 of 26 takedowns on him.

## Strengths

- One-punch and combination power: 7 knockdowns in 57 UFC minutes, and 2.45 KD per 100 head strikes landed against a 0.84 league rate.
- Output: about 6.3 significant strikes landed per minute (inflated by finishing flurries) on 50% accuracy.
- Takedown defense of 88%, so he keeps fights standing.
- Durability so far: zero knockdowns absorbed.

## Weaknesses

- Barely touches the body, which makes his attack easy to read for elite defensive strikers.
- One five-round bout on record, and it ended in round 1. His championship-round cardio is unknown.
- A reported 50 lb cut for his first title fight.
- Elite strikers and grapplers have not had long with him: Burns and Brady were stopped inside four minutes.

## Durability and cardio

Never knocked down in the UFC. He went 15 minutes twice and stayed busy in both (164 and 208 significant attempts). There is no data past round 3.
