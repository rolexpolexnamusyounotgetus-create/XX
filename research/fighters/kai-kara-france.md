## Style breakdown

The numbers describe a counter-punching striker with real power. He lands 40% of his significant strikes, well below the division, but defends 64% and scores knockdowns at more than twice the flyweight rate. He kicks the legs more than most: 11% of attempts and 21-22% of landed strikes, against about 9% attempted for the division. He works at range (93% of attempts), almost never strikes in the clinch (1%), rarely wrestles (6 of 20 takedowns) and has never attempted a UFC submission.

## Strengths

- Power: 8 knockdowns in 159.6 minutes (0.75 per 15 min). He has first-round KOs of Garbrandt and Erceg and a clinch KO of Bontorin.
- Defense: 64% striking defense and 84% takedown defense career (9 of 56).
- Leg kicks that land at a high rate in long fights (26 of 35 vs Moreno in 2022; 22 of 29 vs Albazi).

## Weaknesses

- Grappling exposure: two submission losses (Royval guillotine 2020, Pantoja RNC 2025), 11 submission attempts against him, and a close loss to control-heavy Albazi.
- Low accuracy: volume does not always win rounds.
- Knocked down 4 times in the UFC and stopped by a body kick once.
- Inactivity since mid-2025, at 33.

## Durability and cardio

His only KO/TKO loss is the Moreno body kick. The four knockdowns he has absorbed came against Royval (2), Garcia and Moreno. His output rises through his five-round fights (rounds 2-5 run 1.7 to 3.3 times round 1), but rounds 4 and 5 come only from the 2023 Albazi fight.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1582, 12th among flyweights active since 2025 in the snapshot. His decayed rates (31.5 weighted minutes) rest on the Erceg KO and the Pantoja loss, so career rates are a better guide: 4.46 landed and 3.24 absorbed per minute. Five of his 8 UFC wins went to decision, and 3 of his 5 losses came inside the distance.
