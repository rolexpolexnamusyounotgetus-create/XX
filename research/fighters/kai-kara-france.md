# Kai Kara-France: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Orthodox, 5 ft 4 in, 69 in reach, born 1993-03-26 (age 33). Nickname "Don't Blink". New Zealander, trains at City Kickboxing in Auckland. Pro record 25-12 (1 NC) (Wikipedia; ESPN 25-12); UFC record 8-5 (ufcstats).

## Current status

- **Last fight:** Rear-naked-choke loss to Alexandre Pantoja at 1:55 of round 3 for the flyweight title, UFC 317 (Jun 28, 2025); CBS wrote that Pantoja submitted him "with ease". His second title shot, after the interim-title loss to Brandon Moreno (UFC 277, 2022).
- **On a break since Nov 2025:** He told the UFC he would take at least a year off to pursue cultural and personal interests outside fighting. Ariel Helwani, after speaking with his team, reported that he is "not retiring necessarily, but he is taking a break". He withdrew from the drug-testing pool, which took him out of the rankings on Nov 18, 2025 until he tells the UFC he is back (MMA Mania; Wikipedia).
- **Now (Nov 6, 2026):** No report of a return, a booking or re-entry to the testing pool was found (Google News and Bing News searches). He is not in the official flyweight top 15 of Nov 3, 2026. Manel Kape named him as an opponent for after the Van fight (Oct 2026). The scouting file marks him inactive (`active: false`).
- **Injury history:** a knee injury cost him a Feb 2023 fight (UFC 284) and a training concussion a Sep 2023 fight with Kape (UFC 293), per Wikipedia. No injury is linked to the current break.

## Style breakdown

The numbers describe a counter-punching striker with real power. He lands 40% of his significant strikes, well below the division, but defends 64% and scores knockdowns at more than twice the flyweight rate. He kicks the legs more than most: 11% of attempts and 21-22% of landed strikes, against about 9% attempted for the division. He works at range (93% of attempts), almost never strikes in the clinch (1%), rarely wrestles (6 of 20 takedowns) and has never attempted a UFC submission.

## Strengths

- Power: 8 knockdowns in 159.6 minutes (0.75 per 15 min). He has first-round KOs of Garbrandt and Erceg and a clinch KO of Bontorin.
- Defense: 64% striking defense and 84% takedown defense career (9 of 56).
- Leg kicks that land at a high rate in long fights (26 of 35 vs Moreno in 2022; 22 of 29 vs Albazi).

## Weaknesses

- Grappling exposure: two submission losses (Royval guillotine 2020, Pantoja RNC 2025), 11 submission attempts against him, and a close loss to control-heavy Albazi.
- Low accuracy: volume does not always win rounds.
- Knocked down 4 times in the UFC and stopped by a body kick once.
- Inactivity: no fight since Jun 2025 and on a self-imposed break, at 33.

## Durability and cardio

His only KO/TKO loss is the Moreno body kick. The four knockdowns he has absorbed came against Royval (2), Garcia and Moreno. His output rises through his five-round fights (rounds 2-5 run 1.7 to 3.3 times round 1), but rounds 4 and 5 come only from the 2023 Albazi fight.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1582, 12th among flyweights active since 2025 in the snapshot. Five of his 8 UFC wins went to decision, and 3 of his 5 losses came inside the distance.
