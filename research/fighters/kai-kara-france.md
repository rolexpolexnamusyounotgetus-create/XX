## Style breakdown

The numbers describe a counter-punching striker with real power. He lands 40% of his significant strikes, well below the division, but defends 64% and scores knockdowns at more than twice the flyweight rate. He kicks the legs more than most flyweights: 11% of his attempts and 21-22% of his landed strikes go to the legs, against about 9% attempted for the division. He works almost entirely at range (93% of attempts) and almost never strikes in the clinch (1%). He rarely wrestles himself (6 of 20 takedowns career) and has never attempted a submission in the UFC.

## Strengths

- Power: 8 knockdowns in 159.6 minutes (0.75 per 15 min). He has first-round KOs of Garbrandt and Erceg and a clinch KO of Bontorin.
- Defense: 64% striking defense and 84% takedown defense career (9 of 56).
- Leg kicks that land at a high rate in long fights (26 of 35 vs Moreno in 2022; 22 of 29 vs Albazi).

## Weaknesses

- Grappling exposure: two submission losses (Royval guillotine 2020, Pantoja RNC 2025) and 11 submission attempts against him in total. Control-heavy opponents have won close fights against him (Albazi).
- Low accuracy, so his volume does not always add up to rounds won.
- Knocked down 4 times in the UFC and stopped by a body kick once.
- Inactivity and age (33) since his last fight in mid-2025.

## Durability and cardio

His only KO/TKO loss is the Moreno body kick. The four knockdowns he has absorbed came against Royval (2), Garcia and Moreno. His output rises through his five-round fights (rounds 2-5 run 1.7 to 3.3 times round 1), but rounds 4 and 5 come only from the 2023 Albazi fight.

## What the numbers say

Elo 1582, 12th among flyweights active since 2025 in the snapshot. His decayed rates (31.5 weighted minutes) are dominated by the Erceg KO and the Pantoja loss, so they are thin. His career rates are a better guide: 4.46 landed and 3.24 absorbed per minute. Five of his 8 UFC wins went to decision, and 3 of his 5 losses came inside the distance.
