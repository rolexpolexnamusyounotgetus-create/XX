# Dominick Reyes: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. "The Devastator". Southpaw, 6 ft 4 in, 77 in reach, born 1989-12-26 (age 36). UFC record 11-5 (ufcstats); pro record 17-5 (ESPN, Wikipedia).

**Re-check, Nov 6, 2026:** status, ranking, record, injuries and appearance were re-verified on the open web (sources at the end). Rankings come from ufcalendar.com's mirror because ufc.com returned 403.

## Current status

- **Title:** none. He challenged Jon Jones at UFC 247 (Feb 8, 2020) and lost a unanimous decision (48-47, 48-47, 49-46) after out-landing Jones 116-104 in significant strikes. He then lost to Jan Blachowicz for the vacant belt at UFC 253 (TKO R2).
- **Form:** after four straight losses (Jones, Blachowicz, Prochazka, Spann) and a 19-month layoff, he is 5-1 since June 2024. His last two fights are a split decision over Johnny Walker (UFC 327, Apr 11, 2026, Miami) and a first-round TKO of Azamat Murzakanov (UFC 333, Abu Dhabi, Oct 24, 2026): a head kick put Murzakanov on shaky legs, a straight left dropped him, and ground strikes ended it at 2:40 (MMA Fighting). After UFC 333 he spent the week feuding publicly with commentator Daniel Cormier over the broadcast.
- **Next:** none booked as of Nov 6, 2026. He is on no UFC card in the ESPN schedule feed (through Feb 2027) or the Sherdog events list (through UFC Fight Night 298, Feb 6, 2027).
- **Ranking:** #4 light heavyweight in the Meta UFC rankings (Oct 31, 2026) and #7 in the UFC media-panel rankings (Nov 3, 2026, up one), per ufcalendar.com. Wikipedia also lists #4 in the Meta rankings as of Oct 24.
- **Injuries:** none reported.

## Style breakdown

A rangy southpaw who fights at distance (77% of decayed attempts) and looks for one big shot early, usually the straight left (a head kick set up the Murzakanov finish): six of his seven UFC knockouts came in round 1. Against Jones he worked the body hard (48/55 body strikes landed). In longer fights he turns to low kicks. On top he can pour on ground strikes (Smith). He rarely shoots: 0.09 takedowns per 15 min decayed.

## Strengths

- Knockout power: 2.7 knockdowns per 100 head strikes landed (decayed), against a light heavyweight league rate of 1.3.
- Hard to hit: 2.9 significant strikes absorbed per minute decayed. Opponents land 49%.
- Takedown defense 91% decayed; opponents control him for only 26 s per 15 min.

## Weaknesses

- Chin: four KO/TKO losses, including round-1 stoppages by Spann and Ulberg. 2.9 knockdowns absorbed per 100 head strikes taken (decayed).
- Modest volume (8.2 attempts per minute decayed). Walker out-landed him and the decision was split.
- Leg-kick defense: Walker landed 29 of 38.

## Durability and cardio

He went five rounds with Jones, and his landed rate in rounds 2-3 of five-round fights ran above round 1 (1.20, 1.29), dipping in round 4 (0.65); rounds 3-5 of that curve come from the Jones fight alone. His fights tend to end early either way: 12 of 16 UFC bouts ended inside the distance.
