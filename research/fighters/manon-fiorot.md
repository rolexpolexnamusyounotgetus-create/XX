# Manon Fiorot ("The Beast"): scouting dossier

As of Nov 6, 2026. Stats: `data/derived/fighters.json` and `data/raw/` (ufcstats snapshot to Oct 31, 2026). Status, record, ranking and appearance were re-checked on the web on Nov 6, 2026 (sources at the end).

## Current status

- **Last fight:** lost to Alexa Grasso by unanimous decision, 28-29 x3, at Noche UFC (Sep 12, 2026, Glendale), in a bout billed as a title eliminator (Wikipedia). She was dropped in R1.
- **Before that:** stopped Jasmine Jasudavicius with ground punches at 1:14 of R1 (Oct 18, 2025, Vancouver). Lost a 47-48 x3 decision to Valentina Shevchenko for the flyweight title at UFC 315 (May 10, 2025, Montreal).
- **Title picture:** Natalia Silva won the vacant women's flyweight title over Wang Cong at UFC 332 (Oct 3, 2026), and Grasso is the expected next challenger ([Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/articles/Matches-to-make-after-UFC-332-Natalia-Silva-vs-Alexa-Grasso-leads-the-list-203051)).
- **Next fight and injuries:** nothing booked as of Nov 6, 2026 ([UFCalendar](https://www.ufcalendar.com/fighters/manon-fiorot)), and no news item about her since the Grasso fight was found, so no injury is reported.
- **Ranking:** No. 3 at women's flyweight and No. 7 women's pound-for-pound (UFC media-panel rankings of Nov 3, 2026, mirrored by UFCalendar and ufcrankings.org); No. 4 in UFC's algorithmic Meta rankings (Oct 31).
- **Titles:** none.
- **Record:** 13-3 pro (Wikipedia, ESPN, UFCalendar); 8-2 in the UFC. She is 36 and from Nice, France. Before MMA she was a karate black belt and made the French national karate team (Wikipedia).

## Style

She is a volume striker. She throws 13.6 significant strikes per minute over her career, about 1.5 times the flyweight average, mostly punches to the head with a busy body attack. Body strikes are 12% of her attempts but 23% of what she lands. Against Shevchenko she switched to the clinch: 29 of 33 clinch strikes and 35 of 37 body strikes landed. ufcstats does not say whether those were knees or punches. She kicks the legs rarely. Her three KO/TKO wins all came when she swarmed a hurt opponent. Stance: ufcstats, UFCalendar and ESPN list her as orthodox, while Wikipedia's infobox says southpaw; the scouting file keeps orthodox.

## Strengths

- Output: she lands 5.56 significant strikes per minute career (division 3.98).
- Takedown defence: 87.5% career. Opponents average 43 s of control per 15 min against her (decayed; division 180).
- Late rounds: she out-landed both five-round opponents in R5.

## Weaknesses

- Accuracy: 39% decayed (division 43%). She lands only about a quarter of her head strikes against good movers (51 of 196 vs Grasso, 39 of 197 vs Namajunas).
- Leg-kick defence: 0.81 leg strikes absorbed per minute, decayed (division 0.63).
- Her own takedowns: 9 of 31.
- Sharp counter-punchers drop her: once each by Shevchenko and Grasso.

## Durability and cardio

- **Durability:** no KO losses, but two knockdowns absorbed, both in her two losses. That is 0.65 per 100 head strikes absorbed (division 0.25).
- **Cardio:** a clear strength. Her output in five-round fights runs 1.00, 0.95, 1.05, 1.46 and 1.46 of round 1, against a division curve of 1.00, 0.90, 1.01, 1.14, 1.32. That is two fights only.

## What the numbers say

Her Elo is 1672, fourth among active flyweights, behind Shevchenko (1750), Silva (1722) and Blanchfield (1676), all three of whom she has fought. The engine should give her very high volume, below-average accuracy, near-immunity to takedowns, a pace that holds or rises late, and modest single-shot power (1 knockdown scored in 10 UFC fights). Against counter-strikers, or opponents who kick her legs, she should lose close decisions. Against fighters who need takedowns she should win on volume.

## Appearance

Checked against ESPN studio photos (headshot and cutout dated Sep 10, 2026; cutout dated May 8, 2025) and ESPN's lead photo from the Grasso fight. No likeness sheet exists for her.

- **Hair:** long, wavy and dark blonde with a centre parting, worn loose in studio photos. For fights it is in tight braids gathered into two knots at the back (Noche UFC, Sep 2026). The first pass recalled dark hair; that was wrong.
- **Skin:** light, with freckles.
- **Tattoos:** none visible on the arms, shoulders, abdomen or legs.
- **Kit:** a black UFC Venum set with knee-length compression-style shorts (Sep 2026 studio photo and fight photo); the white Venum studio set in May 2025.
- **Build:** 5 ft 7 in with a 65 in reach (ufcstats). Very lean and muscular, with sharply defined shoulders and abdominals.
