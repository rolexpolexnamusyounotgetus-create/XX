## Style

He is a switch-stance wrestler (ufcstats lists him as switch). He shoots 9.1 times per 15 minutes and lands 3.7 (league 4.3 and 1.5), then stays on top. Control runs 569 seconds per 15 minutes career, about 63% of his cage time (league 174 seconds). He hunts the back: all five of his UFC submissions are rear-naked chokes, and the Garcia stoppage came from back control. Older previews describe a wrestling-heavy game that pushes a fast pace and then works for submissions ([Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/articles/5/Preview-UFC-Fight-Night-214-Rodriguez-vs-Lemos-188364)). Standing, 81% of his attempts go to the head and few go to the legs. His striking is mainly a lead-in to the shot.

**Strengths:** takedown volume and accuracy (48% decayed), long control, back takes, late finishes (4 of 7 in round 3, two in the final 20 seconds), and fights that build in his favor.

**Weaknesses:** early stand-up. Over the last three years opponents land 58% of their distance strikes on him (league 44%). He has scored one knockdown in 16 UFC fights, so he has little threat to hold strikers off. Takedown defense is fine (78% over three years), but he rarely wins striking exchanges.

## Durability and cardio

His only UFC losses are first-round knockouts: King Green at 0:33 in his only five-round main event (2023), and Torres. With the Rebecki knockdown that makes 3 knockdowns absorbed since 2023. His decayed chin rate is 1.51 per 100 head strikes absorbed (league 0.95). His cardio looks good: in three-round fights his landed rate runs 1.00, 1.14 and 1.23 by round, and he came back after losing R1 to Aliev and after being dropped in R2 by Rebecki.
