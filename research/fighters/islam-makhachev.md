# Islam Makhachev: scouting dossier

As of 2026-11-06. He/him. Southpaw, 5 ft 10 in, 70 in reach, born 1991-10-27 (age 35). UFC record 18-1 (ufcstats); pro record 29-1.

## Current status

- **Title:** UFC welterweight champion. He took the 170 lb belt from Jack Della Maddalena with a 50-45 sweep at UFC 322 (Nov 15, 2025), after giving up the lightweight title (UFC 317 in June 2025 was for the vacant belt, per ufcstats).
- **Last fight:** UD over Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330, Philadelphia, Aug 15, 2026 (49-46, 49-46, 48-47). It was his 17th straight UFC win, a promotional record.
- **Next:** No bout booked. Dana White said Makhachev verbally agreed to face Carlos Prates at UFC 336 (Jan 30, 2027, Toronto) and then backed out. The UFC booked Prates vs Michael Morales for an interim title in that slot, and the winner is expected to face Makhachev later in 2027. On Oct 31, 2026 he posted that his next fight will be "one of the last" of his career. Al Jazeera and MMA Fighting report he wants to wait until after Ramadan. On Nov 4 Dana White said "I think it's money" and that two fights a year "would be nice"; on Nov 5 Prates' manager, Lucas Lutkus, said Makhachev gave the UFC no reason for turning January down.
- **Ranking:** #1 men's pound-for-pound in the UFC media-panel rankings (ufc.com, last updated Nov 3, 2026). Since June 2026 the UFC also publishes the Meta UFC rankings, an Elo model with no pound-for-pound list (last updated Oct 31, 2026). CBS Sports also had him #1 (Sep 2026).
- **Injuries:** None reported as of Nov 6, 2026 (he was cut at UFC 330).

## Style breakdown

A sambo-based chain wrestler. He works opponents to the fence, ties up a body lock and goes through trips and mat returns until one lands. Once on top he passes to side control or takes the back, and finishes with arm-triangles (Oliveira, Dober), D'Arce chokes (Poirier, Moicano), rear-naked chokes and kimuras. Standing, he throws few strikes but lands them: 56% significant accuracy decayed against a 48% welterweight league rate, and opponents land 27% of head strikes on him against a 39% league rate. The southpaw left straight and left kicks are his main weapons. The left high kick ended Volkanovski and dropped Garry.

## Strengths

- Takedown offense and control: 3.2 takedowns per 15 min, and 485 s of control per 15 min decayed against a 167 s league rate.
- Takedown defense: 91% career.

## Weaknesses

- Low output: about 3.4 significant attempts per minute. Rangy, busy strikers can win minutes on the feet, as Garry did in round 3.
- At 170 he has not finished anyone (two 25-minute decisions). Bigger opponents are harder to hold down.
- Age 35, and he openly frames his remaining fights as few.

## Durability and cardio

Two knockdowns absorbed in 19 UFC fights. His one loss is a 2015 first-round KO by Adriano Martins. He has gone 25 minutes three times (Volkanovski in Feb 2023, Della Maddalena, Machado Garry) and finished Poirier in round 5. His 5-round output curve (1.00, 1.24, 0.89, 0.67, 0.54 of round 1) falls late, but most of that drop is control time, not fatigue.
