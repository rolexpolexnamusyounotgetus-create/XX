## Method

**Universe and protocol.** Every fight in `data/derived/backtest_dev.json` (both men had at least one prior UFC fight),
fights dated 2026-01-01 or later dropped on load, and any plugin output for a fight outside that set discarded at the
door (several upstream files still carry 2026 rows: `methodround.json`, `ratings.json`, `preufc.json`, the ML stage-1
table; none of them reaches a fit or a score here). Replay window = the hidden-data protocol's validation years,
2024-01-01..2025-12-31: 865 fights, 854 decisive, on 84 card dates. History = 2019-01-01..2023-12-31 (1,999 fights), used
only to train the stackers, through each model's strictly out-of-sample 2019-2023 predictions.

**Replay.** For each card date in order, every registered model is brought up to date with data strictly before that
date and predicts the card:

| schedule | models | what "refit" means |
|---|---|---|
| per card | `ml_lr`, `feat_lr`, every stacker | refit on every decisive fight before the card (ML: 2010 on; stackers: 2019 on) with the hyper-parameters frozen at their pre-2024 choice |
| per month | `ml_lgb`, `ml_xgb` | refit before the month's first card (tree fits are the slow part on the shared box); later cards that month use the same fit |
| sequential / frozen | `elo`, `elo_sig`, `mkt_rating`, `ratings_*`, `preufc_elo`, `engine_v*`, `mr_*`, `ml_lr_static`, `ml_lr_annual`, the lines | pre-fight ratings that already include every earlier result (Elo, WHR, market rating), or fits frozen before 2024 (simulator global scales, `mr_S` 2024 / Jan-1-2025 refit, `ml_lr_static`) |

Every fit records `fit_through` (its last training fight); the harness refuses any prediction whose `fit_through` is on
or after the card date (none tripped). Each prediction is stored per backtest fight id as `[P(A), refit cutoff,
fit_through]` with the model's version string, and the replay carries its UTC timestamp. Heavy fits are cached in
`out/blindtest_cache/` keyed by model version and cutoff, so a rerun after adding one model only fits that model.

**Reproduction check.** With the cutoff at 2024-01-01 the harness's ML refits reproduce `ml.json`'s frozen 2024-2025
predictions to 5e-5 (logistic, LightGBM, XGBoost, 865 fights), and the logistic design rebuilt from the current
`tools/ml.py` matches the stage-1 cache bit for bit.

**Look-ahead selftest** (`python3 tools/blindtest.py selftest --cards 6`). It replays the first six cards twice. The
second time, every outcome dated on or after the 4th card (2024-02-10) is flipped: in the backtest labels, the ML table
and the feature-LR table.
- Result: all 40 predictions up to that card are bit-identical for the 13 refit models and stackers tested.
- Predictions after it move, by up to 0.021 for `moe_noline`, so the flip took effect.
- Stored upstream predictions (Elo, market rating, WHR, ML v2, the simulator) are covered by their own tools'
  truncation and flip tests. This selftest cannot change them.

**Symmetry.** Scoring uses the probability given to the actual winner. Every model is corner-symmetric by
construction (antisymmetric logits without an intercept, mirrored training rows, both corner orders averaged), and the
refit models and stackers are re-predicted with A and B swapped on every replayed fight: max |P(A,B) + P(B,A) - 1| is
at most 4e-16 for the logistic models and stackers and 6e-8 for XGBoost (see the models table below).

**Scoring.** Log loss (primary), Brier, accuracy (ties count half), ECE (10 mirrored bins) and the calibration slope
(mirrored, no-intercept logistic of the outcome on the model's logit; 1 = calibrated, above 1 = underconfident). 95%
intervals from 2,000 event-cluster bootstrap resamples of the 84 card dates, the same draws for every model and every
paired difference. The headline table is the **common set**: fights every model with at least 95% window coverage
predicts (the closing line is missing for a handful of fights, so this is the binding constraint). Cumulative curves
are recomputed after every card; segments are on the common set; the newcomer table also covers partial-coverage
models on their own fights.

**Registered models** (`python3 tools/blindtest.py list`): the closing and opening lines (benchmarks); walk-forward Elo
and strike-share Elo; the market rating (Kalman filter on earlier closing lines); `tools/ratings.py`'s round-level WHR,
its rating stack and ratings + covariates; the pre-UFC-seeded Elo; the `tools/ml.py` logistic (per-card refit, plus
the frozen and January-refit controls), LightGBM, XGBoost and their blend; the MoE page feature LR; the simulator v3,
v4 and the rejected v4.0.1 (`tools/evalv.js` validation runs: frozen engine, walk-forward profiles, leak-free learned
stat-line rates; the MoE stackers use the newest engine whose validation decision in `version_history.json` is not
`kept: false`, now v4, the engine in src/; `BLINDTEST_MOE_ENGINE` pins another);
method/round models S and SM; and the stackers: the `ml.json` pre-line stack (Elo + ML + market rating), the MoE
stacker with `moe.json`'s chosen configuration in three information classes (no line, opening line, closing line),
and experiments (marked) that add the WHR, method/round S or the pre-UFC Elo as experts, or recalibrate on the last
two years. Two plugins in `tools/blindtest_plugins/`:
- `ml2.py`: ML v2 (`tools/ml2.py`), its own stacks, and `moe_noline_ml2` / `moe_close_ml2`. It registers once
  `data/derived/ml2.json` exists.
- `noengine.py`: `moe_noline_noeng` / `moe_close_noeng`, the MoE stackers without the simulator. These are the
  controls for the engine's contribution.

## Integration recipe

**Adding a model (about ten lines).** In `tools/blindtest.py` or a new `tools/blindtest_plugins/<name>.py`:

```python
from blindtest import register, Stored

@register("ml2", group="model", desc="ML v2 (tools/ml2.py), walk-forward per its protocol")
def _ml2(ctx):
    d = ctx.json("ml2.json")                      # None when the file is absent -> the model is skipped
    if not d:
        return None
    P = {i: r["p"] for i, r in d["fights"].items()}                       # P(A wins), backtest fight ids
    hist = {i: r["p"] for i, r in d["fights"].items() if r.get("set") == "oof"}  # strictly out-of-sample 2019-23
    return Stored(P, history=hist, version="ml2.json built " + d["meta"]["built"])
```

A model that learns subclasses `Model` with `fit(cutoff, P)` (data strictly before `cutoff`; return
`{"fit_through", "n_train"}`), `predict(fids, P)` and, for the symmetry check, `predict_swapped(fids, P)`; give it
`refit="event"` or `"month"`. A stacker is one line: `Stack(ctx, ["elo", "ml_lr", "ml2"])` or
`MoEStack(ctx, dict(_MOE_BASE, ml2="ml2"), "absent")`. Then `nice -n 5 python3 tools/blindtest.py replay`: cached base
models are not refit, only the new model and the stackers run. A new simulator version needs nothing here: once
`tools/evalv.js --version v<N> --split val` has made its single recorded validation look, `engine_v<N>` registers itself
from `data/derived/evalv/v<N>_val.json`. The MoE stackers switch to it unless `version_history.json` records it as
rejected (as happened to v4.0.1 at 7:18 pm on Nov 6, during this run's first replay: that replay was redone with v4).
The harness never runs a validation look itself.

**Exporting the production stack.** `nice -n 5 python3 tools/blindtest.py export` refits `moe_close` (a line is given)
and `moe_noline` (no line) on every dev fight 2019-2025. The 2019-2023 rows use out-of-sample expert predictions and
the 2024-2025 rows the replay's blind ones. The result goes to `data/derived/blindtest_stack.json` in `src/ml/moe.js`'s
format, with 120 Python test vectors. `node tests/test_blindtest_stack.js` checks the page evaluator against them:
max |diff| 2.2e-16 and exact corner swaps. Re-export after every replay that changes the engine or an expert.

**Using it for selection.** The window is the protocol's validation set, so the harness is where versions are
compared and chosen; every comparison it prints is paired on identical fights with event-cluster intervals. A model
or stacker variant added after looking at these numbers (the experiments here) is selection on the validation window:
keep it only if it wins by a margin whose interval excludes zero, and then confirm it on the forward ledger before
calling it better.

**Forward ledger.** `python3 tools/forward_ledger.py predict --event <date>` writes the card's write-once file from the
newest pre-lock odds snapshot and the page's package, evaluated by `tools/forward_ledger.js`:
- The MoE stack, with and without the line. `--stack auto`, the default, uses `blindtest_stack.json` when it exists;
  `--stack page` uses `moe_page.json`, whose engine weights were fit on engine 3.0.0.
- Its experts: feat_lr (always from `moe_page.json`), Elo, the ML page model, the market rating and the simulator
  from src/ (FightEngine.summarize, both corner orders).
- Method and round from the method-and-round model, scaled to the stack. `p_a` = the stack with the line captured
at prediction time, `p_a_noline` = without it; both, plus Elo and the market, are scored by
`python3 tools/forward_ledger.py score` after the event (ufcstats via the Greco1899 CSVs and ESPN's scoreboard as the
second source; only ledger bouts are recorded). Matching was checked offline on a 2025 dev card (12 of 12 bouts,
both sources agreeing, corner order swapped on half of them); write-once, supersede and scoring were checked on a
scratch ledger with synthetic results. The first real file (Nov 7 card) is to be written on the day, before the
22:00 UTC lock, with the newest engine. A dry run on that card at 7:40 pm Nov 6 (`--dry-run --dry-out` to the scratchpad,
200 sims per order) ran end to end with `blindtest_stack.json` and engine 4.0.0 and wrote nothing under
`data/predictions/`.

## Caveats

1. **This is the validation window, not a vault.** 2024-2025 is the protocol's selection set.
   - The production stackers use `moe.json`'s configuration, chosen by forward-chained CV inside 2019-2023.
   - The rows marked "exp." were added after earlier looks at 2024-2025, and so were ML v2's own stacks. That includes
     the top of the table: `moe_close_ml2`, `ml2_stack_line` and `moe_close_gated`. Their edges over `moe_close`
     (-0.0005 to -0.0018) are inside the noise.
   - Treat them as candidates for the forward ledger, not as wins.
2. **Not everything refits.** Several models are sequential ratings or fits frozen before 2024: Elo, the market rating,
   the WHR and rating stack, pre-UFC Elo, the simulator's global scales and method/round S.
   - Their parameters were tuned on 2019-2023, so their 2019-2023 history rows (the stackers' training data) are
     in-sample for those few parameters. That can make a stacker trust them a little too much.
   - For the engine this is visible: it gets 0.15-0.31 weight, and the no-engine control shows the weight buys
     nothing.
   - ML v2 refits monthly and the trees refit monthly (CPU on the shared box). Everything else that learns refits
     before every card.
3. **Unverified inputs.** No verifier has signed off on `ratings.json` (4:44 pm build, now on preufc's strict
   `net_elo_h`), `preufc.json` (verifier relaunched) or ML v2 (`ml2.json`, 4:52 pm). Their rows are scored here as
   published. The production stack (`moe_*`, `blindtest_stack.json`) uses none of them.
4. **Universe.** These are backtest fights: both men need at least one prior UFC fight, so debuts are excluded, and
   draws are left out of the winner metrics. The 11 fights without a closing line are outside the common set.
   - The forward ledger must handle debutants with fallbacks (line, else Elo with a 1500 debut rating) that were never
     scored here.
5. **Resolution.** 84 card dates give absolute intervals about ±0.025 wide. Paired differences between stack variants
   (0.001-0.004) are below what two years can resolve. Only the large gaps are firm:
   - stack vs single models;
   - the close vs the open;
   - Elo vs everything.
6. **Ledger vs replay.** The forward package is close to the blind-tested stack, but not identical:
   - feat_lr at prediction time comes from `moe_page.json`, not the replay's per-card refit;
   - the engine runs with the page's learned rates rather than out-of-fold ones;
   - the ML page model is the full fit through 2025;
   - the market line is the consensus at prediction time, not the close.

   So p_a on the ledger sits between `moe_open` and `moe_close` in what it knows.
7. **No-line probabilities on newcomers run hot.** Slope 0.81 on the 258 newcomer fights. In the Nov 7 dry run, one
   bout had p_a_noline 0.04 against a no-vig market of 0.21. The ledger's headline `p_a` uses the line, and
   `p_a_noline` is scored separately.
8. **Scoring command.** `forward_ledger.py score` depends on the Greco1899 CSVs on raw.githubusercontent.com (they can
   lag an event by days) and on ESPN's scoreboard JSON. `--manual` adds results by hand from ESPN or Sherdog. Matching
   was checked on a 2025 dev card and on a scratch ledger, and has not yet been run on a live card.
9. **Upstream 2026 rows.** Several upstream files still carry 2026 rows: `methodround.json`, `ratings.json`,
   `preufc.json` and the ML stage-1 table, which drops 480 rows from 2026 on load. The harness discards them at the
   door. Nothing from 2026 reaches a fit or a score.
