    AFTER YOU JOIN

    What happens next

    No experience needed. Nobody expects you to know everything on day one.

    1. 1

      We say hi.

      A short welcome in English or Spanish, your pick. No quiz, no homework.

    2. 2

      We invite you to one thing near you.

      A trivia night, a sunset walk, a text bank. Bring a friend so you're not the only new person.

    3. 3

      We only send what you asked for.

      One letter a week, on Thursdays. Texts only if you tick the box.
