    UNDER 18?

    14 to 17? Here's how it works.

    Same club, same short form, a few extra protections. Join free from 14.

    • Your link skips your name

      Under 18, your invite link gets a random code, never your first name.

    • Email, not texts

      No club texts before 18. No adult organizer messages you one-on-one: group chats always have two adults.

    • A grown-up's OK when it matters

      A parent or guardian says yes first for anything off-site, after dark or at a rally, and before we post a photo or clip where you can be recognized.

    • Pre-register at 16

      Florida lets you pre-register to vote on or after your 16th birthday. That step is yours: no stickers, points or prizes for it, ever.
