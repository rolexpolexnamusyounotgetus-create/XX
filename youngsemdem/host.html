What hosting means

You pick the night. We back you up.

  1. You pick it

    Trivia, a picnic, a study night, a sunset walk, a Spanish conversation table. If you would show up to it, it counts.

  2. We list it

    It goes on the events page with an RSVP, a calendar file and a reminder, so you know who is coming.

  3. You're not alone

    A crew guide (DEMO) checks the plan with you, and two adults are on site whenever anyone under 18 comes.

The house rules

Every Young Dems night checks these boxes

Members start at 14, so these aren't optional. They're what makes it easy for anyone to say yes.

  • A public place

    A park, a library room, a café anyone can walk into. Never someone’s home.

  • Alcohol-free

    Always. Same for vapes, cannabis and tobacco.

  • Open to 14 and up

    In any party or none. Two adults on site whenever anyone under 18 comes, and a guardian’s OK if it ends after dark.

  • An access note

    Step-free entry, seating, a quiet corner, and who to tell what you need. Write it before you share the invite.

  • No forms, no data

    Nobody collects forms, signatures or personal information at our nights. People do official stuff themselves, on official sites.

  • Photos with a yes

    Ask before you post anyone. Under 18, a guardian says yes first.

The 3-week plan

From idea to a full room in 3 weeks

  1. 3 weeks out

    Pick it and pitch it

    Choose a public place and a date (skip the second Thursday: that’s the county party’s monthly meeting). Pitch it with the button below. Ask two friends to co-host.

  2. 2 weeks out

    Make it look good

    We list it (DEMO). Make your flyer and story card below, send the invite texts, and post the card once.

  3. 1 week out

    Lock it in

    Confirm the room, finish the access note, and pick who says hi at the door. If anyone under 18 RSVPd, confirm your two adults.

  4. The night

    Say hi first

    Name tags at the door, introduce first-timers to two people, and keep it to what you promised. Photos only with a yes.

  5. The day after

    Say thanks, plan the next

    Text everyone a thank-you, tell your crew guide how it went, and post a 60-second clip if people said yes.

Hosts get the good stuff

Merch goes to organizers

Hosting counts as a volunteer shift. Rewards go to organizing, never to registering, renewing or voting.

  • Shift Done

    Host a night and you unlock the Shift Done sticker and the celery tote from the next merch drop (DEMO perk).

  • Squad of Three

    Three friends join with your link and the Young Dems tee is yours (DEMO perk).

  • First pick

    Hosts get first pick of seats at the next Seminole Sunday (DEMO perk).
