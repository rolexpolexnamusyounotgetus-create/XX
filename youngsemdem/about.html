THE HONEST PART

What's real?

This site is a demo, built to show the chapter what its website could be. Some of it is checked fact. A lot of it is made up on purpose so you can see how things would work. Here's exactly which is which.

How to read the tags

  • REAL Checked against a source, with the date.
  • DEMO Made up for this preview.
  • DRAFT Written, but not approved or in effect yet.
  • Stock photo · not members Models, not members.

Real

  • Voting rules, deadlines, dates and official links, checked Nov 9, 2026.
  • Membership rules from the chapter's dues page: ages 14 to 39, $20 a year paid in January, and a non-voting membership for people who can't pay.
  • The chapter itself. YDSC is real, and the Florida Young Democrats list it as a chapter.
  • Numbers with a source link next to them. Election results stay marked unofficial until the county certifies them.
  • Every sticker fact. Tap a sticker and you get its source.
  • Links to the county party, the elections office and ActBlue.

Made up DEMO

  • Every event: dates, venues, hosts and how many are going.
  • Every person: names, bylines, quotes, the team below and The Thursday Letter's writer.
  • Every clip, and every @demo handle.
  • Counters and squad numbers. They never tick up by themselves, and each one says DEMO.
  • City crews. They're ideas waiting for a host, not groups that meet yet.
  • Forms. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Not decided yet

  • The policies (privacy, teen safety, clip rules, conduct, accessibility) are drafts, not legal advice, and not in effect.
  • The chapter hasn't signed off on this site yet.
  • The "Paid for by" line in the footer is a placeholder until the chapter settles its committee status.
  • The Spanish is written by hand and still waiting for a fluent reviewer.
  • Social accounts stay unlinked until the chapter confirms them, and youngsemdems.com isn't live.
  • Who moderates clips, who's on the safety team, and whether the chapter publishes its money. We don't promise any of it until the chapter does.
  • Real photos of real members come later, with everyone’s OK. Until then: stock photos, tagged.
