        1. A public place

          A park, a library room, a café anyone can walk into. Never someone’s home.

        2. Alcohol-free

          Always. Members start at 14.

        3. An access note

          Step-free entry, seating, a quiet corner, and who to tell what you need.

        4. Our teen safety rules

          Two adults on site, daytime for door-knocking, a guardian’s OK when it ends after dark.
