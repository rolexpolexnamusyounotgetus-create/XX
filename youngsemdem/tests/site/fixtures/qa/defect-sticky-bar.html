How it works

Pick a hangout, tap I'm in, and bring someone. Every event is free and open to any party or none.

Trivia nights, canvass walks by the lake and letter-writing at the library. Come once, stay if you like it.

Questions about voting get straight answers with the official link next to each one.

Most of us are students or just starting work, from Sanford to Oviedo and everywhere between.

We meet on weeknights and some Saturday mornings, always somewhere you can reach by car or bus.

Bring a friend who has never been to anything political. That is the whole point.

What to bring

Nothing but yourself. Snacks are on us at most events, and we post the plan the day before.

If you need a ride or an accessibility change, say so when you RSVP and we will sort it out.

New faces are the best part of every night, so do not worry about knowing anyone.

We keep it friendly, local and short, and we finish on time so you can get home.

See you soon at the next one.
