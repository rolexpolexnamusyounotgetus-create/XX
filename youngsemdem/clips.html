      HOW THE REEL WORKS

      Kind clips, real people, no scoreboard.

      • A person checks every clip

        Usually within 3 days. Nothing goes public before that, and only you can see yours while it waits.

      • Report anything

        Tap ··· then Report on any clip. Reports can be anonymous, and we never tell the uploader who reported.

      • No views. No like counts.

        Your heart is just for you. Post because it matters to you, not for a number.

      • Under 18? A parent says OK first

        If anyone under 18 shows up in a clip, a parent or guardian signs off before it goes public. No last names, schools or addresses.

      • Captions on, always

        Every clip here has captions in English and Spanish. Turn them off with the CC button.
