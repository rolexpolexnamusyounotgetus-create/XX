02The one thingDo it this week

Small step, real deadline.

Mail ballot · everyone

Your mail ballot request runs out Dec 31.

52days to go

Every Florida mail ballot request on file ends Dec 31, 2026. Ask again so you're covered for the 2027 city elections and for 2028.

How to re-request

Rule: F.S. 101.62(1)(a). Ask the Seminole Supervisor of Elections when new requests start counting: 407-585-8683. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

Heads up · Jan 1, 2027

Student IDs stop counting at the polls.

Starting Jan 1, 2027, Florida stops accepting student IDs at the polls (it's being challenged in court). Bring a driver's license, state ID or passport, and check voteseminole.gov before you go.

03DebriefResults unofficial until certified

Nov 3, debriefed.

Seminole picked the Democrat for governor by nearly ten points. Statewide, the Republican won by about one. Both things are true.

The county still has to certify its results (the deadline is Nov 16), so treat every number here as unofficial for now. What isn't in doubt: Seminole showed up. And the biggest group of young voters in this county belongs to neither party. They're who we're here for, too.

However the night landed for you, you don't have to process it alone. Tacos & Talk on Thursday, Nov 19 is a no-pressure debrief. Come as you are.

08The Thursday LetterEvery Thursday

Hi, it's Maya.

MMaya, Oviedo · Demo writer

On election night I was standing outside a polling place with a clipboard and a lukewarm cold brew when a woman I'd never met asked if she could hand out water with me. She stayed two hours. I didn't get her last name, but I got the point: most people are one invitation away from showing up.

However Nov 3 landed for you, this week is for breathing and regrouping. We're doing tacos in Sanford on Thursday (details above), and Jordan has five free things to do around the county this weekend, including the one with the good empanadas.

One small thing with a real deadline: your mail ballot request runs out Dec 31. Put a reminder in your phone to ask again.

See you Thursday.
Maya, for the Young Dems xo
