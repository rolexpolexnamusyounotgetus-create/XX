          Volkanovski stops Evloev

          Volkanovski, 38, retained the featherweight title with a TKO of the previously unbeaten Movsar Evloev (20-0) at 4:54 of round 2: a right hand on the fence, then punches from half guard. Evloev took him down three times in round 1, more than anyone had in a single round. He fought through a chest infection that week. Pro record 29-4, UFC 16-3.

          Gaethje is the champion

          Gaethje became undisputed lightweight champion when Ilia Topuria's corner stopped their fight after round 4, with Topuria's orbital bones broken. Topuria had out-landed him 97 to 91 and scored the only knockdown, from body shots. Gaethje turns 38 on Nov 14. UFC record 11-5, pro 28-5.

          League data, 2010 to Oct 2026

          Age: both 38

          Across UFC fights, the chance of being knocked down by a head strike rises about 4% a year after 30 and knockdown power falls about 3.6% a year. Fighters aged 38 to 40 won 36.9% of their fights and lost 25.5% by KO. They are six weeks apart in age, so it is a wash between them, but it raises the finish rate.

          Size: weak overall, noisy at the top

          Fighters moving up one class showed no measurable penalty once Elo is controlled for (+0.03 log-odds, standard error 0.10), and were knocked down at the same rate per head strike (ratio 1.02).           Markets are hard to beat

          Under the hood

          How the engine works

          Every fighter in the UFC stats snapshot (through Oct 31, 2026) gets a profile built from round-by-round numbers, weighted toward recent fights with a two-year half-life and pulled toward the division average in proportion to how little data there is. A fighter with 20 UFC minutes reads close to average; one with 300 reads close to his own record.

          Each simulated fight runs as competing clocks, re-drawn after every event. At distance both men throw strikes at rates set by one's attempts and the other's attempts allowed, and each strike lands or misses on the log5 of attacker accuracy and defender defense, by target. Clinch entries, takedowns, ground passes, sweeps, stand-ups and submission attempts each have their own clocks, fitted so the pair's expected clinch and control time matches their records.

          Clean head shots can knock a man down, with a chance built from the puncher's knockdown rate, the receiver's chin, age past 34, prior KO losses and moving up in weight. After a knockdown the attacker may follow him down; the referee can stop it or he can recover. Leg kicks slow a man, body shots drain the tank, and fatigue cuts output, accuracy and power. Three judges score each round with weights fit to real UFC scorecards, each with a personal taste and their own noise. Fight-night form varies every fight.

          The global settings are tuned on UFC fights from 2019 to 2023 and reported on fights from 2024 on that the tuning never saw. The backtest panel above shows that report.
